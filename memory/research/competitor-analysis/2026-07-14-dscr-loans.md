# Greenstreet DSCR Competitor Analysis

Greenstreet can occupy a defensible position that no reviewed competitor fully owns: a lender-specific DSCR qualification experience that combines reverse calculation, visible assumptions, scenario comparisons, dated source evidence, and named licensed review. ## Content strategy benchmark

### DSCRLens

- **Observed strength:** Articles commonly show a 2026 date, read time, table of contents, key takeaways, field note, FAQ, author card, contextual links, and calculator CTA.
- **Observed strength:** Separate pages address foreign nationals, Airbnb, LLCs, down payment, requirements, cash-out, multifamily, BRRRR, and state topics.
- **Observed weakness:** A named operator story is present, but a named licensed mortgage, compliance, tax, or legal reviewer was not visible on sampled guides.
- **Exploit path:** Pair real investor examples with a licensed review record, direct guideline citations, method versions, and anonymized evidence.

### DSCR Finder

- **Observed strength:** A methodology page discloses comparison factors and weights: 40% rates, 20% DSCR flexibility, 15% capacity, 15% closing speed, and 10% investor features.
- **Observed strength:** Rate, lender, calculator, state/city, and blog hubs form a clear commercial path.
- **Observed weakness:** Multiple pages overlap on current-rate intent. Sampled material also places promotional starting rates near a higher typical range.
- **Exploit path:** Maintain one canonical, dated rate page and label illustrative best-case, typical-qualified, and challenging scenarios with their assumptions.

### NerdWallet

- **Observed strength:** The guide identifies Ryan Brady, CFP, and editor Sally Lauckner, includes review/disclosure links, and was updated March 31, 2026.
- **Observed strength:** It answers definition, fit, pros and cons, formula, requirements, application steps, alternatives, and tips in a decision sequence.
- **Observed weakness:** The page is educational rather than a lender-specific qualification system.
- **Exploit path:** Match its authorship and review clarity while giving users an auditable scenario result and a route to improve a weak file.

### EveryCalc

- **Observed strength:** The tool reverse-sizes principal from rent, vacancy, taxes, insurance, HOA, rate, term, and lender DSCR floor.
- **Observed strength:** It exposes maximum loan, maximum principal and interest, effective rent, resulting DSCR, method, safeguards, and related tools.
- **Observed weakness:** It does not model Greenstreet-specific programs, profile rules, state restrictions, or an application path.
- **Exploit path:** Show which variable binds the scenario, test low/base/high cases, and connect each constraint to a reviewed requirements page.

### DSCR Capital Partners

- **Observed strength:** Company NMLS `#2591548`, broker-owner Arin Baghermian NMLS `#1220456`, author pages, licensing notices, Equal Housing language, and product disclosures create a strong expert entity.
- **Observed strength:** Down-payment and foreign-national pages use updated dates, scenario tables, document lists, FAQs, and profile-specific CTAs.
- **Observed weakness:** Sampled foreign-national pages show differing down-payment and pricing-premium figures, while source attribution and aggregation methods are limited.
- **Exploit path:** Publish one assumption-controlled matrix, cite source dates beside thresholds, and document how scenario bands were produced.
