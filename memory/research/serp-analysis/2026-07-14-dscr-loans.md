# DSCR Loans SERP Analysis

## Headline finding

The `DSCR loans` result set is medium-high difficulty for an unproven domain. Greenstreet has a more defensible entry through an assumption-rich qualification calculator, transparent rate scenarios, borrower-profile matrices, and reviewed state prepayment guidance than through generic loan copy.

### Site-fit interpretation

- **New or low-authority site, Estimated:** Hard for the head term; target tool and state/profile long tails first.
- **Growing site, Estimated:** Viable with an original calculator, transparent assumptions, expert review, and tightly linked support pages.
- **Established finance site, Estimated:** Competitive with current data, cited claims, and link support.

## Five defensible content opportunities

1. **Qualification engine, Estimated:** Answer `how much DSCR loan can I qualify for` with maximum loan amount, payment, DSCR, taxes, insurance, expenses, editable lender thresholds, and worked examples.
2. **State prepayment guidance, Estimated:** Publish sourced hard, soft, and step-down explanations, then add only attorney-reviewed state pages with visible dates and disclaimers.
3. **Borrower-profile matrix, Estimated:** Compare first-time investors, LLCs, foreign nationals, Airbnb properties, credit tiers, reserves, LTV, and minimum DSCR in one requirements hub.
4. **Transparent rate scenarios, Estimated:** Replace contradictory headline figures with dated best-case, typical-qualified, and challenging scenarios tied to FICO, LTV, DSCR, property type, points, prepayment structure, and loan size.
5. **Scenario-based fit guide, Estimated:** Recommend structures by deal need, such as maximum LTV, low DSCR, short-term-rental income, cash-out refinance, foreign-national ownership, or no-prepayment preference.

## Greenstreet gaps against the observed standard

- The homepage currently presents conflicting best-tier rate claims, which prevents a single quotable answer.
- No external citations support rate, requirement, legal, or market claims.
- The page does not identify a named author, licensed reviewer, editorial standard, or update owner.
- FAQ and summary content are not structured for extraction with visible question-answer sections and schema.
- Calculator methodology and assumptions are not presented as a standalone search resource.
- Composite testimonials cannot provide the same trust value as attributable, consented customer evidence.
- The topic architecture does not yet provide dedicated, internally linked pages for rates, requirements, profiles, and state rules.

## Recommended response format for the homepage

1. Open with a two-sentence definition and fit statement for `DSCR loans`.
2. State that pricing is scenario-dependent; link to a dated rate page rather than placing unsupported ranges in multiple sections.
3. Give the calculator a distinct route and a concise explanation of inputs, outputs, assumptions, and methodology.
4. Add a short requirements table with links to the full eligibility guide and profile pages.
5. Add a sourced prepayment explainer with a link to reviewed state guidance.
6. Add named author/reviewer identity, credentials, last-reviewed date, and editorial/compliance policy links.
7. Add visible FAQs and valid structured data only after answers are finalized and verified.
