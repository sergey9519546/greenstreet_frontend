## Cannibalization controls

- Keep calculator intent on its own interactive URL. The homepage should introduce and link to the tool, not duplicate its full methodology.
- Separate current-rate content from lender comparisons. Rate intent demands dated assumptions; lender-selection intent demands transparent criteria.
- Keep broad requirements, down payment, credit score, and reserves together. Give Airbnb and foreign-national queries separate pages because their underwriting inputs differ.
- Start first-time-investor and LLC terms as substantial requirements sections. Split them only after unique guidance, examples, and source support are available.
- Separate general prepayment mechanics from state-specific rules. Each state page needs current legal review and a visible review date.
- Give `DSCR loan vs conventional loan` its own side-by-side page rather than folding it into a general pros-and-cons article.
- Do not use `no income verification investment property loan` as an interchangeable synonym for `DSCR loans`. Explain the difference between borrower-income review and property-income/documentation requirements.
