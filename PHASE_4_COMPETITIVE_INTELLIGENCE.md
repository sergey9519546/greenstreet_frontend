## 8. RISK ANALYSIS

### Competitive Threats

#### **High Risk:**
1. **LendingTree enters DSCR niche** - They have resources to dominate
2. **Google launches DSCR calculator** - Zero-click future
3. **Major lender builds own calculator** - Disintermediation

#### **Medium Risk:**
4. **AI answer engines bypass your site** - ChatGPT/Perplexity steal traffic
5. **New well-funded startup** - VC-backed competitor
6. **Regulatory changes** - DSCR product changes

#### **Low Risk:**
7. **Copycat competitor** - Easy to copy calculator
8. **SEO algorithm changes** - Standard risk

---

### Mitigation Strategies

**For Threat #1 (LendingTree):**
- Build broker network (stickier than consumer)
- Focus on B2B moat
- Create API ecosystem

**For Threat #2 (Google zero-click):**
- Own the "DSCR calculator" brand
- Build tool that requires interaction (multi-step)
- Focus on loan matching (can't be zero-click)

**For Threat #4 (AI engines):**
- Optimize for AI citations (be the source)
- Add citations to training data
- Partner with Perplexity/ChatGPT for direct integration
