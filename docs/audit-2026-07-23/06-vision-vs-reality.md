# Ultimate Audit — Report 06: Vision vs. Reality

**Scope:** The gap between the "DSCR Sovereign OS" vision corpus (`docs/dscr_loan_office/`, 51 docs, 2.3 MB) and the shipped Greenstreet product (`src/`: 29 React pages, 25 deterministic engine modules, a thin Express API, one LLM endpoint).
**Method:** Read-only. Highest-signal docs read in full; `src/` grepped to confirm presence/absence of every promised capability. No repository files were modified.
**Date:** 2026-07-23

---

## (A) Executive Verdict

**Greenstreet shipped a genuinely good deterministic DSCR calculator wearing the marketing skin of an institutional AI lending platform it has not built and, at its current team scale, cannot build.** The "DSCR Sovereign OS" described across 51 master/definitive/blueprint documents — graph-native three-plane architecture, TimesFM/TFT rent forecasting, XGBoost approval prediction, R-vine copula Monte Carlo, CECL credit-loss modeling, conformal uncertainty, a JSONB Evidence Vault with confidence auto-decay, hybrid AI-OCR intake, a live Kill-Switch Monitor polling RentCast/Treasury — **exists in zero lines of shipped code.** A grep of `src/` for `timesfm|embedding|vector|rag|copula|xgboost|conformal|kalman|cecl` returns nothing but the words "memory: 1GiB" (a Firebase config) and statute text. What *did* ship is real and non-trivial: correct dual-track DSCR math, a 19-lender provenance-tagged matrix, 50-state prepayment/usury law encoding (147 KB), tax/reassessment, ARM-reset and a Vasicek rate-path Monte Carlo, all behind 57 engine tests — plus exactly **one** AI feature: a `narrate` endpoint that sends already-computed numbers to a model at `api.z.ai` and returns 2–3 sentences of plain English, explicitly barred from generating a single number. The vision is 90% aspirational fiction bolted onto a 10% real, well-built calculator. Compounding this, the product has no stable identity (≈10 competing product names), no single source of truth (two literal `(1)` duplicate docs, ~18 near-duplicate "master" titles, and a self-admitted corrections log walking back fabricated citations), and just executed a hard broker-marketplace → direct-lender pivot in marketing copy while leaving the engine's core marketplace mechanic (rank 19–"60+" competing lenders per deal) untouched — an unresolved business-model contradiction.

## (C) Doc-Corpus Health

**Scale & redundancy.** 51 markdown docs, 2.3 MB, in one flat folder. No index enforces precedence beyond `00_MASTER_README.md` — which is not a product spec at all but a **manifest for a 1.2 GB data-download ZIP archive** (Treasury/Zillow/FEMA/Fannie datasets), describing a "CA-DSCR" algorithm and "Default Probability Score" that, again, do not exist in `src/`.

**Explicit duplicates.** Two literal `(1)` copies:
- `DSCR_Command_Center_v7_Master_Consolidated_Audit (1).md` (430 KB — the single largest file).
- `dscr_sovereign_os_upgrade_intelligence_report (1).md` vs `…report.md` — **not identical** (179 vs 176 lines); a near-dup that has drifted, so a reader cannot know which is current.

**Near-duplicate titles (version drift).** ~18 files carry "Master / Definitive / Complete / vN": e.g. `…MASTER BLUEPRINT`, `DSCR_Sovereign_OS_Definitive_Master_Blueprint_v3`, `THE COMPLETE SOVEREIGN MASTER DOCUMENT`, `DSCR_Underwriting_Engine_v14_Complete_Master_Document` **and** `…_Consolidated_v16` (v14 and v16 coexist), `AEGIS_DSCR_Complete_Usable_Master_Doc_v3`, plus three overlapping "Upgrade Intelligence Reports" (`…report.md`, `…report (1).md`, `…Report_v2.md`). Multiple documents each assert they are the authoritative master; none references the others as superseded.

**Self-admitted contradiction & walk-backs.** The corpus contains dedicated cleanup docs — `DSCR_Blueprint_Verification_Corrections_Log.md`, `DSCR_Appendix_B_Research_Resolution_Report.md`, `recheck_deep-research-report.md`, `dscr_sovereign_os_deep_debt_analysis.md`. The `upgrade_intelligence_report` openly downgrades its own prior claims: *"the exact 'TabPFN-2.5, 50K rows, 2K features, NeurIPS 2025 D&B track' framing remains insufficiently verified … Earlier references to a specific May 2026 LightGBM/XGBoost/CatBoost backtest and a 'Nature 2026' credit-risk paper should also be normalized to precise, verifiable sources before they are treated as settled facts."* The corpus is, in effect, debugging its own hallucinated citations in-band.

**Single source of truth?** No. The **plan** (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-06-22-dscr-site-pages.md`) nominates `UNIFIED_HUB.md` (118 KB) as *"⭐ CANONICAL SOURCE … single source of truth for ALL website copy, naming, numbers."* But (a) UNIFIED_HUB itself is pre-pivot (broker/marketplace framing, "60+ programs"); (b) its naming rule ("customer-facing copy uses only 'Greenstreet Finance' + 'Sovereign OS'") was **abandoned** — `grep -i sovereign src/` = 0 hits; and (c) it coexists with `THE COMPLETE SOVEREIGN MASTER DOCUMENT`, the v3 Definitive Blueprint, and the v16 Underwriting Engine doc, none of which defer to it. The README is the *only* doc that describes reality accurately, and it does so by explicitly disowning the corpus: *"Do not treat root-level research files as part of the app source tree."*

**Fabricated-precision smell.** Docs are dated to the day ("June 17, 2026"), cite paywalled sources with "verbatim quote pending subscription access," invent regulatory events (SR 11-7 "replaced by SR 26-02 on April 17, 2026"), and stamp themselves `CLASSIFICATION: SOVEREIGN`. The confidence of the prose is inversely correlated with what shipped.

## (D) Strategy Coherence

**The pivot is real, recent, and marketing-only.** `git log` (125 commits total; 16 pivot-related, all near HEAD) shows a hard repositioning: `826ef8e "Reframe LenderIntel as 'Our DSCR Programs' (we are the lender, not a marketplace)"`, `754aa03 "…direct-to-investor (we are the broker AND lender)"`, `3f381dd "Repurpose /brokers to an investor-direct page"`, `f80a0c5 "Repurpose /partners broker portal to the investor workspace"`, `31f455f "InvestGO portal: reframe broker-facing labels to investor"`. This was a copy-and-label sweep across pages — **not** an engine or model change.

**Four business models are asserted at once, and they conflict:**
1. **Direct lender** — shipped site: *"Greenstreet is the direct lender: no broker markup, no middleman, one underwriting desk"* (`BorrowerProfilesPage.tsx`); *"we ARE the lender AND the broker — direct to investors"* (`SolutionsPage.tsx`).
2. **Broker / lender marketplace** — the *engine's flagship feature* still matches and ranks **19 competing lenders** per deal (`lenders.ts`, `matchLenders`, `lenderMatchScore.ts`), and the site still shows a "60+ programs" logo wall. A direct lender does not shop 60 rivals for you; lender-matching *is* a broker/marketplace mechanic. The pivot renamed the page ("Our DSCR Programs") but left the marketplace machinery intact.
3. **Non-QM wholesale lender / RMBS issuer** — `THE DEFINITIVE BLUEPRINT: BUILDING THE BEST NON-QM WHOLESALE LENDER` and the six-function value chain (Originate→Underwrite→Service→Securitize→Hedge→Replenish) describe becoming a Verus/Angel-Oak-style issuer with warehouse lines and securitization — an enormously capital-intensive posture with zero supporting code.
4. **SaaS decision-engine** — the README, `platform`/`products` copy, and "no LLM math, deterministic, auditable" positioning sell the *engine itself* as the product (a tool brokers/investors use).

These are not adjacent go-to-market tactics; they are different companies (a lender holds capital and credit risk; a marketplace routes to third parties; an issuer runs a capital-markets desk; a SaaS vendor sells software). The docs never reconcile them, and the pivot resolved the marketing surface toward (1) while the codebase still implements (2) and the strategy corpus still argues (3) and (4).

**Post-pivot doc/code coherence: low.** The pivot did not propagate to the vision corpus (still broker/marketplace/wholesale-issuer), to UNIFIED_HUB (the nominal SoT, still "60+ programs" marketplace), or to the engine (still a lender-matching marketplace core). Only the page copy moved.

## (E) Findings by Severity

### CRITICAL

- **[C-1] The core product thesis is vaporware.** Every differentiating "Sovereign OS" capability — ML underwriting, TimesFM/TFT forecasting, copula credit risk, conformal uncertainty, CECL loss, graph runtime, evidence vault, live data, memory — has **zero implementing code** (Scorecard #9–20, #25). The shipped product is a deterministic calculator; the vision is an institutional AI risk platform. Marketing/positioning that leans on the vision (UNIFIED_HUB elevator: *"next-generation AI-native system of action … stress-tested underwriting in under 90 seconds"*) materially misrepresents the product. *Cite:* `TimesFM 2.5 LoRA Upgrade Blueprint.md`, `dscr_sovereign_os_architectural_debt_and_math.md` vs. `package.json` (no ML deps) + empty grep of `src/`.

- **[C-2] "Architectural debt" is audited against a system that doesn't exist.** `dscr_sovereign_os_architectural_debt_and_math.md` critiques "the current t-copula Monte Carlo," "if the XGBoost approval predictor is retrained weekly," "the TFT/TimesFM income projections," and "the Evidence Vault" as *existing* components needing 8 upgrades. None exist. A debt register written against imaginary infrastructure is a governance red flag: it will drive "upgrade" work on foundations that were never poured, and it signals the authors may not distinguish specified-from-shipped. *Cite:* debt doc Debts 3, 5, 8 vs. `src/engine/monteCarlo.ts` (independent `normalRandom` draws, no copula).

- **[C-3] Shipped Monte Carlo does the exact thing the spec forbids.** MASTER BLUEPRINT §III.2: *"10,000 Trials using a t-copula … Gaussian copulas are forbidden."* Shipped code (`monteCarlo.ts`, `monteCarloRatePath.ts`) uses **independent Gaussian (`normalRandom`) shocks and a single-factor Vasicek path** — no copula, no tail dependence, no correlation matrix at all. This is not just under-delivery; it is the specifically-prohibited method, presented in `MonteCarloPage.tsx` as institutional stress testing.

### HIGH

- **[H-1] Business model is internally inconsistent (lender vs. marketplace vs. issuer vs. SaaS).** See §D. The post-pivot "we are the lender, not a marketplace" claim is contradicted by the engine's own flagship lender-matching/ranking feature and the "60+ programs" logo wall still on the site. *Cite:* `git log` pivot commits; `SolutionsPage.tsx`/`BorrowerProfilesPage.tsx` copy vs. `src/engine/lenders.ts` + `matchLenders`.

- **[H-2] Contradictory lender-count claims (12 / 17 / 19 / "60+").** Engine ships **19** lenders (`lenders.ts`); `index.ts` comment says "19-lender"; site marketing says **"60+"** programs; the plan/UNIFIED_HUB cites both a **"17-lender verified production matrix"** and a **"12-lender matrix"** in the same document. Four numbers for one panel; the customer-facing "60+" is >3× the real matrix. *Cite:* plan doc §"Pinned facts" ("17-lender") and Task-2 copy ("12 Lenders ranked") vs. `lenders.ts` (19) vs. site "60+".

- **[H-3] No single source of truth; corpus is self-contradicting.** Multiple simultaneous "master/definitive" docs, literal `(1)` duplicates that have drifted, coexisting v14/v16 and three Upgrade reports, and a nominated SoT (UNIFIED_HUB) whose own naming/model decisions were abandoned. A team member cannot determine current truth from the corpus. *Cite:* §C; `DSCR_Blueprint_Verification_Corrections_Log.md`.

- **[H-4] Naming/brand sprawl — ≈10 product identities for one product.** In the docs: **DSCR Sovereign OS**, **AEGIS** (5 docs), **20X DSCR Deal Engine** (16 docs), **(DSCR Loan) Command Center v7** (7 docs; also hard-coded "Command Center v7.0" in `reserveEngine.ts`), **Pro Deal Desk** (14 docs), **Advisor-Grade DSCR Decision Engine**, **DSCR Dual Truth Engine**, **Godmode Deal Desk**. In code: **Greenstreet Finance** (brand) + **InvestGO** portal — itself renamed **DSCRGo → InvestorGO → InvestGO** across `b8b38a5`/`198bf66`. Even the plan's canonical "Sovereign OS" engine name ships **0 times** in `src/`. No brand persists cleanly from vision to product. *Cite:* grep counts in `docs/` and `src/`; `git log` rename commits.

### MEDIUM

- **[M-1] The one AI feature is thinner than implied and routed to a third-party endpoint.** `src/routes/narrate.ts` is a 65-line endpoint: it forwards already-computed numbers to `process.env.ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL || "https://api.z.ai/api/anthropic"` (Z.ai's Anthropic-compatible GLM endpoint), model default `claude-sonnet-4-6`, `max_tokens: 200`, and returns 2–3 explanatory sentences. Positioning that implies "AI underwriting" is unsupported; this is AI *narration* of deterministic output, hosted off-Anthropic. *Cite:* `narrate.ts:14,20,49`.

- **[M-2] "Evidence Vault / provenance" is a static label, not the promised system.** Provenance *tags* ship (396 occurrences; `ProvenanceLabel`, `VERIFIED_PRIMARY` on lender claims) — a real, good practice. The provenance data is also frozen at author time (e.g. lender notes tagged `[UNVERIFIED]`, `2026-06`), so "anti-decay" is inverted — it silently ages. *Cite:* `lenders.ts` provenance vs. absent vault/diff.

- **[M-3] Site is a repurposed FINRA-compliance template.** The plan's route map and Task 7 reveal DSCR features were find-replaced onto a communications-surveillance product's routes (`/products/communications-archiving-supervision` → "DSCR Calculator"; strings like "WORM", "17a-4", "eComm surveillance", "compliance champion" swept out). Internal href semantics still encode the old product, raising maintenance/SEO/analytics debt and explaining some of the naming chaos. *Cite:* plan doc Route map + Task 7 mappings.

- **[M-4] Unverified metrics acknowledged internally, risk of shipping anyway.** The plan flags hero stats "7s / 99.14% / 88% / 99%+" as **UNVERIFIED** and warns not to cite "7s/30s/90s as hard claims until benchmarked," yet "under 90 seconds"/"in seconds" performance claims persist in UNIFIED_HUB and page copy for a pipeline (OCR→forecast→match) that isn't built. *Cite:* plan §"Stat-card decision"; UNIFIED_HUB elevator.

### LOW / OBSERVATION

- **[L-1] What shipped is genuinely strong in its lane.** 25 engine modules, 57 tests, correct dual-track math, 147 KB of encoded 50-state law, provenance discipline, and an explicit architectural guardrail (`engine/index.ts`: *"Do NOT add LLM calls to this folder — math only. Every number … computed here, deterministically"*). The determinism-first discipline is the healthiest thing in the repo and is the credible product. The strategic error is *marketing* it as the Sovereign OS rather than as "the best deterministic DSCR calculator," which it plausibly is.

- **[L-2] The `six-function-doctrine` and `upgrade_intelligence_report` are the corpus's sane center.** Both explicitly counsel *against* end-to-end AI underwriting and *for* a deterministic spine with governed augmentation ("enforce exact numerical truth at the core"). If any docs should be promoted to canonical and the rest archived, these two plus the README are the candidates. The product would be more coherent if the org believed its own most sober documents rather than its most grandiose ones.

## Bottom Line

Greenstreet built the floor of its vision well and then wrote 2.3 MB of documentation describing the skyscraper as if it were occupied. The deterministic engine is real, tested, and defensible. The "Sovereign OS" — the ML, the forecasting, the sovereign data, the graph runtime, the memory — is a research-and-marketing artifact with no load-bearing code. Add an identity crisis (≈10 names, no source of truth) and an unreconciled lender-vs-marketplace-vs-issuer business model, and the strategic risk is not technical debt but **narrative debt**: the gap between what is said and what exists is now large enough that the docs actively mislead anyone — including the team's own future agents — about what the product is.
