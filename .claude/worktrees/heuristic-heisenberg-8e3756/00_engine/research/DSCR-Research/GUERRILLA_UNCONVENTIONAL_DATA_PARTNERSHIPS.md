## 1. COMPANIES THAT HAVE DSCR BORROWERS BUT DON'T LEND

### 1A. Property Management Software — The Sleeping Giant

**The Players:** Buildium (3.5M+ units), AppFolio (2M+ units), Rent Manager (1.5M+ units), TurboTenant (500K+ units), Avail (now part of Realtor.com, 300K+ units), TenantCloud (250K+ units)

**Why They're Gold:** Every single user of property management software is, by definition, a landlord. And not a casual one — they're organized enough to use software. They have rent rolls inside the platform. They have property addresses. They have lease expiration dates. They have maintenance records that signal property condition. This is **underwriting data pre-packaged and delivered**.

**The Insight Nobody Has:** Property management software companies are in a SaaS grind. Their ARPU is $50–$200/month. They churn at 5–8% monthly. They desperately need new revenue streams and stickiness. A DSCR partnership that generates $500–$2,000 per referral while making their platform more valuable (users can finance expansion FROM the platform) is a no-brainer value proposition.

**Partnership Mechanics:**
- **"Get Pre-Approved for Your Next DSCR Loan"** button inside the dashboard, triggered when a user:
  - Adds a new property (acquisition financing)
  - Views property cash flow reports (cash-out refinance signal)
  - Has a lease expiring in 90 days (refinance before re-leasing)
  - Has owned a property >12 months (seasoned refinance candidate)
- The rent roll data **already inside Buildium** eliminates 40% of the documentation burden for DSCR underwriting. You're not just getting a lead — you're getting a pre-underwritten lead.
- Revenue share: 50–75 bps per closed loan. On a $300K average DSCR loan, that's $1,500–$2,250 to Buildium per closed loan. If 2% of their 3.5M-unit user base closes one loan per year, that's $10.5M–$15.7M in new annual revenue for Buildium.

**Estimated Lead Volume:** Buildium alone could generate 15,000–25,000 qualified DSCR leads per year. Across all PM software, the addressable pool is 50,000+ leads annually.

**Competitive Moat:** Once you integrate with Buildium's rent roll API, a competitor would need to replicate the entire integration and convince Buildium to switch. First-mover advantage here is extreme.

### 1B. Rental Listing Platforms — The Intent Signal

**The Players:** Zillow Rental Manager (8M+ listings/year), Apartments.com / CoStar (12M+ listings), Rent.com (2M+ listings), Facebook Marketplace Rentals (unknown but massive), Craigslist (still dominant in many markets)

**Why They're Gold:** Listing a property for rent is a **high-intent signal** that someone either just acquired a rental property (needs purchase financing) or has owned one and may want to extract equity (cash-out refinance). The timing is perfect — they're actively thinking about their rental property's financial performance.

**The Insight Nobody Has:** Zillow Rental Manager users are a self-selecting cohort of serious landlords. Casual owners use a yard sign. People who list on Zillow are optimizing for rental income — exactly the mindset of a DSCR borrower. And Zillow already collects property address, estimated rent, and some owner information. That's 60% of a DSCR pre-qualification.

**Partnership Mechanics:**
- **"Just Listed Your Rental? See If You Qualify for a Cash-Out Refinance"** — triggered immediately after listing
- Zillow already shows "Zestimate" for property value. Combined with listed rent, you can compute a **real-time DSCR** and show the borrower: "Your DSCR is 1.45 — you qualify for up to $XXX,XXX cash out"
- This is a **calculation that creates its own demand**. Most landlords don't know their DSCR. Showing them they qualify for cash they didn't know they could access is conversion magic.
- Revenue share: 50 bps per closed loan + $10–$25 per application submitted (Zillow monetizes every interaction)

**Estimated Lead Volume:** 30,000–50,000 qualified leads per year from Zillow alone. Across all rental platforms: 80,000+.

**The Trojan Horse:** Start with a Zestimate + Rent → DSCR calculator widget that Zillow can embed for free. It provides value to their users (knowing your DSCR is useful). Then upsell the qualification flow.

### 1C. Real Estate CRM & Deal Analysis Tools — The Active Shopper

**The Players:** PropStream (500K+ users), DealCheck (200K+ users), REIKit (50K+ users), FlipperForce (30K+ users), Realeflow (25K+ users), FreedomSoft (15K+ users)

**The Insight Nobody Has:** DealCheck and PropStream already have the data needed for DSCR pre-qualification: property address, estimated ARV, estimated rent (from integrated rent estimators), estimated rehab costs. A user who saves a deal in DealCheck with a positive cash flow projection is literally telling you: "I want to buy this property and I need a loan."

**Partnership Mechanics:**

- Deal analysis summary auto-populates the DSCR application: address, ARV, projected rent, rehab budget

- Integration depth: full API pipeline from deal analysis → DSCR pre-qualification → loan application → doc upload → closing
- Revenue share: 75–100 bps per closed loan (higher share justified because these are the highest-converting leads)

**Estimated Lead Volume:** 5,000–10,000 high-intent leads per year from this category. Lower volume but **3–5x conversion rate** compared to cold leads.

### 1D. Tax & Accounting Software — The Secret Roster

**The Players:** TurboTax (30M+ self-employed filers), H&R Block (20M+ filers), QuickBooks Self-Employed (5M+ users), FreshBooks (2M+ users), Xero (500K+ US users)

**Why They're Gold:** **Schedule E filers are rental property owners.** The IRS literally tags them for you. In 2023, approximately 10.6 million tax returns included Schedule E rental income. Every single one of those is a potential DSCR borrower. And the tax software KNOWS who they are — they have the Schedule E data, the property addresses, the rental income, the expenses, the depreciation schedules.

**The Insight Nobody Has:** TurboTax knows your rental income down to the dollar. They know your expenses. They know your depreciation. They can compute your DSCR more accurately than any rent roll estimator. And they serve this data to 10 million rental property owners every single tax season.

**Partnership Mechanics:**
- **"Maximize Your Rental Property Tax Savings with a DSCR Refinance"** — positioned as a tax optimization strategy, not a loan pitch
- Tax season timing (January–April) creates a natural financial review moment. "Your rental properties generated $XX,XXX in income last year. Did you know you could access $XXX,XXX in equity at a DSCR of Y.ZZ?"
- The partnership frames DSCR as a **tax strategy tool**: cash-out refinance proceeds are loan proceeds (not taxable income), interest is deductible, and timing the refinance can optimize depreciation schedules
- Revenue share: 25–50 bps (lower share because volume is massive and TurboTax has leverage) + co-branded "Tax + Finance" advisory experience

**Estimated Lead Volume:** 50,000–100,000 leads per year from this category. The highest volume channel, but conversion rates will be lower (3–5%) because many Schedule E filers are small-scale landlords with one property who aren't actively seeking financing.

**Privacy Landmine:** Tax data is the most sensitive category. Partnership would likely require: (a) explicit user opt-in, (b) no raw tax data sharing — only pre-computed qualification signals, (c) SOC 2 Type II compliance, (d) potential IRS Circular 230 considerations. The legal infrastructure is significant but the prize is worth it.

**The Insight Nobody Has:** Obie and Steadily are landlord-insurance-only platforms. **100% of their customers are DSCR borrowers or candidates.** There is zero waste in this lead source. Obie in particular has been aggressively growing and would benefit enormously from a financing partnership that makes their insurance product more valuable ("Get insured AND financed in one place").

- Dual-trigger: when someone gets an insurance quote for a NEW property (purchase), AND when someone updates coverage on an EXISTING property (refinance signal)
- Revenue share: 75–100 bps per closed loan + reciprocal referral (you recommend their insurance to your borrowers)

**Estimated Lead Volume:** 10,000–20,000 highly targeted leads per year. Small volume but potentially the **highest conversion rate of any channel** (8–15%) because of perfect intent alignment.

**Reciprocal Value:** This is a two-way street. You send your DSCR borrowers to Obie for insurance. Obie sends their policyholders to you for financing. The mutual flywheel is powerful.

### 1F. Hard Money Lender Platforms — The Bridge-to-DSCR Pipeline

**The Players:** Patch of Land, Groundfloor (crowdfunded hard money, 100K+ investors + borrowers), Sharestates, Lima One Capital (hard money + DSCR), Visio Lending (hard money → DSCR), Kiavi (hard money → DSCR)

**Why They're Gold:** This is the **single most valuable unconventional partnership channel** and it's not close. Every hard money borrower is a FUTURE DSCR borrower. Hard money loans are 6–18 month bridge loans. When they mature, the borrower needs permanent financing. DSCR loans ARE that permanent financing. The conversion is not a maybe — it's a certainty for every performing loan.

**The Insight Nobody Has:** The hard money → DSCR refinance pipeline is a **100% predictable lead funnel with a time delay**. If you know when a hard money loan originates, you know with ~80% certainty that borrower will need a DSCR refinance in 6–18 months. You can calendar the follow-up. You can pre-underwrite the refinance before the hard money loan even matures. This is not lead generation — it's lead **harvesting**.

**Partnership Mechanics:**
- **"Bridge → DSCR" seamless refinance pipeline**
- At hard money origination: "Your bridge loan matures in 12 months. We'll pre-qualify your DSCR refinance now so you're ready"
- At 6 months before maturity: "Your DSCR refinance is pre-approved. Lock your rate now"
- At 3 months before maturity: "Close your DSCR refinance and avoid extension fees"
- Revenue share: 50–75 bps per closed refinance + potential for hard money platform to **white-label the DSCR product** (they keep their brand, you provide the capital and underwriting)

**Estimated Lead Volume:** 20,000–40,000 refinance leads per year with **15–25% conversion rates** because the need is time-certain and unavoidable.

**The Strategic Play:** Some hard money lenders (like Lima One and Kiavi) already have their own DSCR products. The play for the smaller platforms is: "You don't need to build a DSCR program. We'll be your DSCR program. You keep originating hard money, we'll handle the permanent financing, and we'll both make more money." This is a **channel partnership**, not a referral partnership.

### 1G. 1031 Exchange Facilitators — The Time-Crunched Buyer

**The Players:** Accruit (10,000+ exchanges/year), IPX1031 (Fidelity's exchange arm, 20,000+ exchanges/year), Asset Preservation (5,000+ exchanges/year), Equity 1031, Realty Exchangers

**Why They're Gold:** A 1031 exchange has a **strict 45-day identification period and 180-day closing deadline**. Every single exchange participant MUST acquire a replacement property on a fixed timeline. This creates extraordinary urgency. And DSCR loans are **perfect for 1031 exchanges** because they close fast (2–3 weeks vs. 45+ days for agency loans) and qualify based on property cash flow, not the borrower's personal income (which may be complex due to the exchange structure).

**The Insight Nobody Has:** 1031 exchange facilitators are order-takers. They process paperwork. They don't monetize the financing side of the exchange AT ALL. They facilitate $50B+ in exchanges annually and capture maybe $1,000 per exchange in fees. Meanwhile, the DSCR lender who finances the replacement property captures $3,000–$8,000 per loan. The value is flowing entirely to lenders, and the facilitator — who has the direct relationship — gets table scraps.

**Partnership Mechanics:**
- **"Identified Your Replacement Property? Get DSCR Pre-Approved in 48 Hours"**
- Exchange facilitator introduces DSCR lender at identification stage (Day 1 of the 45-day window)
- DSCR pre-qualification happens in parallel with exchange paperwork
- Closing guaranteed within the 180-day window (or your money back — a powerful guarantee)
- Revenue share: 75–100 bps per closed loan + referral fee to the facilitator
- **Premium positioning**: "1031 Exchange DSCR — Guaranteed Close in 21 Days or Less"

**Estimated Lead Volume:** 5,000–10,000 high-urgency leads per year with **20–30% conversion** because the time pressure is immense and DSCR is the best product for 1031 scenarios.

## 3. THE "API-FIRST" PARTNERSHIP MODEL — Be the Stripe of DSCR

### The Stripe Playbook Applied to DSCR

Stripe didn't become a $95B company by selling payment processing. They won because **they made it trivially easy for any platform to embed payments**. Five lines of code and you're accepting payments. The API was the product.

The same playbook applies to DSCR lending. **The best API wins.** If you can make it possible for any partner to embed "Check Your DSCR Qualification" in their app with less than a day of engineering work, you become the default DSCR infrastructure layer.

### API Endpoints (Minimum Viable)

| Endpoint | Method | Purpose | Response Time |
|----------|--------|---------|---------------|
| `/v1/prequal` | POST | Instant DSCR estimate + rate indication | <2 seconds |
| `/v1/application` | POST | Submit full application | <5 seconds |
| `/v1/application/{id}` | GET | Check application status | <500ms |
| `/v1/documents/upload` | POST | Upload supporting documents | <3 seconds |
| `/v1/rate-lock` | POST | Lock rate for 30/45/60 days | <2 seconds |
| `/v1/webhooks` | — | Real-time status updates to partner | Event-driven |

### Technical Requirements for API Partnerships

1. **OAuth 2.0 authentication** — partner authenticates once, gets refresh tokens
2. **Rate limiting** — generous limits for production partners (1,000 req/min)
3. **Sandbox environment** — full test environment with synthetic data
4. **Webhook delivery** — guaranteed at-least-once delivery with retry logic
5. **SDKs in major languages** — JavaScript, Python, Ruby, PHP (what partners actually use)
6. **Documentation that doesn't suck** — interactive API explorer, code samples for every endpoint
7. **Idempotency keys** — prevent duplicate applications on network retries
8. **PII handling** — partner can pass data without storing it; you become the data controller

### Developer Experience as Competitive Advantage

The reason Stripe won wasn't just their API — it was that **developers loved using it**. Clean documentation, excellent error messages, a beautiful dashboard, and responsive support. For DSCR API partnerships:

- **One-click sandbox provisioning** — no sales call needed to start building
- **Interactive API playground** — test every endpoint in the browser
- **Partner dashboard** — real-time analytics on leads, conversions, revenue
- **Dedicated partner engineering** — Slack channel with <1 hour response time
- **Migration support** — if a partner is using a competitor's API, we help them migrate

## 5. THE "DATA EXCHANGE" VALUE PROPOSITION — Why Partnerships Stick

### The Mutual Value Flywheel

Partnerships fail when they're one-directional. The best partnerships create a **mutual data exchange** where both parties get something they couldn't get alone.

```
┌─────────────────────┐         ┌─────────────────────┐
│   PARTNER HAS:      │         │   YOU HAVE:         │
│                     │         │                     │
│ • Rent rolls        │  ←──→   │ • DSCR qualification│
│ • Property data     │         │ • Rate indications  │
│ • Owner contact     │         │ • Pre-approval      │
│ • Insurance data    │         │ • Closing timeline  │
│ • Tax return data   │         │ • Market rates      │
│ • Deal pipeline     │         │ • Refinance timing  │
└─────────────────────┘         └─────────────────────┘
```

**What you give partners that makes them STICK:**

1. **Financing qualification data**: Partners can use "Your users are pre-qualified for $X in DSCR financing" as a product feature. Buildium can say: "We help you not just manage your properties — we help you finance your next one."

2. **Refinance timing intelligence**: You can tell partners when their users should refinance. This creates re-engagement opportunities. "Hey, rates dropped — your DSCR refinance would save you $400/month" is a notification that drives app opens.

3. **Market rate data**: Partners can display real-time DSCR rates as a feature. "Current DSCR rates in your market: 7.00–7.50%". This is content that keeps users coming back.

4. **Portfolio expansion signals**: When a user pre-qualifies but doesn't close, that's a signal they're shopping. Partners can use this to surface relevant content (new listings, deal analysis, insurance quotes).

**What partners give you that you can't get anywhere else:**

1. **Verified rent data**: Actual lease amounts, not estimates. This is GOLD for DSCR underwriting and eliminates rent fraud risk.
2. **Property condition data**: Maintenance logs, inspection reports, insurance claims — signals of property condition that affect LTV.
3. **Behavioral signals**: How often a user logs in, what properties they analyze, when they list a unit — all leading indicators of financing intent.
4. **Entity information**: LLC structures, EINs, registered agents — data that accelerates entity-level DSCR underwriting.

### Data Sharing Agreements & Privacy

**The Golden Rule**: Never share raw user data. Share **derived signals** only.

- **BAD**: "Here's a list of our users who might want DSCR loans"
- **GOOD**: "When your user clicks 'Check DSCR Qualification,' we receive their property address and estimated rent through an encrypted API call with their explicit consent"

**Required Legal Infrastructure:**
1. Data Processing Agreement (DPA) — standard GDPR/CCPA compliance
2. End-user consent flow — clear opt-in before any data sharing
3. Data minimization — only collect what's needed for qualification
4. Retention policy — delete PII after 24 months of inactivity
5. SOC 2 Type II certification — non-negotiable for enterprise partners
6. Breach notification — 72-hour notification requirement
7. Audit rights — partners can audit your data handling annually

## 7. THE "HIDDEN GOLDMINE" PARTNERSHIP — The Ones Nobody Sees

### 7A. County Tax Assessor Offices

**The Data:** Every county assessor's office has a database of every rental property in their jurisdiction. How? Properties that receive a homestead exemption are owner-occupied. Properties WITHOUT a homestead exemption are likely investment/rental properties. This is a binary filter that identifies ~100% of rental properties.

**The Scale:** There are approximately 48 million rental units in the United States across approximately 15 million rental properties. The county assessor's office has every single one catalogued with: property address, owner name, owner mailing address (often different from property address — a signal it's a rental), assessed value, tax payment status, and sometimes even the property's use code (residential, multifamily, commercial).

**How to Access It:**
1. Many counties sell their tax roll data commercially for $500–$5,000 per county
2. Some states (like Florida) have statewide databases available online
3. Third-party aggregators like DataTree (by First American), PropStream, and Melissa Data already compile this data
4. The key insight: filter for non-homestead residential properties + out-of-state owner mailing addresses = DSCR borrower

**The Partnership:** Partner with a tax roll data aggregator to create a "DSCR Prospect Score" for every non-owner-occupied property in America. Not a cold call list — a prioritized outreach list scored by: property value, ownership duration (longer = more equity = refinance candidate), number of properties owned (portfolio = higher value borrower), and recent purchase date (<12 months = bridge-to-DSCR candidate).

### 7B. Permit Databases — The Renovation Signal

**The Data:** When someone pulls a building permit for a rental property, they're signaling renovation activity. This could indicate: (a) a recent purchase requiring rehab (hard money → DSCR refinance), (b) a value-add strategy (rehab → refinance at higher ARV), or (c) property improvement before selling (but these people still need bridge financing).

**The Scale:** There are approximately 2 million building permits pulled annually for residential renovations. A significant percentage are on investment properties.

**How to Access It:**
1. Many municipalities publish permit data online
2. Permit-tracking services like BuildZoom, Permits.com, and ConstructConnect aggregate this data
3. The key filter: permits pulled at non-owner-occupied addresses (cross-reference with assessor data)

**The Partnership:** Partner with BuildZoom or similar to create a "Renovation → Refinance" pipeline. When a permit is pulled on an investment property, trigger outreach: "Renovating your rental? Did you know you can refinance at the after-renovation value with a DSCR loan?"

### 7C. HOA Management Companies — The Accidental Landlord Database

**The Data:** HOA management companies (FirstService Residential, Associa, CINC Systems) manage communities with rental caps. They track which units are rented vs. owner-occupied because they enforce rental restrictions. They know: unit owner name, mailing address, tenant information, and lease terms.

**The Scale:** FirstService Residential alone manages 1.6 million units across 8,500+ associations. Associa manages 5 million+ units. These companies have **the most granular rental property data in existence** — down to the unit number, lease expiration, and tenant payment history.

**The Partnership:** This is the most unconventional and potentially the most valuable. HOA management companies are not tech-savvy. They run on legacy software. They have no idea their data is valuable to lenders. A partnership that helps them monetize their rental property data (with owner consent) while providing DSCR financing to their rental unit owners is a win-win-win.

### 7D. Utility Companies — The Vacancy Signal

**The Data:** When a rental unit's utility usage drops to near-zero, the unit is vacant. When it spikes, someone just moved in. Utility companies have real-time occupancy data for virtually every residential unit in America.

**The Partnership:** While direct utility company partnerships are extremely difficult (privacy, regulation), third-party services like UtilityAPI and Standard Data already aggregate utility data with consumer consent. These platforms could provide occupancy verification — replacing the expensive and fraud-prone "rent verification" step in DSCR underwriting.

### 7E. The Ultimate Hidden Goldmine: Eviction Court Records

**The Data:** When a landlord files for eviction, they've identified themselves as a property owner AND they're experiencing a cash flow disruption (no rent coming in). This is the perfect moment to offer a DSCR cash-out refinance: "Having trouble with your rental? A DSCR refinance can give you the cash cushion to weather the vacancy."

**The Scale:** There are approximately 3.6 million eviction filings per year in the United States. Each one identifies a landlord in distress — exactly the type of borrower who needs liquidity.

**How to Access It:**
1. Eviction records are public court records, accessible through court databases
2. Aggregators like EvictionLab (Princeton University), LexisNexis, and TALX compile eviction data
3. The key filter: eviction filings by individual landlords (not corporate apartment complexes) = DSCR borrower candidate

**Ethical Consideration:** This is a sensitive channel. Outreach to landlords in eviction proceedings must be handled with empathy, not opportunism. The framing should be: "We help landlords maintain their investments through difficult periods," not "We prey on landlords in distress."
