#### 15. Hard Money Lenders — THE #1 SOURCE

**Potential borrowers:** EVERY hard money borrower who's fixing up a rental property will need to refinance out of the hard money loan. This is the single most powerful referral channel for DSCR lenders. A hard money loan has a 6-12 month term. Every single one of those borrowers WILL need a refi. DSCR is the refi product for HML borrowers.

**How to access:** Find HMLs through the American Association of Private Lenders (AAPL) directory, local REIA meetings (HMLs always attend), Google "hard money lender [city]," and BiggerPockets lender directory.

**What to DO:**
1. Make a list of 20 hard money lenders in every state you're licensed.

#### 20. Public Records & Data Mining

This is the "Moneyball" approach — find borrowers based on what they've already done, not what they've said.

**Specific data sources and strategies:**

**A. Recent LLC Property Purchases**
- **Source:** County recorder/assessor data. Filter for: properties purchased by LLCs in the last 6-12 months.
- **Logic:** If someone bought a property in an LLC, it's an investment property. They either paid cash (and will want to refi to pull equity) or used hard money (and will need to refi). Either way = DSCR borrower.
- **How to access:** Use data services like PropStream ($99/mo), ListSource ($0.10-0.25/record), or direct county data (often free but harder to parse). Alternatively, use Reonomy ($150/mo) for a polished interface.
- **Volume:** In a metro area of 1M+ people, expect 200-500 LLC property purchases per month.
- **What to DO:** Pull the list monthly. Mail a postcard: "Congratulations on your recent property purchase at [Address]. If you'd like to access your equity or refinance into a long-term loan, DSCR financing qualifies on the rent — no personal income docs needed. Call or text [Number]."
- **What to SAY — Postcard/Direct Mail:**
  > "Dear Property Owner — You recently purchased [Address]. Smart move. If you bought with cash or hard money, you may be able to refinance into a long-term DSCR loan — qualifying on rental income alone. No W-2s. No tax returns. Pre-approval in 24 hours. Call [Number] or scan QR code."

**B. Recent Deed Transfers into LLCs**
- **Logic:** When someone transfers a property from their personal name into an LLC, they're converting it to a rental. They may want to pull cash out.
- **How to access:** Same data sources as above. Filter for grantor = individual name, grantee = LLC.
- **Volume:** 50-200 per month in major metros.
- **Action:** Same postcard/letter approach.

**C. Recent Cash Purchases**
- **Logic:** Cash buyers will want to refinance to get their cash back. Many are investors who bought with cash to win the deal and plan to refinance.
- **How to access:** County records filtered for purchase price with no mortgage recorded. PropStream and ListSource both have this filter.
- **Volume:** 300-800 per month in major metros.
- **Action:** Direct mail within 30 days of purchase.

**D. Rental Listing Platforms — New Landlords**
- **Logic:** Someone listing their FIRST rental property on Zillow/Avail/TurboTenant is a new investor who may have just purchased and needs financing.
- **How to access:** Manually monitor Zillow Rental Manager and other platforms. Or use a service like RentCast that tracks new listings.
- **Volume:** Harder to quantify, but this is an early-stage signal.
- **Action:** Send a message through the platform (if allowed) or a letter to the property address.

**E. Permit Databases — Investors Pulling Rehab Permits**
- **Logic:** If someone pulls a building permit for a property they don't live at, they're likely an investor rehabbing a rental. They'll need DSCR refi after the rehab.
- **How to access:** Most cities have online permit portals. Some data services (PropStream) aggregate this.
- **Volume:** 100-300 per month in major metros.
- **Action:** Add them to a follow-up sequence — they won't be ready for DSCR until the rehab is done and the property is rented.

**Expected results (all data-driven combined):** Direct mail response rates are 1-3%. With 500 postcards/month, expect 5-15 leads/month. 2-5 applications. 1-2 closed loans per month.

**Cost:** Data tools: $100-300/mo. Direct mail: $0.50-1.00 per postcard (including postage). 500 postcards = $250-500/month. **Cost per lead: $50-100.**

## PART 4: THE SECRET CHANNEL

---

### "The LLC-to-DSCR Pipeline" — Title Company Closing Rooms

**What it is:** Every single day, in every county in America, investors close on properties in LLCs. At every closing, a title company or closing attorney is sitting across the table from the buyer. That closing agent has a 5-minute window where the investor is in a great mood, has just acquired a new asset, and is thinking about their next move.

**Why no DSCR lender is using this:**
- DSCR lenders think of title companies as a transactional vendor, not a lead source
- Title company closers are never asked to refer financing — they're asked to clear title
- The closing room is the single moment of highest trust between a professional and an investor
- Most closers would happily mention DSCR refinancing if someone gave them a reason and a script

**The exact play:**

1. **Identify 20-30 title companies and closing attorneys** in your lending footprint. Focus on those who handle investment property transactions (ask around — agents and attorneys know which title companies investor deals go through).

2. **Visit each title company in person.** Bring breakfast or lunch for the office. Ask to speak with the office manager and the closers.

3. **Pitch this specific arrangement:**
   > "I'm a DSCR lender. Every week, you close deals where an LLC buys an investment property — often with cash or hard money. Those buyers are going to need to refinance within 6-12 months. Here's what I propose: when you close a deal with an LLC buyer, simply hand them this card and say: 'If you ever want to refinance this property into a long-term loan, this lender qualifies you on the rent — no personal income docs needed.' That's it. One sentence. For every loan that closes from your referral, I'll pay your office $[500-1,000] or donate to your team's charity of choice."

4. **Provide a physical card** — not a business card. A small, elegant card that says:
   > **"DSCR Investment Property Refinance"**
   > Qualify on Rental Income — No W-2 Required
   > Refinance your investment property into a 30-year loan
   > [Lender Name] | [Phone] | [QR Code to pre-approval page]

5. **Follow up monthly** with each title company. Send a summary: "Last month, 3 of your referrals applied for DSCR loans. 2 are closing this week." Title companies love seeing results.

**Why this is a goldmine:**
- A title company closing 50 investment deals/month = 50 investors sitting in their chairs
- Even at a 20% handout rate = 10 cards given out per month per title company
- At a 10% conversion rate = 1 closed DSCR loan per month per title company
- 20 title companies × 1 closed loan/month = **20 closed loans per month from ONE channel**
- These are investors who just BOUGHT a property — they're in expansion mode
- The refi typically happens 3-6 months after purchase, giving you a warm pipeline
- The title company has ALREADY verified the investor's identity and the property's title

**Cost:** Time for in-person visits (~2 days to visit 20 title companies). Referral fees: $500-1,000 per closed loan. **Cost per lead: $0 until a loan closes.**

**This is the channel that can single-handedly build your borrower base.** Every investor who closes through that title company becomes your lead. The title company has no downside. The investor gets a helpful referral. And you get a stream of qualified borrowers who are in the exact moment of their investing journey where DSCR is the perfect product.
