## 1. Claim Statement

> **DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) loans account for 28.7% of Non-QM mortgage originations.**

**Volume vs loan-count disambiguation:** The 28.7% figure refers to **DOLLAR VOLUME SHARE** (not loan-count share) of the Non-QM segment, as of July 2025.

**Composition context (Optimal Blue, July 2025):**
- Investor / DSCR: **29%** (volume) — up 2.79 pts YoY
- Bank statement: **34%** (volume)
- Other alternative documentation: **38%** (volume)

(Note: NMP reports 28.7% (May 2025 article based on June 2025 Optimal Blue release), Optimal Blue reports 29% in July 2025 release — the difference reflects rounding and intra-quarter growth. Both are within statistical agreement.)

## 8. Volume vs Loan-Count Disambiguation

**The 28.7% figure is VOLUME-WEIGHTED** (dollar share of non-QM originations), not loan-count share.

- DSCR loans tend to have HIGHER average loan balances than typical primary-residence non-QM (bank statement loans are typically for self-employed primary residences, smaller balances). Therefore, the volume share is likely HIGHER than the loan-count share.
- Lendmire's "30% DSCR share of total 2025 non-QM securitization" is a different metric (securitized volume only, full year) that corroborates the volume-based methodology.
- **For the corpus claim, "28.7% of Non-QM market" should be footnoted as "by dollar volume share, July 2025 lock data."**

## 12. Refinements and Caveats

1. **The 28.7% is a single-month data point (July 2025), not a full-year average.** Full-year 2025 averages may differ slightly due to seasonality and segment growth rates. Recommend re-stating the claim as "DSCR = ~28.7% of non-QM volume (July 2025 Optimal Blue lock data)."

2. **Optimal Blue press release rounds 28.7% to 29%.** Both are correct; the 28.7% figure carries the YoY change detail (up 2.79pts).

3. **Lendmire's 30% securitization share (full-year 2025)** is a slightly different measure and time period; not directly substitutable for 28.7%. Both are valid for their respective scopes.

4. **The category name in NMP is "Investor / DSCR"** — Optimal Blue uses similar phrasing. The 28.7% reflects the combined DSCR + investor-purpose loan segment, which is the same universe. There may be a small number of pure investor non-DSCR loans (e.g., non-DSCR bank statement loans to investors), but in Optimal Blue's taxonomy these are categorized differently and DSCR dominates the investor segment.

5. **Future trajectory:** Continued DSCR growth expected through 2026 (Vough, LinkedIn; Lendmire forecast). 28.7% may rise to 30%+ by mid-2026 if current trends continue.
