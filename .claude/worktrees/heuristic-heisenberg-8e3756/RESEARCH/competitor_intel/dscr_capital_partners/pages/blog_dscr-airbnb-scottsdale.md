## Arizona's STR Rules: Preemption, But With Strings

Arizona is unusually STR-friendly at the state level — state law preempts cities from outright _banning_ short-term rentals. But that doesn't mean "anything goes," and Scottsdale has been one of the more aggressive enforcers:

- **City license required** — Scottsdale requires STR operators to obtain and post a city license, in addition to an Arizona **Transaction Privilege Tax (TPT)** license.
- **Operating standards enforced** — occupancy limits, noise rules, a designated local emergency contact, and liability-insurance requirements consistent with state law. Fines for non-compliance have risen.

A DSCR loan finances the property regardless; licensing determines whether you can legally run the STR income the projection assumes. Get licensed and compliant from day one — it protects both the cash flow and the asset.
