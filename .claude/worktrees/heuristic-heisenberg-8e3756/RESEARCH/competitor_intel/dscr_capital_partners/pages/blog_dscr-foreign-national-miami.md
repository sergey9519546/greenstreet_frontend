## What Foreign National Borrowers Need

### Typical Foreign National File

- **No US credit / no SSN required** — foreign credit references or international bank reference letters are used instead
- **No US tax returns or income docs** — qualify on the property's DSCR
- **Valid passport** (and visa where applicable)
- **US bank account** for reserves and closing funds
- **Down payment:** typically 25–30% (≈70–75% LTV)
- **Reserves:** generally 6–12 months PITIA
- **Vesting:** US LLC is common and welcome
