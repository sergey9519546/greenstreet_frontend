## Why Atlanta Works for DSCR Investors

Atlanta's investor case rests on **corporate depth** (Delta, Coca-Cola, Home Depot, UPS, plus a growing tech and film economy), **population growth** (the Atlanta metro continues to absorb in-migration from the Northeast), and **landlord-friendly Georgia law** with no statewide rent control.

## Atlanta Sub-Markets & Neighborhoods

### East Atlanta, Kirkwood, Edgewood

Inner-perimeter cash flow + appreciation. SFR entry $320K–$500K, strong rents, DSCRs 1.10–1.30 at 75% LTV. Mid-renovation neighborhoods with continued upside.

### West End, Adair Park, Pittsburgh

Stronger cash flow. SFR entry $220K–$380K, DSCRs 1.20–1.40 at 75% LTV. BeltLine proximity has driven appreciation.

### Decatur, Avondale Estates

Established residential with strong tenant credit. Premium pricing with tighter DSCRs but lower turnover risk.

### Marietta, Smyrna, Alpharetta

Suburban SFR markets. Strong tenant credit from corporate employment. Family-rental dominant.

## Atlanta DSCR Loan Requirements

### 2026 Requirements — Atlanta

- **Minimum credit score:** 620 (best pricing at 740+)
- **Minimum DSCR:** 0.75 (1.0+ preferred)
- **Maximum LTV:** Up to 85% purchase / 75% cash-out refinance
- **Loan amount:** $100,000 to $15,000,000
- **Property types:** SFR, condo, 2–4 unit, 5+ unit multifamily, condotel
- **Loan terms:** 30-year fixed, ARM options, interest-only
- **Borrower types:** Individual, LLC, corporation, foreign national
- **Reserves:** Typically 3–6 months PITIA
