## 5. Pilot proposal template (1-pager, customized per broker)

### Section 1: Executive summary (3 sentences)

"[Broker Name] is one of the top [rank] non-QM/DSCR brokers in [state]. We propose a 6-month pilot partnership giving [Broker Name] full access to our portfolio-DSCR analytics platform at no cost, in exchange for anonymized loan-level data to improve our model and a public case study at pilot end."

### Section 2: What we offer (5 bullets)

- Full Tier 4 v1 access (portfolio-DSCR analytics, adverse action reason codes, MRM docs)
- White-glove onboarding (1 FTE × 2 weeks dedicated to your team)
- Direct Slack channel to our product team
- Quarterly business reviews
- Co-marketing rights (logo on our site, joint case study)

### Section 3: What we ask (3 bullets)

- 30-min kickoff call within 2 weeks of signing
- Access to anonymized loan-level data (with your standard NDA)
- One named point of contact on your team

### Section 4: Pilot economics

- **Cost to [Broker Name]:** $0 for 6 months
- **Your Year 1 cost if you convert:** $X/year (tier-based, see Tier 4 v1 pricing model — Thread M)
- **What we save you (estimate):** $Y/year in underwriting FTE costs

### Section 5: Success metrics (3-6 months)

- # of loans processed through our platform
- Avg underwriting time per loan (vs baseline)
- Adverse action reason code accuracy (validated against your manual review)
- Your team NPS for our product

### Section 6: Timeline

- Week 1: Sign MoU + kickoff call
- Week 2-3: Onboarding + data integration
- Week 4-24: Active pilot (6 months)
- Week 25-26: QBR + decision on Year 1 conversion

## 6. Pilot MoU — key terms (2-page)

**Standard terms (non-negotiable for v1 pilots):**
- 6-month term
- Full access at $0
- 30-day termination clause (either party)
- Standard NDA for data sharing
- No exclusivity (broker can use other analytics tools in parallel)
- No commitment to convert after pilot

**Negotiable terms:**
- Co-marketing rights (use of broker name in case study)
- Data scope (full vs. anonymized vs. aggregate only)
- SLA commitments (uptime, support response time)
- Number of broker LOs with access (limit to 10-20 for v1)

**Hard line (won't negotiate):**
- We retain all IP rights to our platform + models
- We can use anonymized/aggregated data for model improvement
- We do NOT provide compensation for pilot data sharing

## 7. Pilot success criteria (5 metrics)

| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| # loans processed | 50+ per quarter | Platform telemetry |
| Underwriting time reduction | 30%+ vs baseline | Broker self-report + telemetry |
| Adverse action accuracy | 90%+ vs manual review | Quarterly audit (10-20 sample) |
| Broker team NPS | 40+ (industry avg ~30) | Quarterly survey |
| Renewal intent | 70%+ say "likely" or "definitely" | Quarterly QBR exit interview |

## 8. Conversion playbook (pilot → paid Year 1)

### Trigger: pilot month 5 QBR

- Review success metrics with broker
- Present Year 1 commercial proposal (per Thread M pricing model)
- Address any blockers identified during pilot
- Co-develop Year 1 success plan

### Pricing conversation framework

1. **Anchor on value, not cost:** "You saved $X in FTE time and got $Y in faster funding decisions. Year 1 is $Z."
2. **Tier to broker size:** Starter / Pro / Enterprise per Thread M pricing model
3. **Multi-year discount:** 10% off Year 2 if Year 1 paid (reduces churn risk)
4. **Case study leverage:** "We'll feature you as a case study at MBA Annual 2027 — that's X leads per month in equivalent value"

### Common objections and responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive" | "How much did you spend on [legacy tool X] last year? Our pilot data shows we cut your underwriting time by 30% — that's $X in FTE savings alone." |
| "Not enough features" | "Which 2 features are blocking you? We can prioritize in our Q1 2027 roadmap if you sign a 2-year agreement." |
| "Need to see ROI longer" | "We'll extend your pilot by 3 months at 50% off (pro-rated)." |
| "Already using competitor" | "Great — let us run a 30-day head-to-head. If we don't win, we'll refund your pilot." |
| "Internal procurement will take 6 months" | "We can start a letter of intent now; switch to paid when your procurement clears." |
