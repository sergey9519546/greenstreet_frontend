## Refinement / Caveats

1. **Loss number rounding:** 0.03% (3 bps) is the headline. KBRA's October 2025 refresh says "below 5 bps." Original 0.03% may be slightly conservative depending on dataset cut; **recommend corpus phrasing: "approximately 3 basis points (below 5 bps)"** to be safe.
2. **Cumulative default refreshed to 3.2%** in October 2025 update (KBRA-rated subset) — the **3.8% figure includes both KBRA-rated and non-KBRA-rated (via Cotality data)** per NMN; the 3.2% is KBRA-rated only.
3. **Sample composition:** 475K loans / ~600 securitizations / 2015–April 2025 — does not include pre-2015 NQM legacy.
4. **Vintage concentration:** 2019–2020 vintages elevated (~5–5.5%) due to COVID; 2022–2023 at ~4–4.1%.
5. **Cross-check:** Fitch reports non-QM impairments +9 bps in April (slowing pace); DBRS Morningstar 60+ day aggregate at 3.78%. KBRA's 3.8% sits in consensus.
6. **NMN disagrees on directional trend:** NMN quotes KBRA's own statement that "NQM delinquency and defaults have been trending higher. Although it may be premature to call this an inflection point…" — corpus should note "rising trend" caveat.
