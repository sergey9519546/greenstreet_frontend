## Critical Restrictions
- **STR Income:** Max 70% LTV/CLTV and DSCR >= 1.0 (12-mo documented only)
- **IO:** Min 700 FICO, Max 75% LTV (1.0+) or 70% LTV (0.75); not eligible No-Ratio
- **Buydowns:** NOT eligible
- **First-Time Investors:** Min 700 FICO, DSCR > 1.0, no exceptions
- **First-Time Homebuyers:** NOT eligible
- **Non-Permanent Resident Alien:** Max 75% LTV, No Cash Out
- **2-4 Unit:** Max 75% LTV
- **Warrantable Condo:** Max 75% LTV
- **Non-Warrantable Condo:** Max 75% LTV
- **Rural:** NOT eligible
- **Declining Markets:** Max LTV -5%

## Reserves
- Loan <= $500K: 3 months
- $500K-$2M: 6 months
- Additional 6 months for other financed properties

## Cash-Out
- LTV > 60%: Max cash out $500K
- LTV <= 60%: No limit
- Must not be used for personal use

## Borrower Eligibility
- US Citizens
- Permanent Resident Aliens (with I-151, I-551, etc.)
- Non-Permanent Resident Aliens (E-1, E-2, E-3, EB-5, G-1-G5, H-1B, L-1, NATO, O-1, R-1, TN — SSN required)
- LLC, LP, GP, Corp (4 max entity owners; 25%+ ownership guarantor)
- Layered entities: up to 2 layers (no trust layering)
- **NOT eligible:** Diplomatic immunity, asylum, IL land trust, blind trust, land trust, irrevocable trust, non-profit, OFAC, Foreign Nationals, ITIN, tenants in common (unless matching borrowers)

## Pre-foreclosure Constraints
- Max 20 financed properties per borrower/guarantor
- Max 10 financed properties or $7.5M UPB serviced by Pennymac

## Required Documents
- Form 1007 (1-unit income property)
- Form 1025 (2-4 unit small residential income property)
- 120-day credit / appraisal / preliminary title
- Fannie Mae CU <= 2.5 OR Freddie LCA <= 2.5 OR Clear Capital CDA / Stewart ARR
- Two appraisals required for >$2M loan or HPML flip
