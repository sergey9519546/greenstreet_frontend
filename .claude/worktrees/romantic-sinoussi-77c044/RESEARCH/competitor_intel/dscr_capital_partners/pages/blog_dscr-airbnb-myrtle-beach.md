Myrtle Beach is one of the largest drive-to beach STR markets on the East Coast, with a lower entry price than most coastal markets and a deep base of vacation-rental demand along the Grand Strand. ## Two Local Wrinkles: Zoning and Condo Type

Two Myrtle-Beach-specific items are worth checking before you write an offer:

- **Zoning overlay** — short-term rentals are broadly part of the economy here, but the City of Myrtle Beach restricts STRs to certain zoning/overlay districts, and rules differ in North Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach, and unincorporated Horry County. Confirm the parcel allows STR use.
- **Condo vs. condotel** — much of the inventory is condo and oceanfront resort-style product. A standard warrantable condo finances like any DSCR; a **condotel** (resort/hotel-style with a rental program) is a different underwrite — see our [condotel DSCR program](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/blog/dscr-loan-for-condotels/). Knowing which one you're buying up front avoids a late-stage surprise.
