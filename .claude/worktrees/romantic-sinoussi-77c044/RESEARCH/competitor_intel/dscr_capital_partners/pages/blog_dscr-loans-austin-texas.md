## Why Austin Works for DSCR Investors

Austin's investor reset is the story of 2026. **Pricing corrected 15–25%** from 2022 peaks across most sub-markets, while rents have stabilized. The result: DSCRs that didn't pencil for years are now workable in Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Round Rock, and east Austin. The **tech-employment base** (Tesla, Apple, Oracle, Indeed, plus a deep startup ecosystem) supports continued tenant demand.

## Austin Sub-Markets & Neighborhoods

### East Austin (78702, 78721, 78723)

Mid-renovation cash-flow side of Austin. SFR entry $400K–$650K, strong rents, DSCRs 1.05–1.25 at 75% LTV. Strong appreciation upside.

### Cedar Park, Round Rock, Pflugerville

Suburban SFR with the best DSCR math in the Austin metro. Entry $375K–$525K, rents $2,200–$2,900, DSCRs 1.10–1.30 routine.

### Mueller & North Loop

Walkable urban-suburban with strong tenant credit. Higher entry pricing requires larger down payments.

### Buda, Kyle, San Marcos

Outer-ring cash flow. SFR entry $300K–$420K, strong DSCRs (1.20–1.35) at 75% LTV. San Marcos has Texas State University rental dynamics.

Is Austin still a good DSCR market after the 2022 correction? +

Yes — arguably better than it was at the 2022 peak. Pricing reset 15–25% across most sub-markets while rents stabilized. Cedar Park, Pflugerville, and east Austin now produce DSCRs that didn't pencil 18 months ago.

What is the minimum credit score for an Austin DSCR loan? +

620 minimum; best pricing at 720+. Austin's higher entry prices typically require 680+ for the strongest pricing.

Can I get a DSCR loan for an Austin Airbnb? +

Yes — but verify the City of Austin STR ordinance carefully. Austin STR licensing is restrictive, with Type 2 (non-owner-occupied) STRs heavily limited. We finance permitted STRs using AirDNA full-year projections.

Can I do a DSCR cash-out refinance in Austin? +

Yes. Austin DSCR cash-out refinances are available up to 75% LTV. Common for investors who bought before the 2022 correction looking to recapture equity for other deals.

Are foreign national DSCR loans available in Austin? +

Yes. Austin is a meaningful foreign national DSCR market, particularly with tech-sector buyer activity from India, China, and the EU.
