## Frequently Asked Questions

Is now a good time to cash out a Phoenix rental after the run-up? +

Many owners have large trapped equity from the appreciation cycle. A DSCR cash-out (up to 75% LTV) lets you pull that equity and redeploy without selling or triggering a taxable event — qualifying on rent, not income.

Can I cash out a Phoenix or Scottsdale short-term rental? +

Yes. We underwrite STRs on AirDNA projected income. Confirm the city’s short-term-rental permit rules first (Scottsdale and some Phoenix areas regulate them).

Does Arizona have rent control that affects my DSCR? +

No — Arizona prohibits local rent control, so we qualify on market or in-place lease rent without a regulatory cap to model around.
