## Illinois Rental Market Snapshot — 2026

Illinois has the second-highest property tax rate in the country (effective rate ~2.0% in Cook County, ~2.1% in some collar suburbs). That single line item moves DSCR math more in Illinois than anywhere else outside of New Jersey. The math reverses downstate, where property tax burdens drop closer to the national average and DSCRs strengthen accordingly.

### What we're seeing on the ground in 2026

- **Chicago neighborhoods (Logan Square, Avondale, Pilsen, Humboldt Park):** Median entry $475K-$650K, 2-flats from $550K, gross rents $2,200-$3,600/unit. DSCRs typically 0.95-1.15 — appreciation-plus-cash-flow play, not pure yield.
- **Suburban Cook + DuPage/Will/Lake (Naperville, Oak Park, Aurora, Joliet, Schaumburg):** SFR median $385K-$550K, rents $2,200-$3,200, DSCRs 1.05-1.30. The bulk of Chicago metro DSCR volume closes here.
- **Rockford / Peoria / Quad Cities:** SFR median $145K-$215K, rents $1,150-$1,650. DSCRs 1.35-1.65 — cleanest cash flow in the state.
- **Champaign-Urbana, Bloomington-Normal:** University-anchored, $215K-$310K SFR, rents $1,400-$2,200. STR-on-game-weekend strategy plus 12-month leases produce blended DSCRs of 1.20-1.40.
- **Springfield:** State-capital stability, SFR median $165K-$245K, rents $1,200-$1,650. DSCRs 1.25-1.45.

## Why Illinois DSCR Pricing Looks Different (and Where the Real Cost Sits)

Three Illinois-specific factors move DSCR pricing vs. a comparable Indiana or Wisconsin deal:

- **Judicial foreclosure + Cook County backlog.** Illinois is a judicial foreclosure state — average timeline to sheriff sale is 14-18 months statewide, 18-24+ months in Cook County. Lenders price this risk: expect 0.125-0.25% higher rates and one extra month of reserves required on Cook County collateral.
- **Property tax burden.** Effective property tax rates of ~2.0% (Cook) and up to 2.3% (south suburban Cook) compress DSCR math directly. A $1,000/month tax bill on a $600K Chicago property is unremarkable; the same property in Indianapolis would have a $300/month bill.
- **Transfer taxes.** Chicago city transfer tax of 0.75% (buyer) + 0.30% (seller) plus Cook County 0.05% plus Illinois state 0.10% stacks to roughly **1.20%** of purchase price in transfer taxes on Chicago city closings. Downstate is much lighter.

What's _not_ a problem: Illinois is otherwise a stable, mature DSCR market. The wholesale lenders we shop all approve Illinois broadly, including 2-4 unit small multifamily, which is one of Chicago's most popular property types.

## Chicago 2-Flats and 3-Flats: A DSCR Sweet Spot

Chicago's neighborhood housing stock is dominated by 2-flats and 3-flats — small multifamily where the owner historically lived upstairs and rented downstairs. From a DSCR lender's perspective, these are gold:

- **All units' rent counts toward DSCR.** A Logan Square 2-flat with $2,600/month upstairs and $2,400/month down counts $5,000/month against PITIA. Single-family at the same price point would have ~$3,200 in rent.
- **2-4 unit is still residential lending.** No commercial underwriting, no DCR-based cap-rate model — same DSCR math as a SFR, just with stacked rent.
- **80% LTV available on 2-4 unit purchases.** Same leverage as SFR, despite the higher unit count.
- **Conversion-up strategy.** Many investors buy a 2-flat priced as a 1-unit (because of cosmetic condition) and convert/reposition the second unit. DSCR cash-out refi later supports this.

The Chicago multifamily playbook does require attention to two compliance items: building must be legally non-owner-occupied (CRLTO does not apply to investor-occupied buildings under 7 units, but city certificate-of-occupancy must reflect the actual use), and any unit count above what the C of O shows is a problem for the appraiser.

## Chicago STR (Airbnb) DSCR: Licensed Only

Chicago has one of the strictest STR licensing regimes in a major US city. Properties must be registered (Shared Housing Registration or Vacation Rental License) through a city-licensed intermediary platform, and the city caps the number of licensed STRs in many neighborhoods. Unlicensed STR cannot be financed as STR — only as a long-term rental.

We finance permitted Chicago STRs using AirDNA full-year revenue projections (typically haircut to 70-80% of projected gross). The market is real but limited; most investors looking for STR DSCR in Illinois find downstate university markets or the Galena / Starved Rock vacation corridor easier to work in.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Is Chicago a good DSCR market in 2026? +

Chicago neighborhood markets (Logan Square, Pilsen, Avondale, Humboldt Park, Bridgeport) are workable for DSCR investors targeting 2-flat and 3-flat conversions. Pure cash flow is tight given Cook County's heavy property tax burden, but appreciation-plus-cash-flow strategies pencil. Suburban Cook County and the collar counties (DuPage, Will, Lake) generally cash flow better.

What is the minimum credit score for an Illinois DSCR loan? +

620 minimum; best pricing at 720+. Most Illinois DSCR closings happen at 680-720 with 25% down.

How does Cook County's foreclosure timeline affect DSCR pricing? +

Illinois is a judicial foreclosure state and Cook County is the slowest jurisdiction in the country — 18-24 months from notice to sheriff sale is typical. DSCR lenders price this risk in: expect 0.125-0.25% higher rates and 1 extra month of reserves required on Cook County collateral vs. other Illinois counties.

Are Chicago 2-flats and 3-flats financeable with DSCR? +

Yes. 2-4 unit small multifamily is one of the strongest DSCR property types. Chicago has a deep inventory of 2-flats and 3-flats in neighborhoods like Logan Square, Avondale, and Pilsen that work well for DSCR. Rent across all units rolls into the DSCR calculation.

Can I get a DSCR loan in downstate Illinois? +

Yes. Rockford, Peoria, Champaign-Urbana, Springfield, Bloomington-Normal, and the Quad Cities all see active DSCR financing. Downstate has materially lower property taxes than Cook County, which strengthens DSCRs at the same price point.

How do Chicago STR rules affect DSCR financing? +

Chicago has strict STR licensing — only registered short-term rentals (Shared Housing Registration) and intermediary platforms with city licenses are legal. We finance permitted Chicago STRs using AirDNA projections, but the legal STR inventory is limited. Suburban municipalities have their own STR rules; check before contracting.
