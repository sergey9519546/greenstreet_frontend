## The Smoky Mountain Cabin DSCR Profile

The Smokies are one of the most established short-term-rental markets in the country — overnight tourism _is_ the local economy, which is exactly why lenders are comfortable here. The program mirrors the universal [Airbnb / Short-Term Rental DSCR program](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/blog/dscr-loan-for-airbnb/): we qualify the cabin's projected STR income against the new payment, not your W-2.

## What's Different vs. a Regulated Metro Like Nashville

Unlike Metro Nashville, Sevier County and the Gatlinburg/Pigeon Forge corridor do **not** impose a restrictive non-owner-occupied permit ban — overnight cabin rentals are permitted and expected. You'll still need the standard city/county **overnight rental permit and business license**, but the zoning question that kills deals in regulated metros generally isn't the obstacle here.

The real underwriting nuance is income realism. The market softened off its 2021–2022 peak, so we typically underwrite Smoky Mountain cabins at **70–75% of AirDNA projected gross** (and lean on trailing-12-month actuals when the cabin has booking history). Budget carefully for the operating-cost line too — cleaning, management, and the higher Sevier County occupancy/lodging tax eat into real cash flow even when the loan itself pencils.
