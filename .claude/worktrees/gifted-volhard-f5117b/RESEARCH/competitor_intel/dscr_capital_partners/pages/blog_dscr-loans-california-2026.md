## What Is a DSCR Loan?

A DSCR loan — Debt Service Coverage Ratio loan — qualifies the borrower based on the rental income of the subject property rather than personal income. The qualifying ratio is the property's gross monthly rent divided by its monthly debt obligations (PITIA: principal, interest, taxes, insurance, HOA).

### DSCR Formula

DSCR = Gross Monthly Rental Income ÷ Monthly Debt Obligations

A 1.0 DSCR means the property breaks even. Higher ratios produce better pricing.

Because qualification is based on the property, DSCR loans require no W-2s, no tax returns, no employment verification, and no debt-to-income calculation.

## Why California Is a Strong Market for DSCR Loans

Three forces are driving California DSCR activity: **institutional retreat** from the SFR space leaving room for individual investors, **build-to-rent expansion** in the Inland Empire and Central Valley, and **tenant law clarity** after AB 1482's full implementation. Coastal markets remain expensive but pencil at lower LTVs; inland markets — Riverside, San Bernardino, Bakersfield, Fresno, Stockton — produce DSCRs above 1.10 on standard 75% LTV financing.

California's tenant protection regime (AB 1482, just-cause eviction, statewide rent caps) is something every California investor should understand before closing. Our DSCR program does not change qualification based on local rent control — it qualifies based on actual or appraiser-determined rent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Are DSCR loans available in California with rent control under AB 1482? +

Yes. AB 1482 caps annual rent increases but does not change DSCR qualification. We use the appraiser-determined market rent or in-place lease for the qualifying calculation. The California rent cap is something to plan around for cash-flow modeling, not a barrier to financing.

Can I get a DSCR loan for an LA or San Diego short-term rental? +

Yes — but check local STR ordinances first. LA, San Diego, and Santa Monica all have STR registration and zoning rules. As long as the property is legally permitted as an STR, our short-term rental DSCR program will finance it using AirDNA projected revenue.

What is the highest LTV available on a California DSCR loan? +

Up to 80% on most California DSCR purchases; 85% case-by-case on stronger files (740+ FICO, DSCR above 1.20). Cash-out refinances cap at 75%. Coastal markets and high-cost areas may see slightly tighter LTV.

Are foreign national DSCR loans available in California? +

Yes. We close foreign national DSCR loans throughout California, with significant Canadian and Asian foreign-investor activity in LA, San Diego, and the Bay Area.

Can I close a California DSCR loan in an LLC? +

Yes. California DSCR loans close in single-member or multi-member LLCs. Note California's $800 minimum franchise tax on LLCs holding California real estate — this matters for cash-flow projections, not qualification.
