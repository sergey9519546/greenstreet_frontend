## Why Orlando Works for DSCR Investors

Orlando's investor pull is unique. The **Disney/theme park economy** sustains the largest concentrated vacation-rental market in the country, with thousands of investor-owned units in Reunion, Champions Gate, Storey Lake, and the I-4 corridor. The Lake Nona/Medical City corridor adds a high-credit, long-term rental tenant base from healthcare, biotech, and the new University of Central Florida medical school.

## Orlando Sub-Markets & Neighborhoods

### Kissimmee, Davenport & ChampionsGate

The vacation-rental capital of the country. SFR pool homes at $400K–$800K with AirDNA projections supporting strong DSCRs. Reunion, Storey Lake, Solara are heavy-investor communities.

### Lake Nona & Medical City

High-credit long-term rental market driven by healthcare and biotech employment. SFR DSCRs 1.05–1.20 at 75% LTV; townhome rentals strong.

### Winter Park & College Park

Older established residential with appreciation history. Higher entry, tighter DSCRs but tenant quality strong.

### Apopka, Ocoee, Clermont

Cash-flow-leaning western Orange County. SFR DSCRs 1.15–1.30 routine at 75% LTV.

## Orlando DSCR Loan Requirements

### 2026 Requirements — Orlando

- **Minimum credit score:** 620 (best pricing at 740+)
- **Minimum DSCR:** 0.75 (1.0+ preferred)
- **Maximum LTV:** Up to 85% purchase / 75% cash-out refinance
- **Loan amount:** $100,000 to $15,000,000
- **Property types:** SFR, condo, 2–4 unit, 5+ unit multifamily, condotel
- **Loan terms:** 30-year fixed, ARM options, interest-only
- **Borrower types:** Individual, LLC, corporation, foreign national
- **Reserves:** Typically 3–6 months PITIA

## Frequently Asked Questions

Is Orlando a good DSCR STR market? +

Orlando is the largest STR DSCR market in the U.S. The Disney/I-4 corridor (Kissimmee, Davenport, ChampionsGate) supports thousands of investor-owned vacation-rental SFRs with AirDNA projections clearing strong DSCRs.

What HOAs allow STRs in the Disney area? +

Reunion, ChampionsGate, Storey Lake, Solara, Windsor Hills, Windsor at Westside, Encore at Reunion, and Bahama Bay are the major STR-permitted communities. Always verify the specific HOA's current STR rules before contracting.

What is the minimum credit score for an Orlando DSCR loan? +

620 minimum; best pricing at 720+. Orlando STR DSCR loans typically require 680+ for the strongest pricing.

Can foreign nationals buy Orlando vacation rentals with a DSCR loan? +

Yes. Orlando is one of our most active foreign national DSCR markets, with significant Canadian, UK, and Latin American buyer activity in the Disney STR corridor.

Are Orlando condotels eligible for DSCR financing? +

Yes. We finance Orlando-area condotels including projects in the Disney corridor and downtown Orlando. Underwriting reviews HOA financials and STR ordinance compatibility.
