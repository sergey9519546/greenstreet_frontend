## Stage 1: AWARENESS (0-3 months before application)

### Triggers
- "I want to start building passive income"
- "I'm buying my first investment property"
- "I'm scaling from 2 to 10 properties"
- "Self-employed, can't qualify for conventional"
- "I'm a foreign national wanting to invest in US real estate"

### Discovery Channels (estimated mix)
| Channel | % | Touchpoint |
|---------|---|------------|
| BiggerPockets community | 25% | Forum, podcast, YouTube |
| Mortgage broker introduction | 25% | Real estate agent referral |
| Google search ("DSCR loan", "investment property loan") | 20% | Organic + paid |
| Social media (Instagram, TikTok) | 10% | RE influencer |
| Podcast (BiggerPockets, REI) | 8% | Long-form discovery |
| Real estate meetup / REIA | 5% | Local network |
| Lead-gen site (LendingTree) | 4% | Comparison shopping |
| Property management platform (Stessa) | 3% | Existing landlord user |

### Key Questions
- "How much do I need down?"
- "What credit score do I need?"
- "Can I close in an LLC?"
- "What's the current DSCR rate?"

### Content Assets Needed
- "DSCR Loan 101" blog post (per persona)
- "DSCR vs Conventional" comparison
- "Can I qualify for DSCR?" quiz
- FAQ page
- Lender comparison table

## Stage 2: CONSIDERATION (1-4 weeks)

### Comparison Behavior
- Quotes 2-3 lenders minimum
- Compares rate + fees + speed
- Verifies state licensing
- Reads reviews (Google, Trustpilot)

### Key Decision Factors (ranked)
1. **Rate** (35% weight)
2. **Speed of close** (20%)
3. **DSCR floor flexibility** (15%)
4. **LTV max** (10%)
5. **Customer service** (10%)
6. **Brand reputation** (5%)
7. **Other (specialty programs, broker relationship)** (5%)

### Key Questions
- "What's my actual DSCR ratio?"
- "How long does DSCR underwriting take?"
- "Can I get a soft pre-qual without hard pull?"
- "Do you accept ITIN / Foreign National?"
- "What's the closing timeline?"

### Content Assets Needed
- Live DSCR calculator
- Rate comparison page
- "How fast can you close?" page
- Specialty program pages (FN, ITIN, STR)
- Pre-qual form (soft pull)

---

## Stage 3: APPLICATION (1-4 weeks)

### Documents Required (typical DSCR)
- Purchase contract (if purchase)
- Lease agreement (if in place)
- Appraisal (1007 market rent + property valuation)
- Entity documents (LLC operating agreement, EIN, Articles)
- Borrower ID (driver's license, passport for FN)
- Bank statements (if reserves verification required)
- Insurance quote (binding-ready)

### Friction Points (high failure modes)
- Insurance binding (Domain 8: 3-15bd depending on geography)
- Appraisal coming in low (mitigation: rental AVM lock at pre-qual like Angel Oak)
- Entity vesting mismatch with state requirements
- Insurance kill criterion (FL/CA/TX/LA Coastal)

### Key Questions
- "What if my appraisal comes in low?"
- "Can I switch from individual to LLC after closing?"
- "How does DSCR handle rent not in place?"

---

## Stage 4: DECISION / CLOSE (15-30 days)

### Final Decision Criteria
- Rate lock terms (30/45/60 day)
- Closing cost transparency
- Title company flexibility
- Insurance bind timing

### Triggers for "Don't Close" (Lost Deal)
- Insurance unbindable (FL coastal >$15K premium)
- Appraisal $50K+ below contract
- DSCR falls below 1.0 with actual rent
- Entity vesting issue (state requires entity, borrower didn't have one)

---

## Stage 5: POST-CLOSE (Months 1-12)

### Onboarding
- Welcome packet (servicing transfer if applicable)
- Insurance renewal reminder
- Tax document prep (1098, interest statement)
- Annual review invitation

### Retention Drivers
1. **First renewal (rate/term refi)** — 24-36 months in, ~60% refinance with same lender
2. **2nd property** — 12-24 months in, ~40% scale up
3. **Portfolio expansion** — 24+ months in, ~30% consider portfolio loan

### Cross-Sell Opportunities
- HELOC / second mortgage
- 1031 exchange planning
- Cost segregation study (tax efficiency)
- Insurance renewal aggregator (Layr, Tarmika)
- Property management platform (Stessa, Roofstock)

---

## Stage 6: RENEWAL / REPEAT (24-60 months)

### Refinance Triggers
- Rate drop ≥75bps
- Property value up ≥15%
- DSCR improvement (1.0 → 1.25+)
- New property purchase (BRRRR or portfolio)

### Repeat Origination Patterns
- Average repeat origination interval: 14 months
- Average portfolio growth per year: +1.5 properties
- Cross-sell attach rate at refi: 40-50%

## Persona-Specific Journey Variants

### Persona 1 (First-Time SFR Investor) — Slowest, Highest Education Need
- Awareness stage: 3-6 months (not aware DSCR exists)
- Consideration: 4-8 weeks (overwhelmed by choices)
- Application: Often requires broker hand-holding
- Decision: Most sensitive to rate; least sensitive to speed
- Post-close: Highest churn; needs education on next steps

### Persona 2 (Scaling Portfolio Builder) — Fastest, Most Loyal
- Awareness: knows DSCR intimately (repeat borrower)
- Consideration: 1-2 weeks
- Application: sophisticated; demands portfolio lender (Insula)
- Decision: most sensitive to rate + speed + portfolio capability
- Post-close: lowest churn; highest LTV

### Persona 3 (Mid-Tier STR Operator) — Most Volatile
- Awareness: often comes from STR community (BiggerPockets, AirDNA)
- Consideration: 2-4 weeks
- Application: STR-specific docs (AirDNA, projected income)
- Decision: needs STR-specialty lender (Easy Street)
- Post-close: medium churn; sensitive to STR regulation changes

### Persona 4 (Self-Employed W-2 Skip) — Most Documentation Friction
- Awareness: often self-directed search ("loans for self-employed")
- Consideration: 2-3 weeks
- Application: high friction on bank statements
- Decision: needs bank-statement specialty (Angel Oak, Acra)
- Post-close: medium churn; high LTV

### Persona 5 (Foreign National) — Highest Service Demand
- Awareness: international referral network
- Consideration: 4-6 weeks (POA restrictions)
- Application: complex FN docs
- Decision: needs FN specialty (Angel Oak, Verus)
- Post-close: medium churn; service-heavy

### Persona 6 (ITIN Borrower) — Highest Acquisition Cost
- Awareness: bilingual broker networks
- Consideration: 4-8 weeks
- Application: ITIN-specific docs
- Decision: rate-sensitive; limited lender pool
- Post-close: high churn; needs continuous education

### Persona 7 (Institutional Portfolio) — Relationship-Driven
- Awareness: family office / wealth advisor
- Consideration: 8-12 weeks (large dollar)
- Application: complex portfolio docs
- Decision: needs Insula/Lima One/Ready Capital
- Post-close: highest retention; relationship-driven
