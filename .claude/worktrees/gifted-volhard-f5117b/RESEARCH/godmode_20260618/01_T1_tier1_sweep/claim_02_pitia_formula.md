# Claim 02 — DSCR PITIA Formula Audit Card

**Audit Date:** 2026-06-18
**Methodology:** 10x Deep-Research Verification
**Claim ID:** DSCR-SOV-CLAIM-02

---

## Claim Statement

**Claim:** For DSCR loans, "PITIA" denotes the monthly debt service used in the DSCR formula:
**PITIA = P&I + (1/12 annual property tax) + (1/12 annual insurance) + HOA dues**
And the DSCR formula is: **DSCR = Gross Monthly Rental Income ÷ PITIA**

**Prior corpus state:** Confirmed by 2 sources (Pennymac, Newfi). Required a 3rd independent source.

## Verdict

# **TIER 1 CONFIRMED**

## Confidence Score: **5 / 5**

## Rationale

- 3rd (and 4th–10th) independent source confirmed.
- The claim is **industry-standard**, not Pennymac-specific. The 1/12 monthly tax/insurance convention is universal across DSCR lenders and derives from conventional mortgage escrow treatment.
- The full PITIA decomposition is mechanically identical across correspondent (Pennymac, Lakeview), wholesale (Newfi, Deephaven, Verus), retail (Griffin Funding, theLender), and reference sources (OfferMarket, Bluerate).
- "A" (HOA) is correctly conditional — included only when the property has association dues.

## Refinements / Caveats

1. **ITIA variant:** When the DSCR loan is structured interest-only, the formula becomes ITIA (Interest + T + I + A) with P = 0. Sources note this variant.
2. **"Monthly debt service" terminology:** Some lenders (e.g., Newfi, OfferMarket) use "Monthly Expenses" or "Monthly Debt Service" interchangeably with PITIA. The acronym PITIA is dominant in DSCR practice; PITI is used when HOA = $0.
3. **Insurance calculation fallback:** When a borrower-provided insurance quote is unavailable, theLender uses an estimate of 0.35%–0.85% of loan amount annually divided by 12. This is lender-specific underwriting variation, not a deviation from the 1/12 convention.
