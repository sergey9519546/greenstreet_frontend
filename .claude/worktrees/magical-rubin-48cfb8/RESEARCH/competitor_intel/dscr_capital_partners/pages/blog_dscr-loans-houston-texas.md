## Why Houston Works for DSCR Investors

Houston's investor case rests on **employment depth and diversification** — energy, the Texas Medical Center, the Port of Houston, NASA Johnson Space Center, and a growing tech/data-center base. **No state income tax**, **no rent control**, and a **landlord-friendly Texas Property Code** further strengthen the cash-flow profile.

## Houston Sub-Markets & Neighborhoods

### Heights, Montrose & EaDo

Inner-loop appreciation plays. Higher entry pricing requires larger down payments to clear DSCR. Strong tenant credit, low vacancy.

### Sugar Land, Katy & Cinco Ranch

Master-planned suburban SFR. Strong tenant base from energy and medical employment. DSCRs 1.05–1.25 routine at 75% LTV.

### The Woodlands & Spring

Premium suburban market with high tenant credit. Multi-million dollar SFR common; condo/townhome more workable for DSCR.

### Cypress, Spring Branch, Aldine

Cash-flow neighborhoods. Lower entry prices ($180K–$320K), strong DSCRs (1.20–1.40) at 75% LTV.

## Houston DSCR Loan Requirements

### 2026 Requirements — Houston

- **Minimum credit score:** 620 (best pricing at 740+)
- **Minimum DSCR:** 0.75 (1.0+ preferred)
- **Maximum LTV:** Up to 85% purchase / 75% cash-out refinance
- **Loan amount:** $100,000 to $15,000,000
- **Property types:** SFR, condo, 2–4 unit, 5+ unit multifamily, condotel
- **Loan terms:** 30-year fixed, ARM options, interest-only
- **Borrower types:** Individual, LLC, corporation, foreign national
- **Reserves:** Typically 3–6 months PITIA

## Frequently Asked Questions

Is Houston a good DSCR market in 2026? +

Yes. Houston combines deep employment diversification, no state income tax, landlord-friendly law, and varied price points across the metro. Cash-flow neighborhoods routinely produce DSCRs above 1.20 at 75% LTV.

What is the minimum credit score for a Houston DSCR loan? +

620 minimum; best pricing at 720+. Houston's varied price points let borrowers in any credit tier find workable deals.

Can I get a DSCR cash-out refinance on a Houston rental? +

Yes. Houston investment properties qualify for DSCR cash-out refinances up to 75% LTV. BRRRR investors common in Spring Branch, Aldine, and Cypress.

Are Houston STR DSCR loans available? +

Yes. Houston STR financing is available — the Medical Center, downtown, and Galleria areas have growing business-traveler STR demand. Verify any HOA or city of Houston STR rules before contracting.

Can foreign nationals buy Houston rental property with a DSCR loan? +

Yes. Houston is a meaningful foreign national DSCR market, particularly with Latin American (Mexico, Venezuela, Colombia) and Indian buyer activity.
