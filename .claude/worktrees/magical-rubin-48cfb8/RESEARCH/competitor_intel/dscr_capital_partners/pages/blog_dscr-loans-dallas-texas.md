## Why Dallas Works for DSCR Investors

DFW's investor case is built on **corporate relocation depth** (Toyota, JPMorgan, Caterpillar, AT&T, Charles Schwab), **population growth** (Collin and Denton counties remain among the fastest-growing in the U.S.), and **landlord-friendly Texas law** with no state income tax and no rent control.

## Dallas Sub-Markets & Neighborhoods

### Uptown, Bishop Arts & Lower Greenville

Inner-Dallas appreciation markets. Higher entry pricing requires stronger down payments for DSCR qualification.

### Frisco, Plano, McKinney

Premier high-growth Collin County suburbs. Top-tier tenant credit. SFR DSCRs slightly tighter at 1.00–1.20 at 75% LTV but lower turnover and capex risk.

### Arlington, Mesquite, Garland

Cash-flow markets. SFR entry $220K–$380K with strong rents. DSCRs 1.15–1.35 routine at 75% LTV.

### Fort Worth (East & South)

Underrated DFW cash-flow side. Lower entry prices, strong rents from healthcare and aerospace employment.

## Dallas DSCR Loan Requirements

### 2026 Requirements — Dallas

- **Minimum credit score:** 620 (best pricing at 740+)
- **Minimum DSCR:** 0.75 (1.0+ preferred)
- **Maximum LTV:** Up to 85% purchase / 75% cash-out refinance
- **Loan amount:** $100,000 to $15,000,000
- **Property types:** SFR, condo, 2–4 unit, 5+ unit multifamily, condotel
- **Loan terms:** 30-year fixed, ARM options, interest-only
- **Borrower types:** Individual, LLC, corporation, foreign national
- **Reserves:** Typically 3–6 months PITIA

## Frequently Asked Questions

Is DFW a good DSCR market in 2026? +

Yes. DFW remains one of the strongest growth markets in the country — Collin County (Frisco, Plano, McKinney) for tenant-credit-driven holds, and Arlington/Garland/Mesquite for cash flow. DSCRs routinely clear 1.10–1.30 at 75% LTV.

What is the minimum credit score for a Dallas DSCR loan? +

620 minimum; best pricing at 720+. Higher-priced Frisco and Plano deals typically need 680+ to clear DSCR comfortably.

Can I get a DSCR loan for a Plano or Frisco rental? +

Yes. Plano and Frisco DSCR loans close routinely. The trade-off: tighter DSCRs at higher LTVs, but excellent tenant credit and low turnover. Larger down payments common.

Are Dallas STR DSCR loans available? +

Yes. Dallas STR financing is available — verify Dallas city STR registration requirements before contracting.

Can foreign nationals buy DFW rental property with a DSCR loan? +

Yes. DFW is a meaningful foreign national DSCR market, particularly with Indian, Chinese, and Mexican buyer activity in Plano, Frisco, and Irving.
