## 0. Executive Summary

DSCR borrowers are a **distinct demographic** from agency borrowers — predominantly small-to-mid portfolio investors using LLCs, with FICO 700-780, age 35-55, $200K-$1M liquid net worth, and 1-10 rental properties. **Verus S&P DSCR Presale 2025** provides the most authoritative pool-level data: 89.44% property-focused borrowers, WA DSCR 1.10x, 63.04% no lease in place at origination, 3.82% 30-day DQ at issuance.

**Headline findings:**
- **89.44%** DSCR loans are property-focused (Verus S&P DSCR Presale 2025, Tier 1)
- **63.04%** of DSCR loans originate with no lease in place (Tier 1)
- **3.82%** are 30-day delinquent at issuance (already underperforming pool cut)
- **WA FICO 746-758** in institutional pools (KBRA 2025-2026 deals)
- **70-80% LLC-vested** (industry standard, Tier 2 estimate)
- **60-70% repeat / 30-40% first-time** (Lender product page disclosures, Tier 2)
- **5-10% Foreign National / ITIN** (lender product pages, Tier 2)
- **Geographic concentration**: TX/FL/GA/NC/AZ top 5 states for DSCR origination volume
- **Channel mix**: 70-80% broker-referred; 10-15% direct lender; 5-10% digital

## 5. Borrower Retention

### 5.1 Repeat Origination Rate

| Metric | Estimated % | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| **Refinance with same lender** | 30-40% | Industry standard; lenders retain 60-70% at refi |
| **Repeat origination within 24 months** | 40-50% | Active DSCR borrowers typically acquire 1-2 new properties/yr |
| **5-year retention** | 50-60% | Brand loyalty moderate |

**Source**: Easy Street Capital, Movement Mortgage, JVM Lending product pages (Tier 2)

### 5.2 Top Retention Drivers

1. **Speed of close** (15-25 day typical; same-lender refinances faster)
2. **Rate lock consistency** (long-term relationship gets preferred lock policies)
3. **Portfolio lender (Insula-style)** for repeat borrowers scaling up
4. **Investor property management integration** (Stessa, Roofstock)
5. **Broker relationship continuity** (broker stays with investor)
