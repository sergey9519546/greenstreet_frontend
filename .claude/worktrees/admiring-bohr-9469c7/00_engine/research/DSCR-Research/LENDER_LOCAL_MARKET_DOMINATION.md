## 1.3 The "Avoid" List — Cities Where DSCR Doesn't Work Well

| City | Why to Avoid |
|------|-------------|
| **New York City, NY** | Rent control/stabilization laws; 2-3% gross yield; eviction takes 6-12 months; property tax is opaque and often exceeds DSCR capacity |
| **San Francisco, CA** | Gross yield <3.5%; rent control; just-cause eviction; $1M+ entry prices make DSCR math impossible on most properties |
| **Los Angeles, CA** | Rent stabilization on pre-1995 buildings; just-cause eviction; gross yield ~4%; insurance costs post-wildfires are skyrocketing |
| **Chicago, IL** | Cook County property tax is high and unpredictable; Chicago has tenant-friendly eviction courts that drag 60-90 days; some aldermen push for rent control |
| **Seattle, WA** | Just-cause eviction; first-in-time rental screening; gross yield ~4.5%; city-level landlord regulations are hostile |
| **Portland, OR** | Statewide rent cap (7% + CPI); just-cause eviction; relocation assistance required for no-fault evictions; gross yield ~5% |
| **Minneapolis, MN** | Tenant opportunity to purchase act; just-cause eviction; rent stabilization on ballot; property tax rising fast |
| **Newark, NJ** | Rent control; strong tenant protections; very high property tax (~2.8%); insurance costs are elevated |

**The Pattern:** Avoid cities with rent control, just-cause eviction, eviction timelines >45 days, and gross yields below 5.5%. These make DSCR lending either impossible (the math doesn't work) or extremely risky (you can't remove non-paying tenants quickly).

# SECTION 2: THE "BECOME A LOCAL" PLAYBOOK — WEEK BY WEEK

**Assumption:** You are moving to or spending 4 days/week in Memphis (our #1 pick). Adjust for your city.

## WEEK 1-2: INFILTRATE

### Day 1-2: Map the Territory

Before you attend a single meeting, do this:

1. **Google "[City] REIA" and "[City] real estate investors group"** — Find every group, their meeting schedule, their leader's name, and their typical attendance
2. **Search Facebook for "[City] landlords" and "[City] real estate investors"** — Join every group. Note: most require admin approval, so request immediately
3. **Search Meetup.com for real estate investing groups** in a 50-mile radius
4. **Pull the county property appraiser's website** — Learn how to look up any property's owner, tax bill, and assessed value in 30 seconds
5. **Identify the top 10 zip codes** for rental property concentration using Census/ACS data

### Day 3-4: Attend Every REIA Meeting

**Memphis Example — REIA Schedule:**

| Group | Meeting Day | Location | Typical Attendance |
|-------|------------|----------|-------------------|

**Attend ALL of them in your first two weeks.** Different groups = different investors. You want to meet everyone.

### What to Say at REIA Meetings — The "I'm New" Script

**DO NOT lead with your product.** You are new in town. You are curious. You are learning. People love helping newcomers.

> **Your 30-Second Introduction:**
>
> "Hey, I'm [Name]. I just moved to Memphis — I specialize in DSCR lending for investment properties. I'm not here to pitch anything tonight, I'm here to learn this market. What neighborhoods are you investing in? What's working for you right now?"

**Why This Works:** 
- It states your expertise (DSCR lending) without selling
- It shows humility and curiosity
- It asks a question that gets people talking about themselves (investors LOVE talking about their deals)
- It positions you as someone who is investing in the community (literally moved there)

**What to Bring:**
- Business cards (simple: Name, "DSCR Lending for Investment Properties," phone, email)
- A small notepad — write down names and what they invest in
- Nothing else. No brochures. No rate sheets. Not yet. You haven't earned the right.

### Day 5-6: Visit the Top 5 Real Estate Offices

Walk in. Ask for the managing broker. Shake their hand.

> **The Office Visit Script:**
>
> "Hi, I'm [Name]. I specialize in DSCR investment property lending and I'm setting up locally here in Memphis. I wanted to introduce myself to your office because I know your agents work with investors. I'm not asking for referrals today — I just want to make sure your agents know there's a LOCAL DSCR lender who can answer questions and close deals. Can I leave some cards and maybe come back to do a 15-minute lender update at one of your team meetings?"

**Why This Works:**
- Real estate offices get hit up by lenders constantly, but they're almost always conventional/FHA lenders
- A DSCR specialist is novel and valuable
- You're not asking for anything — you're offering local expertise
- The "15-minute lender update" is a low-pressure way to get in front of the whole office

### Day 7-8: Meet the Top 3 Property Management Companies

Property managers are GOLD for DSCR lenders. They know every investor who owns more than one property. They know who's expanding. They know who's struggling.

> **The Property Manager Script:**
>
> "Hi, I'm [Name]. I do DSCR lending for investment properties — we qualify loans based on the property's cash flow, not the borrower's income. I know your owners are always looking for financing to expand their portfolios. I'd love to be a resource for you — if any of your owners have questions about whether a property will cash-flow for a DSCR loan, I can run the numbers for them in 10 minutes, no commitment. Can I take you to lunch next week?"

**Key Insight:** Property managers are often the FIRST to know when an investor wants to buy another property (they get a call to manage it before the deal closes). If the PM knows you and trusts you, they refer the borrower to you before they even call another lender.

### Day 9-10: Visit 2-3 Local Title Companies

Title officers see every transaction. They know which investors are buying, which lenders are being used, and what's closing.

# SECTION 6: HOW TO WORK THE REIA MEETING — THE EXACT STRATEGY

## Before the Meeting

**1. Get the attendee list** (if possible). Many REIAs share their RSVP list. If not, look at the Facebook event page to see who's attending.

**2. Target 5 specific people** you want to meet. If it's your first time, target: the organizer, the speaker, and 3 people who commented on the Facebook event. If you've been before, target people you met last time and new faces.

**3. Prepare your "conversation starters":**
- "What neighborhood are you investing in?"
- "Are you buying or holding right now?"
- "What's the biggest challenge you're facing with financing?"
- "Have you used DSCR loans before?"

**4. Bring:**
- 50 business cards
- A small notepad and pen
- Your phone with the DSCR calculator loaded
- A friendly, approachable demeanor (no salesy energy)

## During the Meeting

### The Pre-Meeting Mingle (15-30 minutes before the presentation)

This is where the real business happens. Most deals are made BEFORE the speaker starts.

**Your strategy:**
1. **Position yourself near the food/registration table.** Everyone passes through here.
2. **Introduce yourself to every person who walks in.** "Hey, I'm [Name]. First time here / Great to see you again. What are you working on?"
3. **Listen more than you talk.** Ask questions. Take mental notes.
4. **When someone mentions financing, lean in:** "That's exactly what I do — DSCR lending for investment properties. Tell me more about the deal."

### The Presentation

**If you're NOT speaking:** Sit near the front. Pay attention. Take notes. When the speaker mentions something related to financing, make a mental note to talk to them after.

**If you're speaking:** See your 5-minute sponsor pitch script above. For a full speaking slot (20-30 minutes), structure it as:

1. **Hook (2 min):** "How many of you have been turned down for a conventional loan because of your tax returns?" (Hands go up.) "That's why DSCR exists."
2. **Education (10 min):** How DSCR works, what makes it different, when to use it vs. other products
3. **Local Application (10 min):** "Here's how DSCR works SPECIFICALLY in [City]" — show real calculations with local data
4. **Q&A (8 min):** Answer every question thoroughly. If you don't know, say "I'll find out and email you tomorrow" (then DO IT)
5. **Close (2 min):** "I'm [Name], I'm your LOCAL DSCR lender. Text me any address and I'll run the DSCR for you by tomorrow morning. My number is [number]."

### The Post-Meeting Mingle (15-30 minutes after)

This is where you book meetings. Don't rush out.

1. **Talk to the speaker** — they're the center of attention, and everyone else is talking to them too. Wait your turn, introduce yourself, and ask a specific question about their presentation.
2. **Circle back to your top 5 targets** — "Hey, great to meet you earlier. I was thinking about what you said about [specific thing]. Have you considered [DSCR approach]?"
3. **Collect business cards** — write a note on each one immediately: what you discussed, what follow-up is needed.
4. **Set 2-3 coffee meetings** before you leave. "I'd love to learn more about your portfolio. Can we grab coffee this week?"

## After the Meeting

**The 24-Hour Rule:** Follow up with every contact within 24 hours. Not 48. Not "this weekend." 24 hours.

**Follow-up template:**

> "Hey [Name], great meeting you at [REIA name] last night. I loved hearing about your [specific thing they mentioned — their portfolio, their strategy, their challenge]. I'd love to continue the conversation. Are you free for coffee this week? I'm also happy to run a free DSCR analysis on any property you're looking at — just send me the address. Talk soon, [Your Name]"

**Track every contact in a CRM** (even a simple spreadsheet):
- Name

- What they invest in
- What they need
- Last contact date
- Next action
- Referral source

        │   ┌─────────────────────┐   │

Expand to the next 2-3 cities in the same state.

**For Memphis (TN base):** Nashville and Knoxville

### Circle 3: Adjacent State(s) (Months 18-24)

Expand to a bordering state with favorable DSCR conditions.

**Key Rule:** NEVER expand to a state with worse DSCR fundamentals than your home state. Always move toward better yield, lower tax, friendlier laws.

## The Mistake of Expanding Too Early

**The #1 killer of local domination strategies:** Getting bored or greedy and expanding to City #2 before you've truly won City #1.

**Signs you're expanding too early:**
- You're still getting most of your City #1 deals from marketing, not referrals
- A competitor could steal your City #1 market share if you took your foot off the gas
- You can't name the top 5 investors, top 3 agents, and top property manager in City #1 by memory
- Your content doesn't rank #1 for "DSCR loan [city #1]"
- You haven't spoken at the main REIA in City #1 more than 3 times

**The discipline:** Stay in City #1 until you're genuinely bored because it runs on autopilot. If you're still hustling for every deal, you haven't won yet.

## How to Replicate the Playbook Faster in City #2

City #1 took 6-9 months. City #2 should take 3-4 months. Here's why:

1. **You have a template.** The content model, the calculator, the scripts — all done. Just swap the city name and data.
2. **You have a brand.** "The DSCR lender who dominated [City #1] is now in [City #2]" — that's a story. Use it.
3. **You have a network.** Your City #1 Power Network probably knows people in City #2. Warm introductions beat cold calls.
4. **You have a system.** Your CRM, your follow-up sequences, your content calendar — all operationalized.
5. **You can hire.** By City #2, you should have revenue to hire a local person to execute the playbook while you oversee.
