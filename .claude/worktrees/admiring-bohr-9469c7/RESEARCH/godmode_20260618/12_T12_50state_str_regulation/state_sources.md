## Citation Notes & Conflicts

- **Maryland** — Two conflicting readings: Avalara (Mar 2026) reports Ocean City ended the residential STR moratorium and is moving to a 5-night min (rising to 31 nights in 2027). Checkmate Rentals (2025) says Maryland lacks a statewide STR license, but the 2024 HB 1312 established a Short-Term Rental Commission — verified via Maryland People's Law Library. **Conflict resolution: pre-2024 reading is correct (no statewide license), but 2024 HB 1312 framework is taking effect; we coded Maryland as RESTRICTED given active local frameworks.**
- **Connecticut** — Avalara (Sep 2024) and CT Examiner (Mar 2025) both confirm Public Act 24-38 took effect Oct 2024; conflict-free.
- **Ohio** — Wikipedia & Minut describe Columbus/Cleveland rules; Rent Responsibly notes 2025 SB 104 / HB 109 may impose new caps. **Statewide statute not yet in force as of 2026-06-18** — coded as UNCERTAIN.
