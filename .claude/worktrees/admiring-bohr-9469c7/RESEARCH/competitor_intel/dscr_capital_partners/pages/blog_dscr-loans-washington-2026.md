## What Is a DSCR Loan?

A DSCR loan — Debt Service Coverage Ratio loan — qualifies the borrower based on the rental income of the subject property rather than personal income. The qualifying ratio is the property's gross monthly rent divided by its monthly debt obligations (PITIA: principal, interest, taxes, insurance, HOA).

### DSCR Formula

DSCR = Gross Monthly Rental Income ÷ Monthly Debt Obligations

A 1.0 DSCR means the property breaks even. Higher ratios produce better pricing.

Because qualification is based on the property, DSCR loans require no W-2s, no tax returns, no employment verification, and no debt-to-income calculation.

## Why Washington Is a Strong Market for DSCR Loans

Washington's investor activity is concentrated in two distinct geographies. The **Puget Sound region** — Seattle, Bellevue, Redmond, Tacoma — has high entry prices but strong tenant credit driven by tech employment (Microsoft, Amazon, Boeing). **Spokane and Eastern WA** deliver the cash-flow profile, with SFR entry prices often half of Seattle metro.

Washington enacted statewide tenant protections in recent legislative sessions, including notice-period extensions and a small-property rent-cap framework that varies by jurisdiction. This affects cash-flow modeling but does not change DSCR qualification — we use the appraiser-determined market rent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Are DSCR loans available in Seattle given the high prices? +

Yes. Seattle DSCR loans typically require larger down payments to clear the 1.0 DSCR threshold. Multi-family (2–4 unit) is the most common Seattle DSCR product because the rent stack supports the qualifying ratio more easily than SFR.

Is Spokane a good DSCR market? +

Yes. Spokane is one of the strongest cash-flow DSCR markets in the Pacific Northwest. SFR DSCRs in the 1.10–1.30 range at 75% LTV are routine.

Can I get a DSCR loan for a Seattle STR? +

Yes — provided the property is legally permitted under Seattle's STR licensing regime. Seattle STR licensing is restrictive; we finance permitted STR properties using AirDNA full-year projections.

How do Washington tenant protections affect DSCR underwriting? +

Washington's tenant protections affect cash-flow modeling and operations, not DSCR qualification. We use the appraiser-determined market rent or in-place lease for the qualifying DSCR ratio.

Can I close a Washington DSCR loan in an LLC? +

Yes. WA DSCR loans close in LLCs. Washington has no state income tax — a structural cash-flow benefit relative to neighboring states.
