## Approach & Philosophy

Most DSCR borrowers are surprised at how much pricing varies across wholesale lenders — sometimes by a full percent on the same file. The wholesale-broker model exists for exactly this reason: one file, multiple investor quotes, route to whichever pricing wins. Arin built DSCR Capital Partners around this thesis.

The other side is service. Investment property borrowers tend to be experienced, busy, and short on patience for call-center handoffs. Every file at DSCR Capital Partners works through a single loan officer end-to-end — direct cell phone, direct email, direct accountability.

Equal Housing Lender. Loans for business purposes only (investment property — not subject to TILA/RESPA consumer mortgage disclosures).
