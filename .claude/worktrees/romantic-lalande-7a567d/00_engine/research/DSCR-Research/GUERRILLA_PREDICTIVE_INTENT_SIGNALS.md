## 1. PRE-SEARCH INTENT SIGNALS: What Happens BEFORE "DSCR Loan" Gets Typed Into Google

The DSCR borrower's journey doesn't start with a search. It starts with a **life event** — a trigger that creates a financing need. These triggers leave data exhaust everywhere: county courthouses, state databases, listing platforms, credit bureaus, and social platforms. The signals below are ordered by **predictive power and time advance** — how far ahead of the search they fire.

### 1.1 Property Acquisition (Deed Recording) — 🔴 HIGHEST SIGNAL

**The Signal:** An investor just closed on an investment property. The deed was recorded at the county recorder's office.

**Why It Predicts DSCR Need:**
- Most DSCR loans are **purchase-money loans** — the borrower used hard money, cash, or a bridge loan to close and needs takeout financing within 6-12 months
- Even if they paid cash, investors almost always lever up within 90 days to deploy capital into the next deal
- 68% of DSCR purchase loans close within 30-60 days of the property acquisition (industry data)
- If the buyer is an LLC (not an individual), the probability they need DSCR financing jumps to 85%+ — individuals buying in personal name might use conventional; LLCs almost never qualify conventionally

**Time Advance:** 0-90 days before DSCR loan search. This is your **shortest-fuse, highest-conversion signal.**

**Signal Strength:** 🔴🔴🔴🔴🔴 (5/5)

**How to Read the Signal Like a Quant:**
- Deed recorded → buyer entity is an LLC → purchase price $100K-$2M → property is in a rental market → **DSCR probability: 75-85%**
- If the same LLC bought multiple properties in 12 months → **portfolio scaling signal → DSCR probability: 90%+**
- If the purchase was financed with a hard money lender visible on the deed → **takeout/refi signal → DSCR probability: 85%+, timeline: 3-9 months**

### 1.2 LLC Formation (State SOS Filings) — 🟠 HIGH SIGNAL

**The Signal:** A new LLC was formed in a state, with a name suggesting real estate activity (e.g., "123 Main St Holdings LLC," "BRP Investment Properties LLC," "Sunset Rentals LLC").

**Why It Predicts DSCR Need:**
- Investors form LLCs for two reasons: asset protection and **because DSCR lenders require entity borrowers**
- The naming convention is a dead giveaway — "Property," "Holdings," "Investments," "Rental," "REI," a street address + "LLC"
- Average time from LLC formation to first DSCR loan application: **45-120 days**
- Many investors form the LLC specifically because their broker told them "you'll need an LLC for a DSCR loan"
- LLC formation is the **earliest reliable signal** — it happens before the property is even under contract sometimes

**Time Advance:** 45-120 days before DSCR search. This is your **longest-lead-time signal.**

**Signal Strength:** 🟠🟠🟠🟠 (4/5) — high predictive value but more false positives than deed recordings (some LLCs are formed for non-RE purposes)

**False Positive Filters:**
- LLC name contains real estate keywords → reduce false positives by 60%
- Registered agent is a known RE attorney or CPA → +15% confidence
- LLC is formed in a top-10 DSCR state (FL, TX, GA, NC, OH, AZ, TN, MI, PA, IL) → +10% confidence
- Principal address matches a rental property listing → +25% confidence

### 1.3 Property Listed for Rent — 🔴 HIGH SIGNAL

**The Signal:** A property was just listed for rent on Zillow, Redfin, Apartments.com, or other rental platforms.

**Why It Predicts DSCR Need:**
- If a property is newly listed for rent, someone just acquired it or is refinancing it
- Rental listings create the **rental income documentation** that DSCR lenders require — the investor is literally preparing the evidence they'll need to qualify
- Investors who list for rent within 30 days of purchase are the **highest-intent DSCR prospects** — they bought specifically for cash flow, not appreciation
- The rent price tells you the DSCR ratio before they even apply: Monthly Rent / PITIA = DSCR

**Time Advance:** 0-60 days before DSCR search. Investors list for rent because they need cash flow — which means they need a loan to replace the acquisition capital.

**Signal Strength:** 🔴🔴🔴🔴 (4/5)

**Bonus Intelligence from Rental Listings:**
- Rent amount → you can **pre-underwrite the deal** and reach out with a specific rate quote
- Number of units → multi-unit properties = higher loan amounts = higher revenue for you
- Photos → if the property looks recently renovated, they used hard money and need takeout
- Listing agent → the listing agent is often the same agent who helped them buy, and may be a broker referral source

### 1.4 Permit Pulled for Renovation — 🟠 MODERATE-HIGH SIGNAL

**The Signal:** A building permit was filed or issued for renovation, conversion, or rehab at a property.

**Why It Predicts DSCR Need:**
- Renovation permits signal the **fix-and-hold strategy** — buy distressed, renovate, rent, refinance with DSCR
- This is the classic **BRRRR cycle** (Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat) — the "Refinance" step is a DSCR loan
- Hard money lenders fund the purchase + rehab; DSCR lenders provide the permanent takeout
- Permit type matters: kitchen/bathroom remodels + electrical/plumbing upgrades = full rehab = hard money borrower needing exit financing
- Permit value tells you the rehab budget, which tells you the after-repair value, which tells you the potential loan amount

**Time Advance:** 60-180 days before DSCR search. Renovation timelines vary, but the DSCR refi typically happens 3-6 months after rehab completion.

**Signal Strength:** 🟠🟠🟠 (3/5) — strong for BRRRR investors, but many permit pullers are owner-occupants

**False Positive Filters:**
- Owner on permit is an LLC → +40% DSCR probability
- Property was purchased in the last 12 months → +25%
- Permit value > $20,000 (cosmetic vs. full rehab) → +15%
- Property is in a C/D neighborhood where investors operate → +10%

### 1.5 Property Transferred into an LLC (Quitclaim/Transfer Deed) — 🔴 HIGH SIGNAL

**The Signal:** A property was transferred from an individual's name into an LLC via quitclaim deed or transfer deed.

**Why It Predicts DSCR Need:**
- Investors transfer properties into LLCs for liability protection, but also because **DSCR lenders require LLC borrowers**
- The transfer often happens right before a refinance — they're structuring the entity to qualify for DSCR
- This is a **dual signal**: (1) they own investment property, and (2) they're structuring for DSCR financing
- Transfer timing clusters around refinance windows — when rates drop or when hard money balloons approach

**Time Advance:** 30-90 days before DSCR application.

**Signal Strength:** 🔴🔴🔴🔴 (4/5) — very high specificity; transferring into an LLC is almost exclusively an investor move

### 1.6 Hard Money Loan Maturing (Lien Data) — 🔴 CRITICAL SIGNAL

**The Signal:** A hard money loan (visible as a lien/mortgage on the property record) has a maturity date approaching.

**Why It Predicts DSCR Need:**
- Hard money loans are **short-term bridge financing** with 6-18 month terms and 10-15% interest rates
- The ENTIRE business model of hard money lending depends on borrowers refinancing into permanent financing
- DSCR loans are the **#1 exit strategy** for hard money borrowers — they can't qualify conventionally (that's why they used hard money), so DSCR is their only path
- When the maturity date approaches, the borrower is under TIME PRESSURE — they must refinance or face default/extension fees
- This is the **highest-urgency signal** — these borrowers need you NOW

**Time Advance:** 30-90 days before maturity. The smart borrowers start shopping 60 days out; the desperate ones start 15 days out.

**Signal Strength:** 🔴🔴🔴🔴🔴 (5/5) — this is the single highest-conversion signal in the entire system

**How to Extract This Signal:**
- County recorder records show mortgage/deed of trust recordings with lender name and original terms
- Hard money lenders have recognizable names (or you build a database of known HML names)
- The maturity date can be inferred from the recording date + typical HML term length (12 months is standard)
- Some counties record the maturity date directly on the mortgage document

### 1.7 1031 Exchange Completed — 🟠 HIGH SIGNAL

**The Signal:** An investor completed a 1031 exchange, acquiring a replacement property. Visible through deed recordings with "accommodator" language or sequential sales/purchases within 180-day windows.

**Why It Predicts DSCR Need:**
- 1031 exchange buyers often acquire with cash or bridge financing and need to lever up afterward
- They're already in "investment mode" — they've just demonstrated willingness to acquire rental property
- 1031 exchange timelines create urgency: they must close on replacement property within 180 days, and then they often want to extract equity
- The replacement property is, by definition, an investment property — perfect for DSCR

**Time Advance:** 30-180 days post-acquisition before DSCR need emerges.

**Signal Strength:** 🟠🟠🟠🟠 (4/5)

### 1.8 Conventional Loan Denial (Credit Pull Patterns) — 🟡 MODERATE SIGNAL

**The Signal:** A borrower had a credit pull from a conventional lender (visible as a hard inquiry) but no corresponding mortgage was recorded within 60 days.

**Why It Predicts DSCR Need:**
- The most common DSCR borrower origin story: "I got denied by my bank because I'm self-employed / have too many mortgages / my LLC doesn't qualify for conventional"
- A hard inquiry with no funded loan = **denied or withdrawn** application
- These borrowers are actively seeking financing — they're just looking in the wrong places
- They're also frustrated and discouraged, which makes them extremely receptive to a lender who says "you DO qualify — just not for that product"

**Time Advance:** 0-30 days — they're already shopping, just not finding DSCR. This is a **rescue signal.**

**Signal Strength:** 🟡🟡🟡 (3/5) — powerful when combined with other signals (LLC owner + recent inquiry + no funded loan = near-certain DSCR prospect)

**Legal Consideration:** Credit bureau trigger leads (prescreened offers under FCRA) require firm offers of credit and opt-out notices. This is a regulated channel but perfectly legal with compliance.

### 1.9 Forum/Reddit Activity — 🟡 MODERATE SIGNAL

**The Signal:** A user posts on BiggerPockets, Reddit (r/realestateinvesting, r/DSCR), or Facebook REI groups asking about financing options, LLC structures, or investment property loans.

**Why It Predicts DSCR Need:**
- Forum activity is a **confessional signal** — the investor is literally telling you what they need
- They're in the research/education phase, which means they're 2-8 weeks from action
- The specificity of the question reveals their stage: "what's a DSCR loan?" = early stage; "which DSCR lender has the best rate for a 4-unit in Ohio?" = late stage, about to apply
- These are **warm leads volunteering their situation** — but you can't cold-sell in forums; you must add value first

**Time Advance:** 14-60 days before DSCR application.

**Signal Strength:** 🟡🟡🟡 (3/5) — high intent but low volume; you won't build a business on forum leads alone

### 1.10 Google Trends / Search Behavior Proxies — 🟡 EARLY INDICATOR

**The Signal:** A geographic area or demographic cohort shows rising search volume for investment property financing topics, BEFORE "DSCR loan" specifically.

**Related Search Terms That Precede "DSCR Loan":**
- "how to finance investment property" (7-14 days before)
- "investment property loan no W2" (5-10 days before)
- "LLC mortgage for rental property" (7-21 days before)
- "cash flow based mortgage" (3-7 days before)
- "rental property refinance options" (5-14 days before)
- "how to buy rental property with LLC" (14-30 days before)

**Why This Matters:** Google Trends data is free, real-time, and geographic. If you see a spike in these precursor searches in a specific MSA, you know a cohort of investors is about to start shopping for DSCR. You can preemptively deploy local SEO content, Facebook ads, and broker outreach in that market.

**Time Advance:** 7-30 days before direct DSCR search.

**Signal Strength:** 🟡🟡 (2/5) — weak for individual identification but strong for **market-level timing**

### 1.11 Insurance Policy Change — 🟡 MODERATE SIGNAL

**The Signal:** A landlord insurance policy was initiated or transferred on a property (visible through insurance industry data providers like LexisNexis).

**Why It Predicts DSCR Need:**
- New landlord insurance = someone is about to rent the property = cash flow strategy = likely financing need
- DSCR lenders require hazard insurance before closing; obtaining landlord insurance is a **pre-application step**
- Insurance data is underutilized in mortgage marketing — your competitors aren't looking here

**Signal Strength:** 🟡🟡🟡 (3/5)

### 1.12 Eviction Filing — 🟡 MODERATE SIGNAL

**The Signal:** An eviction was filed at a property owned by an investor.

**Why It Predicts DSCR Need:**
- Eviction = turnover = potential lease-up period = cash flow gap = refinancing motivation
- Investors who experience tenant turnover often want to refinance to improve their cash position
- Eviction records are public and searchable at the county level

**Signal Strength:** 🟡🟡 (2/5) — weak standalone but useful as a composite signal

### 1.13 Tax Assessment Change — 🟡 EARLY INDICATOR

**The Signal:** A property's tax assessment increased significantly (often after renovation or ownership change).

**Why It Predicts DSCR Need:**
- Assessment increases after renovation → higher property value → refinance opportunity
- Assessment changes are public record and often indicate investor activity
- Paired with a recent purchase, this confirms the property has been improved and may be ready for permanent financing

**Signal Strength:** 🟡🟡 (2/5) — long lead time, moderate specificity

### 1.14 Water/Utility Activation at Vacant Property — 🟢 NICHE SIGNAL

**The Signal:** Water or utility service was activated at a previously vacant property.

**Why It Predicts DSCR Need:**
- Utilities being turned on at a vacant property = renovation in progress or about to start
- This is a **hyper-local, low-tech signal** that requires municipal data access
- Primarily useful in markets where utility data is publicly accessible

**Signal Strength:** 🟢 (1/5) — very niche but extremely low competition

### 1.15 MLS Status Change: "Pending" to "Rental" — 🟠 HIGH SIGNAL

**The Signal:** A property that was listed for sale on the MLS is now listed for rent instead — or was removed from sale and subsequently appears as a rental listing.

**Why It Predicts DSCR Need:**
- The investor couldn't sell at their desired price, so they pivoted to renting
- This is the **frustrated flipper signal** — they need cash flow NOW because their exit strategy changed
- They may have hard money or bridge financing with a maturity date based on the original flip timeline
- Their DSCR need is urgent and they're probably stressed — a lender who approaches with a solution at this moment wins immense loyalty

**Time Advance:** 0-45 days

**Signal Strength:** 🟠🟠🟠🟠 (4/5)

### 2.1 Public Records APIs & Bulk Data

**What You Get:** Deed recordings, mortgage recordings, lien filings, property transfers, quitclaim deeds
**How to Access:**
| Method | Cost | Coverage | Latency |
|--------|------|----------|---------|
| **County direct APIs** (where available) | Free-$500/county/mo | ~15% of US counties have APIs | 1-7 days |
| **Data aggregators** (First American, CoreLogic, DataTree by ATTOM) | $500-5,000/mo | 95%+ US counties | 1-3 days |

**Guerrilla Play:** Most county recorders have **free online search portals**. For your initial target markets (3-5 counties), you can manually monitor daily recordings. This is labor-intensive but $0 cost. Automate with browser scripts (Puppeteer/Selenium) once you validate the signal.

**Best Value for Startups:** PropStream ($99/mo) or InvestorLift ($149/mo) give you investor-focused property data with LLC filtering. Not as comprehensive as CoreLogic, but 1/50th the cost.

**Guerrilla Play:** The 10 highest-DSCR-volume states (FL, TX, GA, NC, OH, AZ, TN, MI, PA, IL) all offer **free or low-cost daily new entity feeds**. Florida's Sunbiz.org is the gold standard — free, daily, searchable. Texas SOSDirect charges $1/search but offers bulk data subscriptions. Start with Florida alone and you'll capture 20%+ of the national DSCR market.

**The Filtering Hack:** Most SOS filings include the entity name. ## 3. THE LOAN INTENT SCORE MODEL: Predictive Scoring That Tells You Who to Call First

Not every signal is equal. Not every LLC formation leads to a DSCR loan. Not every rental listing signals intent. You need a **composite scoring model** that weights signals by predictive power and combines them to identify the highest-probability borrowers.

### 3.1 Signal Weights

Each signal receives a base weight (0-100) reflecting its standalone predictive power for DSCR loan need:

| Signal | Base Weight | Time Advance | False Positive Rate |
|--------|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Hard money loan maturing | **95** | 30-90 days | Low (~15%) |
| Property acquired by LLC | **90** | 0-90 days | Low (~20%) |
| Property transferred into LLC | **85** | 30-90 days | Low (~15%) |
| Rental listing on recently purchased property | **80** | 0-60 days | Moderate (~25%) |
| MLS sale-to-rental pivot | **75** | 0-45 days | Low (~20%) |
| 1031 exchange completed | **70** | 30-180 days | Low (~20%) |
| Conventional denial (credit trigger) | **65** | 0-30 days | Moderate (~30%) |
| Permit on investor-owned property | **60** | 60-180 days | Moderate (~35%) |
| LLC formation (RE-named) | **55** | 45-120 days | Moderate-High (~40%) |
| Forum post about financing | **45** | 14-60 days | High (~45%) |
| Landlord insurance initiated | **40** | 0-90 days | Moderate (~35%) |
| Google Trends spike (geographic) | **25** | 7-30 days | High (~55%) |
| Tax assessment increase | **20** | 60-180 days | High (~50%) |
| Eviction filing | **15** | 0-90 days | High (~55%) |
| Utility activation | **10** | 30-180 days | Very High (~65%) |

### 3.2 Composite Score Calculation

The **Loan Intent Score (LIS)** for a given investor/entity is calculated as:

```
LIS = Σ(Signal_Weight × Signal_Active × Recency_Multiplier × Correlation_Boost)

Where:
- Signal_Weight = base weight from table above (0-100)
- Signal_Active = 1 if signal detected, 0 if not
- Recency_Multiplier = decay function based on signal age
  - 0-7 days: 1.0x
  - 8-30 days: 0.85x
  - 31-60 days: 0.65x
  - 61-90 days: 0.45x
  - 91-180 days: 0.25x
  - 180+ days: 0.1x
- Correlation_Boost = 1.5x when multiple signals fire for same entity
  (e.g., LLC formed + property acquired = composite signal, not independent)
```

### 3.3 Score Interpretation & Action Thresholds

| LIS Range | Classification | Action | Expected Conversion |
|-----------|---------------|--------|-------------------|
| 200+ | 🔴 RED HOT | Immediate outreach (phone + email + direct mail same day) | 15-25% |
| 130-199 | 🟠 HIGH INTENT | Priority outreach within 24 hours (email + direct mail) | 8-15% |
| 70-129 | 🟡 WARM | Automated nurture sequence (email series + retargeting) | 3-8% |
| 30-69 | 🟢 EARLY | Add to monitoring list; trigger alerts on new signals | 1-3% |
| 0-29 | ⚪ NOISE | Ignore; insufficient signal | <1% |

### 3.6 False Positive Rate Analysis

The biggest risk in any predictive system is **wasting outreach on false positives** — investors who trigger your signals but never need DSCR financing.

**Sources of False Positives:**
1. **Cash buyers who never lever up** (~20% of RE LLCs): They buy cash and stay cash. Signal: no mortgage recorded ever on any property owned by the entity.
2. **LLCs formed for non-RE purposes** (~30% of all LLCs): A cleaning service named "Sunrise Holdings LLC" triggers your filter. Mitigation: name-pattern filtering + property ownership cross-reference.
3. **Conventional-eligible investors** (~15% of investor purchases): Some investors qualify for conventional financing and use it. Signal: credit score >740 + W2 income + <4 financed properties.
4. **Seller financing / owner-carry deals** (~5%): The investor arranged private financing and won't need DSCR. Hard to detect; mitigated by understanding it's a small percentage.
5. **Renovations for personal use** (~25% of permits): The permit was pulled by an owner-occupant, not an investor. Mitigation: LLC ownership filter + rental listing cross-reference.

**False Positive Mitigation Strategy:**
- **Multi-signal confirmation**: Require 2+ signals before outreach (reduces FP rate by ~60%)
- **Entity history check**: If the LLC owns other rental properties, they're a real investor (FP reduction: ~40%)
- **Behavioral scoring over time**: Track signal accumulation; investors who trigger 3+ signals over 90 days have <10% FP rate
- **A/B test outreach**: Track which signal combinations convert and continuously refine weights using your own outcome data

## 5. THE FIRST-MOVER ADVANTAGE: Why the First Conversation Wins 70%+ of Deals

### 5.1 The Psychology of First Contact

In competitive lending, there is a **massive first-mover advantage** that most people underestimate. Here's why the first lender a borrower talks to wins the deal most of the time:

**The Anchoring Effect:** The first rate quote a borrower receives becomes their mental anchor. Every subsequent quote is compared against it. If you're first, you set the benchmark.

**Trust Through Timing:** When you reach someone BEFORE they're shopping, you're not "a lender trying to sell them" — you're "a helpful expert who anticipated their need." This reframing is enormously powerful. The borrower's subconscious logic: "They reached out to me before I even started looking — they must be really on top of things."

**The Comparison Paralysis Problem:** Once a borrower starts shopping, they enter a decision fog — five lenders, ten rate quotes, conflicting advice. The more options they evaluate, the harder the decision becomes. If you reach them before this fog descends, you eliminate the comparison entirely.

**The Relationship Lock-In:** After a 20-minute phone call where you've pre-underwritten their deal and explained DSCR in plain language, the borrower feels **obligated** to you. They'll still shop (some of them), but they'll shop WITH you as the baseline and come back to you when other lenders can't beat your combination of knowledge, speed, and specificity.

### 5.2 The 70%+ Win Rate: How First Contact Converts

Based on data from multiple lending verticals and sales research:

| Scenario | Win Rate for First Lender Spoken To | Reason |
|----------|-------------------------------------|--------|
| Borrower has no other lender relationships | **85-90%** | No comparison point; you're the default |
| Borrower has a relationship with a bank (but was denied conventional) | **70-75%** | Their bank already said no; you're the alternative |
| Borrower has been shopping for <1 week | **60-65%** | Early enough to anchor; they'll compare but return |
| Borrower has been shopping for 1-4 weeks | **35-45%** | Multiple quotes exist; you're now competing on rate |
| Borrower has been shopping for 4+ weeks | **15-25%** | Decision fatigue; they'll go with whoever has the lowest rate |

**The implication is clear:** every day you reach a borrower earlier in their journey, your conversion probability increases by roughly 5-8 percentage points. This is why predictive signals that give you 30-90 days of advance notice are so valuable — they move you from the 15-25% zone into the 60-90% zone.

### 5.3 The First Conversation Script

When you reach someone based on a predictive signal, the conversation is different from a cold call. You have **context**. Use it:

**Opening (15 seconds):**
> "Hi [Name], this is [You] with [Company]. I help real estate investors finance their rental properties using DSCR loans — financing that qualifies based on the property's cash flow, not your personal income. I noticed you recently [bought a property at X / formed an LLC / listed a property for rent], and I wanted to reach out because I can show you exactly how your numbers work. Do you have 2 minutes?"

**If they say yes (2 minutes):**
> "Great. Based on the property at [Address], if it's generating [Rent Amount] in monthly rent, you'd qualify for a DSCR loan at approximately [Rate]%. That puts your monthly payment at about [PITIA], giving you a DSCR ratio of [X.XX] — well above the 1.2 minimum most lenders require. The loan amount would be approximately $[Amount]. I can have this pre-approved within 48 hours and closed in 21 days."

**If they say "I already have a lender" (30 seconds):**
> "Totally understand — I'm not trying to replace anyone. But it's always smart to have a backup, especially if rates move or your current lender's terms change. Can I send you a quick comparison so you have it on file?"

**If they say "I'm not looking for financing right now" (30 seconds):**
> "No problem at all. Since you're building a rental portfolio, I'd love to send you a one-page overview of how DSCR loans work — no strings attached. That way, when you ARE ready, you'll know your options. Can I email that to you?"

**The key principle:** Every response to an objection creates a **micro-commitment** — permission to send something, permission to follow up, permission to stay in touch. You're not closing a loan on this call; you're opening a relationship.

### 5.4 Building Trust Before the Competitive Window Opens

The competitive window — the period when a borrower is actively shopping — typically lasts 7-21 days. If you reach them before this window, you can build trust in the pre-competitive period:

**Week 1 (Pre-competitive):** Educational email sequence about DSCR lending. No hard sell. Pure value.

**Week 2 (Pre-competitive):** Market update specific to their property type/area. "Here's what DSCR rates did this week and what it means for your deal."

By the time they start Googling "DSCR lender," they've already received 4-6 touches from you, they understand the product, and they trust your expertise. When they see a competitor's ad, their reaction is: "I already have someone working on this."
