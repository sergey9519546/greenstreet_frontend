Quick facts about DSCR Capital Partners (UTM Financial, LLC)

DSCR Capital Partners is a DSCR lender operated by UTM Financial, LLC. NMLS #2591548. Their public positioning focuses on a broad DSCR rate sheet (5.75% from) and a long-tail blog content strategy covering all 50 states and most major metros.

Best for: Standard DSCR files where the borrower found them via search and is looking for a one-stop-shop marketing experience.

Not for: Files that need specialty programs (condotel, FN, sub-1.0) with deep underwriting. Tight-turn files. Files where broker wants competitive pricing across multiple lenders.

Where they win

DSCR Capital Partners strengths

• Marketing breadth. DSCR Capital Partners has built a content footprint covering every state and most major metros. Easy for borrowers to find them via search.

• Standard rate sheet. Their published 'from 5.75%' rate is competitive with the broader market.

• Geographic coverage. They claim coverage in all 50 states.

• Standard program breadth. DSCR, STR, Foreign National, ITIN, LLC, Cash-Out, BRRRR, Portfolio, Condotel — broad coverage at the marketing level.

Weaknesses

Where DSCR Capital Partners struggles

• Real-time pricing. DSCR Capital Partners' published rates are marketing positions. Actual locked pricing depends on the file; brokers report frequent repricing.

• Turn time. Longer than dedicated DSCR shops. Volume + content-driven pipeline can stretch close timelines.

• Specialty depth. Despite the broad marketing, specialty programs (condotel, FN, sub-1.0) are less flexible than dedicated specialists like Visio.

• Single-channel risk. Heavy reliance on SEO content means customer acquisition is concentrated. Service experience can vary with channel.
