## THE CORE THESIS

The real money in DSCR lending isn't the first loan — it's loans 2, 3, 4, 5... Real estate investors are serial borrowers. The BRRRR strategy (Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat) means they need a new DSCR loan every time they acquire a property. If you keep them, one borrower can generate $50K–$200K+ in lifetime revenue.

Yet most DSCR lenders lose **60–75% of borrowers after the first loan**. They lose them to rate shopping on refinance. They lose them to competitors with slightly better pricing. They lose them because they never built a relationship infrastructure that makes leaving painful.

This playbook fixes that. Not with concepts — with **exact scripts, exact timing, and exact economics**. Every section is executable today.

### The "Portfolio Borrower" Economics (5+ Loans)

A borrower with 5+ loans is fundamentally different from a one-time borrower:

- **Revenue concentration**: 5% of borrowers generate 40%+ of total revenue
- **Referral engine**: A 5-loan borrower refers 3–5 new borrowers; a 1-loan borrower refers 0.2
- **Servicing stability**: Portfolio borrowers rarely default (portfolio DSCR provides a cushion)
- **Cross-sell attach rate**: 40–60% vs. 5–10% for single-loan borrowers
- **Price insensitivity**: They'll accept 12.5–25 bps higher rates because the relationship value exceeds the rate savings

**Retention spend returns 65–640x what acquisition spend returns per dollar.** This isn't close. Every dollar diverted from acquisition to retention for existing portfolio borrowers generates dramatically more revenue.

## 3. THE "REFINANCE ALERT" SYSTEM — Your #1 Retention & Repeat-Loan Tool

### Why This Is the Most Powerful Retention Mechanism

- DSCR borrowers refinance at **2–3× the rate of conventional borrowers** due to prepay step-downs, DSCR tier improvements, and the BRRRR cycle
- When a borrower refinances, they make an **active choice** about which lender to use — this is the highest-risk moment for defection
- Most borrowers start shopping 60–90 days before they plan to refinance, giving the incumbent lender a **first-mover advantage** if they initiate the conversation
- **If you don't alert them, a competitor will.**

### How It Works

**Daily Rate Monitoring:**
1. Every business day, pull current DSCR rate sheets for your product lineup
2. Compare against every borrower's current rate (stored in your servicing system)
3. Calculate: Current Rate − Market Rate = Rate Gap
4. If Rate Gap ≥ 50 bps **OR** borrower's prepay penalty just stepped down, trigger an alert

**Alert Tiers:**

| Rate Gap | Alert Type | Urgency |
|---|---|---|
| 25–49 bps | Soft alert — add to next monthly email | Low |
| 50–74 bps | Standard alert — dedicated email | Medium |
| 75–99 bps | Priority alert — email + text | High |
| 100+ bps | Urgent alert — email + text + phone call | Critical |

**Savings Threshold:** Only alert when monthly savings exceed **$100/month**. Below that, the break-even is too long and the alert feels like spam.

### The "Pre-Underwritten Refinance Offer"

The killer feature: **they don't need to apply. You already know they qualify.**

You have their:
- Entity documents (already on file)
- Credit profile (soft pull update if <90 days old)
- Property data (address, appraisal, rent comp)
- DSCR (you've been monitoring it)
- Payment history (you're the servicer)

**The Offer Template:**

> "Your property at [address] could save you $[X]/month with a refinance. Here's your new rate: [Y]%. Want to lock it?"

That's it. One sentence. The borrower says yes or no. No application, no document gathering, no re-underwriting from scratch.

**This alone can generate 20–30% more loans from existing borrowers.**

### Break-Even Analysis Requirements

Every refinance alert MUST include:

| Element | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Current rate vs. new rate | The headline savings |
| Monthly payment reduction | The tangible benefit |
| Closing costs | The upfront investment |
| Prepay penalty (if any) | The hidden cost |
| **Break-even timeline** | The decision metric: "You'll recoup costs in [X] months" |
| **5-year net savings** | The long-term picture |
| Cash-out option (if equity available) | The BRRRR accelerant |

### Revenue Impact

If proactive refinance alerts capture even **15% of refinance volume** that would otherwise go to competitors, the incremental revenue per 1,000-borrower portfolio is approximately **$1.8M–$3.2M annually** (assuming 40% refinance rate, $250K avg loan, $5K per-loan revenue).

## 4. THE PORTFOLIO DASHBOARD — The Tool That Creates Switching Costs

### Why This Makes Leaving Painful

The portfolio dashboard creates **three layers of switching costs:**

**Layer 1: Data Lock-In**
- The platform holds: loan terms, entity structures, property details, rent rolls, expense history, insurance policies, tax assessments, appraisal data, and cash flow projections for every property
- Rebuilding this data at a competitor: 2–4 hours for a 5-property portfolio, 8–15 hours for a 15-property portfolio
- The mere anticipation of data migration creates inertia

**Layer 2: Algorithmic Lock-In**
- Your optimization algorithms are trained on the borrower's specific portfolio data
- Refinance timing recommendations, DSCR tier projections, and equity access calculations improve with data depth
- A new platform starts with zero portfolio history — generic vs. personalized recommendations

**Layer 3: Advisory Lock-In**
- Your relationship manager understands the borrower's strategy, risk tolerance, and growth trajectory
- A new lender needs 6–12 months to develop equivalent institutional knowledge
- Once a borrower trusts the platform as their "portfolio CFO," switching becomes unthinkable

### Core Dashboard Features

| Feature | What It Shows | Retention Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Portfolio-level DSCR** | Aggregate DSCR across all properties | Enables portfolio pricing; creates single health metric |
| **Individual property DSCR** | Per-property DSCR with trend lines | Identifies weak properties before they become problems |
| **Equity tracking** | Current AVM vs. loan balance per property | Drives cash-out refinance conversations |
| **Cash flow waterfall** | Rent → expenses → debt service → net CF per property | Quantifies portfolio performance; keeps borrower engaged |
| **Refinance opportunity alerts** | Rate gap + break-even + one-click action | The #1 repeat-loan driver |
| **Prepay penalty countdown** | Current penalty, next step-down date | Anticipates refinance timing |
| **"Next deal" readiness score** | Available equity + DSCR capacity + pre-qual status | Converts browsing into applications |
| **Portfolio health score** | Single metric (green/yellow/red) across all properties | Creates emotional attachment to "their" dashboard |

### The "Next Move" Recommendation Engine

The dashboard should proactively recommend the borrower's optimal next action:

- **"Refinance [Property A]"** — Rates have dropped 75 bps since your close. Savings: $X/month.
- **"Cash-out [Property B]"** — You have $45K in accessible equity. Use it for your next down payment.
- **"Acquire [Property C]"** — Your portfolio DSCR supports another loan. Pre-qualified for up to $X.
- **"Hold all"** — Your portfolio is optimized. No action needed right now. We'll alert you when something changes.

**This is the difference between "We'd love to do your next loan" (ignorable) and "We've identified $23,400 in potential savings across your portfolio" (actionable and trust-building).**

| Entity documents | Upload required | On file |
| Credit pull | New hard pull | Soft pull update (if <90 days) |
| Property details | Manual entry | Pre-filled from dashboard |
| Rent comp analysis | New analysis | Auto-updated from monitoring |
| Application-to-lock timeline | 5–10 business days | **1–3 business days** |

**The "One-Click Refinance":** For a borrower refinancing a loan you already service, the application should be literally one click — review pre-filled terms, confirm, and rate lock initiates. No document uploads. No re-verification. This creates procedural switching costs that rate discounts alone cannot.

### Portfolio-Level Pricing Model

Instead of pricing each property individually, evaluate the borrower's entire portfolio as a single risk unit:
- Properties with strong DSCR (1.30+) cross-subsidize weaker DSCR properties (1.0–1.10)
- The blended portfolio DSCR determines pricing, not the individual property DSCR
- **Switching penalty:** Once a borrower has 3+ properties at portfolio-level pricing, moving to a competitor means every property reverts to individual-asset pricing — potentially 50–100 bps worse on the next acquisition

## 6. THE REFERRAL ENGINE — Turning Borrowers into Your Sales Force

### The "Refer a Fellow Investor" Program

**Double-Sided Incentive Structure:**

| Referee Loan Size | Referrer Gets | Referee Gets | Total Cost | ROI at 1.5pt Origination |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $100K–$200K | $250 cash | $500 closing credit | $750 | 2.0–4.0× |
| $200K–$350K | $500 cash | $750 closing credit | $1,250 | 2.4–4.2× |
| $350K–$500K | $750 cash | $1,000 closing credit | $1,750 | 3.0–4.3× |
| $500K+ | $1,000 cash | $1,500 closing credit | $2,500 | 3.0–6.0× |

**Why double-sided works:** The referrer doesn't feel like they're "selling out" their friend because the friend also gets something. The referee doesn't feel used because they're getting an exclusive benefit. Double-sided incentives convert **2–3× better** than single-sided.

### When to Ask for Referrals — The 3 Golden Moments

**MOMENT 1: After Smooth Closing (The "Wow" Moment)**

*Script (delivered by LO at closing or Day 1 post-close):*

> "Congratulations — your loan is closed and funded! That was [X] days from application to close.
>
> Quick question — do you know anyone else who's looking for investment property financing? If you refer them and they close, you'll both get $500. Just share this link: [one-click referral link]
>
> I also made this digital card you can text to anyone who asks 'How did you finance that deal?': [referral card link]"

**MOMENT 2: After Rate Drop Alert (The "You Saved Me Money" Moment)**

*Script (in the rate alert email):*

> "Rates just dropped to [X]% for DSCR loans. If you've been thinking about refinancing, now might be the time.
>
> Know someone else who might benefit from lower rates? Forward this email or share your link: [one-click referral link] — you'll both get $500 if they close."

**MOMENT 3: After Portfolio Review (The "I Trust You" Moment)**

*Script (at the end of the annual portfolio review call):*

> "Great review — your portfolio is in solid shape. One last thing: if you have any friends who are building their own portfolios, I'd love to help them the same way. Here's your personal referral link — you'll both get $500 when they close: [link]. No pressure, just wanted you to have it."

### The "Bring a Friend to REIA" Strategy

- Offer to sponsor your borrower's REIA attendance when they bring a friend
- Provide branded materials (not cheesy — think premium deal analysis notebooks, not pens)
- Have your LO attend the REIA as a guest of the borrower, not as a salesperson
- **Cost:** $20–$50 per REIA meeting. **Value:** Face-to-face access to 30–100 investors in a trust-rich environment.

### The "Portfolio Showcase"

When a borrower reaches 5+ loans, offer to create a professional portfolio one-pager they can share with potential investing partners. The one-pager:
- Shows their portfolio growth trajectory
- Includes your branding ("Financed through [Company]")
- Positions the borrower as a serious investor (they love this)
- Creates a viral artifact with your brand embedded

## 7. WIN-BACK PLAYBOOK — When a Borrower Uses Another Lender

### How to Know They Went Elsewhere

| Signal | Detection Method | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| **New lien on their property** | Title monitoring / county records alerts | 0–30 days after competitor closes |
| **Payoff request received** | Servicing system alert | 1–2 weeks before payoff |
| **They stop responding** | CRM engagement scoring (3+ unanswered outreach = flag) | 1–3 months before defection |
| **They ask about payoff amounts** | Servicing call tracking | 2–4 weeks before they commit to a competitor |
| **They stop opening emails** | Email analytics (open rate drops to <10%) | 2–6 months before defection |

### The "No Hard Feelings" Call

*Script (call within 48 hours of discovering the competitor lien):*

> "Hey [First Name], it's [LO Name] from [Company]. I saw [property address] is being refinanced — congrats on getting a better rate, that's great for your portfolio.
>
> I'm not calling to give you a hard time. I just want to make sure you got a good deal. Would you mind sharing what rate and terms they gave you? If we can beat it, great — I'll tell you. If not, you should absolutely stay with them.
>
> Either way, I want you to know we're here for your next deal. And your portfolio dashboard is still active — keep using it even if this refinance goes elsewhere. No hard feelings at all."

**Why this works:**
- It's disarming (you're not angry or desperate)
- It demonstrates confidence (you're willing to compare openly)
- It keeps the door open (the dashboard remains available)
- It plants a seed (most borrowers who leave WILL be disappointed by the competitor, and they'll remember this call)

### The "Rate Check" Offer

For any borrower who shows signs of shopping:

> "Send me what the other lender gave you. If we can beat it, great. If not, you should stay with them. I'll give you an honest comparison in 24 hours."

**Why borrowers come back:** The other lender disappointed them. They experienced:
- Bait-and-switch pricing (quoted one rate, got another) — 10–15% of DSCR borrowers report this
- Slow closing (45+ days) — investors miss deals
- Poor communication (unresponsive LO)
- Hidden fees at closing

When they come back, you greet them with: "Welcome back. Your streamlined application is still on file. We can have a new rate lock for you by tomorrow."

## 8. THE ANNUAL PORTFOLIO REVIEW — The Retention Cornerstone

### Why This Is Worth More Than Any Marketing Spend

- It transforms the lender-borrower relationship from **transactional to advisory**
- It positions you as their **"portfolio CFO"** — not just a source of capital
- Borrowers who receive advisory-level service develop **relationship inertia** that competitors cannot displace
- It creates a structured reason to contact every borrower once a year — no awkward "just checking in" calls
- It surfaces refinance, cash-out, and acquisition opportunities you'd otherwise miss

### Exact Agenda (30 Minutes)

| Time | Topic | What to Cover | Key Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–10 min | **Property Performance** | Rent vs. projected, vacancy, expenses, DSCR trend per property | "Is any property underperforming?" |
| 10–15 min | **Equity & Value** | AVM changes, available equity, cash-out potential | "Do you need capital for your next deal?" |
| 15–20 min | **Rate Check** | Current rate vs. market, break-even on refinance, prepay penalty status | "Is there a refinance opportunity?" |
| 20–25 min | **Acquisition Planning** | Markets they're targeting, pre-qualification status, next-deal readiness | "When are you planning your next purchase?" |
| 25–30 min | **Open Forum** | Any concerns, feedback, or questions | "What could we be doing better?" |

### Exact Script for Opening the Review

> "Thanks for making time for this, [First Name]. I want this to be genuinely useful for you, so here's what I've prepared:
>
> First, I'll walk through how each of your properties is performing — DSCR, cash flow, and any changes you should know about.
> Then we'll look at your equity position and whether there's capital you could access for your next deal.
> After that, I'll show you how your rates compare to today's market — and whether refinancing makes sense.
> Finally, I want to hear about your plans for the next year so I can make sure we're ready to move fast when you find your next deal.
>
> Sound good? Let's start with [Property Address]..."

### What to Deliver Post-Call

Within 24 hours, send a PDF summary including:
- Portfolio DSCR scorecard (per property + aggregate)
- Refinance opportunity matrix (if any)
- Cash-out availability analysis
- Pre-qualification letter for next acquisition (proactive)
- "Recommended next actions" — 1–3 specific, actionable items

## 9. PREDICTIVE CHURN SIGNALS — Knowing When a Borrower Is About to Leave

### The Churn Signal Matrix

| Signal | Timing Before Defection | Detection Method | What to Do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stops opening emails** | 2–6 months | Email analytics (open rate <10% for 60+ days) | Switch channel — call or text. "Hey, just wanted to make sure you're getting our updates." |
| **Doesn't respond to calls** | 2–4 months | CRM engagement scoring (3+ unanswered calls) | Try different LO or channel. Send a value-add (market report, deal alert) with no ask. |
| **Asks about payoff amounts** | 2–4 weeks | Servicing call tracking | Immediate outreach: "I noticed you're checking on your payoff. Are you thinking about refinancing? Let me run the numbers for you — I can show you whether it makes sense." |
| **New lien appears on their property** | Already gone | Title monitoring / county records | Win-back call within 48 hours (see Section 7). |
| **Dashboard login frequency drops** | 3–6 months | Engagement analytics | Re-engagement: "Your portfolio has some updates — [X] properties changed value this month. [Link]" |
| **Credit inquiry by competitor** | 1–3 months | Soft credit monitoring (with consent) | Immediate retention offer: "We see you're shopping. Before you commit elsewhere, let me show you what we can do." |
| **Portfolio DSCR deteriorating** | 3–6 months | Portfolio monitoring | Proactive outreach: "I noticed your DSCR on [property] has declined. Here's what we can do to help — [options]." |

### The Intervention Cascade

| Churn Risk | Population | Intervention | Who Owns It |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Critical** (top 5%) | 5% | Immediate RM call + personalized retention offer + rate match guarantee | Dedicated Relationship Manager |
| **High** (5–15%) | 10% | Proactive refinance analysis + loyalty discount + portfolio review invite | Loan Officer |
| **Medium** (15–30%) | 15% | Engagement campaign + value-add content + event invitation | Automated + LO backup |
| **Low** (30–100%) | 70% | Standard cadence; monitoring only | Automated |

### The "Rate Match Guarantee"

For Critical-risk borrowers:

> "If you've found a better rate with another lender, show us the Loan Estimate. If we can match or beat it, we will — and you keep all the benefits of your existing relationship: your dashboard, your streamlined application, your loyalty discount on future loans. If we can't beat it, we'll tell you honestly and wish you well."
