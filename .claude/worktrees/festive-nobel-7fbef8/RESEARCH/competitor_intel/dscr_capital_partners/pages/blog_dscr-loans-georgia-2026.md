## What Is a DSCR Loan?

A DSCR loan — short for Debt Service Coverage Ratio loan — is an investment property mortgage that qualifies the borrower based on the rental income of the subject property rather than personal income. The qualifying ratio is calculated by dividing the property's gross monthly rental income by its monthly debt obligations (PITIA — principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and HOA).

### DSCR Formula

DSCR = Gross Monthly Rental Income ÷ Monthly Debt Obligations

A DSCR of 1.0 means the property breaks even on cash flow. Higher ratios produce stronger pricing.

Because DSCR loans qualify off the property, they bypass the personal income, DTI, and tax-return hurdles that block many investors from scaling under conventional financing. There are no W-2s, no pay stubs, no tax returns, and no employment verification required.

## How to Calculate Your DSCR for a Georgia Property

Before applying, it helps to estimate your DSCR. A typical example: a single-family rental in suburban Atlanta (Marietta or Lawrenceville) renting for $2,400/month with PITIA of $1,950/month produces a DSCR of 1.23 — comfortably above the 1.0 threshold. For an Airbnb in Savannah's Historic District, the same calculation runs off AirDNA projected nightly revenue rather than a long-term lease.

1. **Estimate gross monthly rent.** Long-term: lease or market rent comp. Short-term: AirDNA projected revenue.
2. **Calculate full PITIA.** Don't forget HOA fees on condos and townhome rentals.
3. **Divide.** Income ÷ PITIA = your DSCR.
