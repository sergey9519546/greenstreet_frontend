## What Is a DSCR Loan?

A DSCR loan — Debt Service Coverage Ratio loan — qualifies the borrower based on the rental income of the subject property rather than personal income. The qualifying ratio is the property's gross monthly rent divided by its monthly debt obligations (PITIA: principal, interest, taxes, insurance, HOA).

### DSCR Formula

DSCR = Gross Monthly Rental Income ÷ Monthly Debt Obligations

A 1.0 DSCR means the property breaks even. Higher ratios produce better pricing.

Because qualification is based on the property, DSCR loans require no W-2s, no tax returns, no employment verification, and no debt-to-income calculation.

## Why Massachusetts Is a Strong Market for DSCR Loans

Massachusetts investor dynamics are dominated by **chronic housing undersupply**, sustained tenant demand from a tech and life-sciences employment base, and a regulatory environment that requires more diligence than most states. There's no statewide rent cap, but local jurisdictions can have meaningful tenant protections — particularly the City of Cambridge and the Boston metro core.

For STR investors, Cape Cod is the dominant market and one of the strongest summer STR concentrations in the Northeast. Boston's STR ordinance is restrictive (owner-occupied requirement in most cases). Most STR DSCR activity in MA happens on the Cape, the Berkshires, and outer-suburb markets.

## Top Massachusetts Markets for DSCR Investors

### Boston Metro

Cambridge, Brookline, Somerville, Newton, Quincy, Malden. High entry pricing — most deals require 30%+ down to clear DSCR. Multi-family (2–4 unit) the dominant DSCR product. Strong tenant credit, very low vacancy.

### Worcester

Massachusetts's cash-flow market. SFR and 2–3 unit prices significantly below the Boston ring. DSCRs 1.10–1.30 at 75% LTV common. Strong tenant demand from regional medical and university employment.

### Springfield & Western MA

Lowest entry prices in the state. SFR DSCRs frequently 1.30+ at 75% LTV. Tenant base steady, market less liquid than the eastern half of the state.

### Cape Cod & Islands

Dominant STR market. Summer-heavy revenue smoothed via AirDNA full-year projections. Martha's Vineyard and Nantucket production extremely high but with much higher entry prices.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Are DSCR loans available in Boston given the high prices? +

Yes. Boston DSCR loans typically require larger down payments (30%+) to clear the 1.0 DSCR threshold. Multi-family (2–4 unit) properties are the most common DSCR product in Boston because the rent stack supports the qualifying ratio more easily than SFR.

Can I get a DSCR loan for a Cape Cod summer rental? +

Yes. Cape Cod STRs are a core part of our short-term rental DSCR program. We use AirDNA full-year projections to smooth the summer-concentrated revenue.

Does Massachusetts have rent control? +

No statewide rent control, but local jurisdictions in the Boston metro have tenant protections that affect cash-flow modeling. DSCR underwriting still uses the appraiser-determined market rent or in-place lease.

What is the minimum credit score for a Massachusetts DSCR loan? +

620 minimum; best pricing at 720+. Boston-metro borrowers should expect to need stronger credit and more reserves to qualify at higher LTVs.

Can I close a Massachusetts DSCR loan in an LLC? +

Yes. MA DSCR loans close in LLCs routinely. MA LLCs have a $500 annual filing fee — modest relative to the loan size on most deals.
