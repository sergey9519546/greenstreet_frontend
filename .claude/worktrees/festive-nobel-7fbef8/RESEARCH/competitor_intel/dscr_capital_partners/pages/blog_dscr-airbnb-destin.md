## The Emerald Coast Beach STR DSCR Profile

Destin and the 30A corridor are among Florida's strongest vacation-rental markets, with premium summer ADRs and deep demand from the Southeast drive-to market. The program mirrors the universal [Airbnb / Short-Term Rental DSCR program](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/blog/dscr-loan-for-airbnb/): we underwrite the property's projected STR income against the new payment, not your W-2.

## STR-Friendly — But Watch the Seasonality

The regulatory picture here is investor-friendly: Florida's statewide preemption limits how far local governments can restrict short-term rentals, and Walton County's beach communities are built around vacation rentals (standard **short-term-rental registration** and a local responsible-party contact apply). The zoning question that blocks deals in regulated metros generally isn't the obstacle on the Emerald Coast.

The underwriting nuance here is **seasonality**. Revenue is heavily summer-weighted and slows November–February. DSCR underwriting uses _annual_ projected revenue, so a strong summer is already blended with the slow winter — but build your reserves around the cash-flow timing, not just the annual average, especially in year one.
