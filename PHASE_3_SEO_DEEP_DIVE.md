### FAQ Section (Schema-Ready)

```markdown
## Frequently Asked Questions

**What DSCR do I need to qualify for a loan?**
Most DSCR lenders require a minimum ratio of 1.0 to 1.25. A 1.0 DSCR means your rental income exactly covers the mortgage payment. Ratios above 1.25 qualify for better rates.

**Do DSCR loans require personal income verification?**
No. DSCR loans (Debt Service Coverage Ratio loans) qualify based on the property's rental income alone. You don't need W-2s, tax returns, or employment verification.

**What's the difference between DSCR and conventional loans?**
Conventional loans require personal income verification and debt-to-income ratio checks. DSCR loans only look at the property's cash flow. This makes them ideal for self-employed investors or those with complex income.

**Can I get a DSCR loan with 1.0 ratio?**
Yes. Several non-QM lenders offer 1.0 DSCR programs, meaning you can qualify even if rental income just breaks even with the mortgage payment. Rates are typically 0.5-1.0% higher than standard DSCR loans.

**How long does DSCR loan approval take?**
Most DSCR loans close in 21-30 days. Pre-approval takes 48 hours. The process is faster than conventional loans because there's no income verification.
```
