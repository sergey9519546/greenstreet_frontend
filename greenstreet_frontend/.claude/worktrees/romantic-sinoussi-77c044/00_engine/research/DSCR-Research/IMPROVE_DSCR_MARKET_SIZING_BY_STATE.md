## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) loan market is one of the fastest-growing segments in US mortgage lending, with estimated 2024 origination volume of **$12-15 billion** and projected 2025-2026 volume exceeding **$20-25 billion**. The market is highly concentrated by state — the top 5 states (FL, TX, CA, GA, AZ) account for an estimated **55-65%** of all DSCR originations, driven by no-income-tax environments, landlord-friendly legal regimes, population growth, and deep rental demand.

**Key findings:**
- **Florida** is the #1 DSCR market nationally — every major DSCR lender actively funds here
- **Texas** is the fastest-growing DSCR market with ~470K net new residents in 2024
- **California** has the highest DSCR volume per-capita but the worst DSCR economics (1.10-1.25 min DSCR vs. 0.75-1.25 nationally)
- **Ohio** offers the highest cash-flow yields (7-12% gross) but smaller loan sizes ($75K-180K)
- **New York** is the most expensive state for DSCR pricing (+0.50-1.00% above Southern states)
- Foreign nationals drive an estimated 15-20% of DSCR volume in FL, TX, and CA
- 14+ national DSCR lenders compete actively; state coverage varies from 50 states (Griffin) to 23+ (Angel Oak)

### 2.4 Why Some States Dominate DSCR Activity

The five key drivers of DSCR market share by state:

1. **No state income tax** (FL, TX, TN, NV, WA, WY, NH, SD, AK) — rental income flows to federal return only; investors keep more
2. **Fast foreclosure/eviction** (TX: 41-day legal minimum; GA: 30-45 days non-judicial) — lenders price more aggressively, expanding the DSCR-eligible universe
3. **Population growth** (FL: +800K/yr; TX: +470K/yr; AZ: +85-100K/yr) — drives rental demand and rent growth
4. **No prepayment penalty restrictions** (FL, AZ, CA allow PPPs; TX, GA, NY prohibit) — PPP availability reduces rates 0.25-0.50%, expanding borrower qualification
5. **No rent control** (TX, GA, FL preempt local rent control; CA, NY, OR have statewide restrictions) — more predictable income for DSCR qualification

### 6.3 How Foreign Demand Affects DSCR Market Dynamics

1. **Price premium acceptance:** Foreign investors accept 0.50-1.00% higher rates vs. domestic investors because DSCR is often their ONLY financing option
2. **Cash buyer competition:** In Miami, 38.5% of transactions close all-cash — many from foreign buyers, compressing the DSCR-eligible deal pool

4. **Currency considerations:** Foreign investors from countries with weakening currencies may accelerate US real estate purchases, creating demand spikes
5. **Seasonal patterns:** Canadian snowbirds concentrate DSCR applications in Q3-Q4 (pre-winter); Latin American investors concentrate Q1-Q2

## APPENDIX B: KEY TERMS & DEFINITIONS

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **DSCR** | Debt Service Coverage Ratio = Rent ÷ PITIA; qualifies loan on property cash flow |
| **PITIA** | Principal + Interest + Taxes + Insurance + Association Dues |
| **PPP** | Prepayment Penalty; 5/4/3/2/1 step-down is standard; prohibited in some states |
| **Non-QM** | Non-Qualified Mortgage; exempt from ATR/QM rules; DSCR is largest sub-segment |
| **LTV** | Loan-to-Value; maximum 80% purchase / 75% cash-out on standard DSCR |
| **STR** | Short-Term Rental (Airbnb/VRBO); 20% income haircut standard in DSCR underwriting |
| **BRRRR** | Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat; DSCR refi is the "Refinance" step |
| **BTR** | Build-to-Rent; institutional-grade SFR communities; growing DSCR refinance pipeline |
| **FIRPTA** | Foreign Investment in Real Property Tax Act; 15% withholding on foreign seller disposition |
| **Good Cause Eviction** | NY 2024 law limiting eviction and rent increases for non-rent-stabilized units |
| **AB 1482** | CA rent control law capping annual increases at 5%+CPI for properties 15+ years old |
