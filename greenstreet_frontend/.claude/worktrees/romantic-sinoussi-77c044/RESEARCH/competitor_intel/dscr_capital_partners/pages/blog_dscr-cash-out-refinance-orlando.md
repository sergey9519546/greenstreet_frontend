Orlando pairs a year-round tourism economy with one of the largest vacation-rental markets in the country. Between long-term rentals and Disney-area short-term rentals, there's a lot of equity to recycle.

This guide covers how a **DSCR cash-out refinance** works specifically in **Orlando** — the local market, the underwriting factors that actually move your number, and how to get a quote in 30 seconds. You qualify on the property's rental income, not your tax returns.

## Why Orlando Investors Cash Out

Orlando cash-out splits two ways: traditional long-term rentals in the metro core, and Disney-area vacation homes in Kissimmee/Davenport that qualify on AirDNA income. We underwrite either. The mechanics mirror our universal [Cash-Out Refinance DSCR program](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/blog/dscr-cash-out-refinance/): up to 75% of appraised value, the existing loan is paid off, and the rest comes back to you — tax-free\* loan proceeds. \*Consult your tax advisor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cash out a Disney-area vacation rental near Orlando? +

Yes — we finance Kissimmee/Davenport resort vacation homes on AirDNA projected income. See our [Kissimmee Airbnb DSCR guide](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/blog/dscr-airbnb-kissimmee/) for the STR specifics.

Long-term vs short-term rental — which cashes out better in Orlando? +

Both work. Long-term rentals qualify on the lease; short-term/vacation homes qualify on projected nightly revenue (at a haircut). The right one depends on the property and community rules.

Does Florida insurance affect my Orlando DSCR? +

Yes — wind and (in flood zones) flood premiums are part of PITIA and move the ratio. Orlando is inland, so it’s often less severe than the coast, but still get the quote early.
