## Why Tampa Works for DSCR Investors

Tampa's investor case rests on three forces. First, **population growth** — Hillsborough County continues to absorb net domestic migration at one of the highest rates in the U.S. Second, **rent-to-price fundamentals** still pencil at 1.10–1.30 DSCRs in many neighborhoods at 75% LTV. Third, **STR depth** — Tampa is a meaningful Airbnb market with growing cruise and Gulf-beach traffic.

## Tampa Sub-Markets & Neighborhoods

### South Tampa

Higher entry pricing ($600K–$1.5M for SFR), strong tenant credit. Multi-family more workable than SFR for DSCR qualification.

### Seminole Heights & Tampa Heights

SFR cash-flow plays at $300K–$550K. Strong rent growth, mid-renovation neighborhood with appreciation upside.

### Brandon & Riverview

Suburban SFR with stronger DSCRs (1.15–1.35 at 75% LTV). Build-to-rent communities expanding in southeast Hillsborough.

### New Tampa & Wesley Chapel

Family-rental dominant. Tenant credit strong, vacancy low. Pasco County (Wesley Chapel) growth corridor.

## Tampa DSCR Loan Requirements

### 2026 Requirements — Tampa

- **Minimum credit score:** 620 (best pricing at 740+)
- **Minimum DSCR:** 0.75 (1.0+ preferred)
- **Maximum LTV:** Up to 85% purchase / 75% cash-out refinance
- **Loan amount:** $100,000 to $15,000,000
- **Property types:** SFR, condo, 2–4 unit, 5+ unit multifamily, condotel
- **Loan terms:** 30-year fixed, ARM options, interest-only
- **Borrower types:** Individual, LLC, corporation, foreign national
- **Reserves:** Typically 3–6 months PITIA

## Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a DSCR loan for a Tampa Airbnb? +

Yes. Tampa STR financing is available under our short-term rental DSCR program using AirDNA full-year projections. Verify Hillsborough County or City of Tampa STR registration requirements before contracting.

What is the typical DSCR for a Tampa investment property? +

Tampa DSCRs run 1.05–1.35 at 75% LTV depending on neighborhood. South Tampa SFR is tighter (often requires 30%+ down to clear 1.0 DSCR); Brandon, Riverview, and Wesley Chapel typically clear 1.15–1.35 routinely.

Are Tampa cash-out refinances available? +

Yes. Tampa investment properties qualify for DSCR cash-out refinances up to 75% LTV. Common BRRRR strategy in mid-renovation neighborhoods like Seminole Heights.

Can foreign nationals buy Tampa rental property with a DSCR loan? +

Yes. Tampa is one of our most active foreign national DSCR markets, with significant Canadian and Latin American buyer activity.

Can I close my Tampa DSCR loan in an LLC? +

Yes. Florida LLCs are landlord-friendly and Tampa DSCR loans close routinely in single-member or multi-member LLCs.
