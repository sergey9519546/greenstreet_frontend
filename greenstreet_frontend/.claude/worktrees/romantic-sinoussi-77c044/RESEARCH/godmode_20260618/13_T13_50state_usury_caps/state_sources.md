## Federal Preemption Note (50-state blanket)

**National Bank Act (12 USC 85)**: National banks may charge the rate allowed by the bank's home state, regardless of borrower's state of residence. **Federal savings banks / savings associations** under HOLA (Home Owners' Loan Act, 12 USC 1464) are similarly preempted from state usury caps. **Federal credit unions** under FCUA (12 USC 1757) are limited to 15% (loans >$25K) or 18% (credit union may adopt higher).

**Implication for DSCR**: Lender structuring determines applicability — licensed state mortgage banker/broker is generally NOT federally preempted, so state caps still apply. National bank charter or federal savings bank is preempted. This is why DSCR programs using national-bank warehouse or conduit partners avoid the state-by-state usury constraint.
