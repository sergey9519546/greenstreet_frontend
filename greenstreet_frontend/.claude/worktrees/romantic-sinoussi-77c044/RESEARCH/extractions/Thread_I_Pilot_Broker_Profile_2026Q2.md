## 2. Pilot broker selection criteria

### Hard requirements (must have)

1. **Wholesale channel active:** Must work with 3+ non-QM/DSCR wholesale lenders (indicates they actively originate non-QM, not just dabble)
2. **Minimum volume:** 50+ loans/yr total; 10+ DSCR/non-QM loans/yr (justifies dedicated integration work)
3. **Geographic focus:** CA, FL, TX, AZ, GA, NC, NV (top DSCR states) — or be willing to expand to these
4. **Years in business:** 3+ years (reduces churn risk)
5. **NMLS compliant:** Active NMLS #, no recent regulatory actions
6. **Tech-forward signal:** Uses LOS, POS, or pricing engine beyond Excel (suggests API integration capability)

### Soft preferences (nice to have)

1. **Non-QM specialization:** 30%+ volume in non-QM or investment property
2. **Geographic concentration:** Single-state or 2-3 state focus (easier pilot to manage)
3. **Existing data sharing:** Already shares loan-level data with partners (banks, investors, etc.)
4. **Willingness to co-design:** Willing to participate in product feedback sessions (vs. just consuming)
5. **Geographic diversity across pilot cohort:** 5 brokers covering 4+ different states reduces correlated risk

### Anti-criteria (red flags)

1. **Predatory lending history** — any fair-lending actions, state AG actions, CFPB enforcement
2. **High churn of LOS** — broker has switched LOS 3+ times in 3 years
3. **Conflicting platform** — already committed to a competing AI/analytics platform
4. **Volume concentration** — single lender represents 60%+ of flow (lock-in risk)

## 5. Pilot economics (cost to acquire, value to extract)

### Cost to acquire 5 pilot brokers (3-month acquisition campaign)

| Activity | Cost |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn outbound tools (Apollo, ZoomInfo) | $500-$1,500/mo × 3 = $1.5K-$4.5K |
| NAMB affiliate fee (year 1) | $2K-$5K |
| Industry event travel + booth (2 events) | $10K-$25K |
| Pilot broker incentives (free Tier 4 access × 6 mo × 5 brokers) | $0 (cost in product, not cash) |
| Account management time (in-house, 25% of 1 FTE × 3 mo) | $15K-$25K |
| **Total acquisition cost** | **$30K-$60K** |

### Value to extract (per broker, 12-month horizon)

- Loan volume routed through Tier 4: 50-500 loans × $150-$500/loan analytics fee = **$7.5K-$250K/yr per broker**
- 5 brokers × midpoint $30K/yr = **$150K/yr ARR by end of Year 1** (if all 5 convert from pilot to paid)
- **Year 1 ROI on pilot program:** $150K revenue / $30K-$60K cost = **2.5-5x return**

### Conversion probability from pilot to paid

- Industry benchmark: 20-40% of free pilots convert to paid (per various SaaS pilot studies)
- Conservative estimate for our pilot: 30% conversion = 1.5 of 5 brokers convert to paid Year 1
- **Conservative Year 1 ARR from pilot program:** 1.5 brokers × $30K = $45K
- **Optimistic:** 3 brokers × $30K = $90K
