## 1. The single most important fix

The site is currently written **to mortgage brokers** — "The DSCR Engine for Non-QM
Brokers," "Trusted by brokers in 32 states," testimonials from "Principal Brokers."
But your customer is **the investor**, and *you are the broker/lender.* Flip every page
to speak to the person who wants the loan.

Your three audiences, in priority order:

1. **STR / Airbnb investors** — your lead story. Want to buy short-term rentals and
   scale a portfolio without their salary capping how many they can own.
2. **Foreign national / overseas investors** — own property abroad, want a US rental
   or Airbnb, have no US credit or US income. Underserved; give them their own page.
3. **Repeat & first-time DSCR investors** — own a rental (or want their first) and need
   a loan that qualifies the property, not their tax returns.

Positioning in one line:
**GreenStreet qualifies the property, not your paycheck — so your income never limits
how much real estate you can own.**

## 3. The STR opportunity section (your signature pitch — polished from your notes)

**Headline:** Seize the short-term rental opportunity.

**Lead:** In 2024, 86% of travelers worldwide booked a short-term rental, and demand
keeps climbing. The opportunity is real — the only question is how to finance it
without your personal income getting in the way. That's exactly what a DSCR loan is
for.

**The idea, in one line:** Because a DSCR loan qualifies on the property's rental
income instead of your salary, there's no limit to the number of short-term rentals
you can own. More doors, more cash flow.

### What it takes to qualify (STR)

- **25% down** minimum
- **600+ credit score**
- **6 months of reserves** set aside
- **The rule that matters:** the monthly rent needs to cover the full payment —
  principal, interest, insurance, taxes, and HOA (lenders call this PITIA). Clear that,
  and you're in DSCR territory.

### What you'll need

**Buying a short-term rental:**
- It helps if you already own an STR within ~250 miles of the new one.
- At least 12 months of history paying a mortgage or rent.

**Refinancing one you own:**
- 12 months of STR income history (24 is ideal), plus 12 months of forward projections.
- No STR track record yet? We can qualify you on long-term rental (LTR) income instead.

> *Note to confirm:* your "86% / +10% by 2025" stat is from 2024 — since it's now
> mid-2026, either cite the source year explicitly ("In 2024…") as written above, or
> refresh it with the latest travel-industry number so it doesn't read as dated.

## 5. How it works (4 steps)

**01 · Tell us about the property.** Address, expected rent, and your down payment.
No pay stubs, no tax returns to get started.

**02 · See your number in 60 seconds.** Your DSCR, the loan amount you qualify for,
your estimated rate, and your monthly payment — in plain English.

**03 · We structure, fund, and title it.** We match your file to the right GreenStreet
program — standard, no-ratio, multi-family, STR, or foreign national — and handle the
loan *and* the title in-house. One team, start to close.

**04 · Close in about 3–4 weeks.** The number you saw up front is the number at
closing. No surprise conditions.

## 9. Tools — reframe from "broker tools" to "investor answers"

- **DSCR / STR Calculator** — Will this property qualify, and for how much? 60 seconds, no income docs.
- **Deal Analyzer** — The full picture before you make an offer: payment, return, and break-even rent.
- **STR / Airbnb Underwriting** — We use real short-term-rental income (ADR × occupancy, seasonally adjusted), not optimistic Airbnb projections — so the number holds at closing.
- **After-Tax Returns** — What you actually keep after depreciation and taxes, every line traceable.
- **Refi Tracker** — When refinancing is worth it: the break-even month and the rate drop that pays for closing costs.
- **ARM Reset Analyzer** — The worst-case payment down the road, before you sign an adjustable loan.
- **Stress Matrix / Monte Carlo** — How the deal holds up if bookings or rates move against you.

## 12. Voice cheat-sheet

- Write to "you," the investor — never "brokers" or "users."
- Lead with the relief ("your income won't limit you"), explain the term second.
- Say the number. ("25% down," "600+ credit," "no income docs" — not "flexible terms.")
- One idea per sentence.
- Own the whole deal: you price it, fund it, and title it.
- Confident, not hyped — the facts carry the weight.
