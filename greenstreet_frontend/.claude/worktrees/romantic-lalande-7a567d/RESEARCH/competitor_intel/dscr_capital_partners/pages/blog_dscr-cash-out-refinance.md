# DSCR Cash-Out Refinance: Pull Equity Out of Your Rental in 2026

Quick Answer

**A DSCR cash-out refinance** replaces your current rental property mortgage with a new larger one, paying the difference to you as cash at closing. Max LTV is 75% (80% on strong files), seasoning is 3–6 months, and qualification uses the property's rental income vs new PITIA — no tax returns, no W-2s, no DTI calculation. Used to fund next acquisitions, recover BRRRR capital, or consolidate higher-rate debt.

A **DSCR cash-out refinance** lets investors pull equity out of an existing rental property without providing income documentation, tax returns, or DTI calculations. The qualifying math is the same as any DSCR loan — gross rent ÷ PITIA — just applied to the new loan amount you're rolling into.

This guide covers exactly how a DSCR cash-out refi works in 2026: LTV caps, seasoning rules, what credit and reserves you need, and the most common scenarios investors use it for.

## How a DSCR Cash-Out Refinance Works

You own a rental property. It has built up equity — either from appreciation, principal pay-down, or a value-add rehab. A DSCR cash-out refi lets you replace your current loan with a new larger one, pocketing the difference in cash, all qualified on the property's rental income alone.

The mechanics:

1. New appraisal establishes current market value.
2. You can borrow up to **75% of that appraised value** on a DSCR cash-out (vs 80–85% on a purchase).
3. Existing loan balance is paid off at closing.
4. Closing costs come out of the proceeds.
5. The remainder wires to you as cash.

## Cash-Out Refi Requirements — 2026

### DSCR Cash-Out Refinance Snapshot

- **Maximum LTV:** 75% (some programs 80% on stronger files)
- **Minimum credit score:** 660 (vs 620 on purchase)
- **Minimum DSCR on new loan:** 1.0 standard, 0.75 case-by-case
- **Seasoning:** 3–6 months ownership before cash-out at new appraised value
- **Reserves:** 6 months PITIA on new loan
- **Property types:** SFR, condo, 2–4 unit, 5+ unit multifamily, condotel
- **Borrower types:** Individual, LLC, foreign national

## Common Scenarios Investors Use This For

- **BRRRR refi.** Cash out post-rehab to recapture down payment + rehab spend. (See our [BRRRR DSCR guide](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/blog/dscr-loan-for-brrrr/).)
- **Down payment for the next acquisition.** Pull equity from a long-held rental to fund a new purchase without selling.
- **Consolidate higher-rate debt.** Pay off a hard-money loan, HELOC, or first DSCR loan with a higher rate by refinancing into current pricing.
- **Pull equity for rehab capital.** Fund a value-add rehab on a different property in the portfolio.
- **Personal liquidity.** Some investors use cash-out to fund non-real-estate ventures or personal needs.

## Why DSCR Cash-Out Beats HELOC for Investors

Many investors compare DSCR cash-out to a HELOC on the rental property. Three reasons DSCR cash-out usually wins for serious investors:

- **HELOCs on investment property are rare and expensive.** Most banks won't HELOC a non-owner-occupied property.
- **HELOC rates float.** DSCR cash-out locks a 30-year fixed rate.
- **HELOC LTV is lower** — usually 65–70% on investment property when available, vs 75% on DSCR cash-out.

HELOC may still win if you only need a small portion of equity and want flexible draw access. For larger amounts or rate certainty, DSCR cash-out is typically better.

## Cash-Out Refi Pricing — 2026

DSCR cash-out rates price 0.25–0.50% above purchase rates due to the higher risk profile. Strong-credit borrowers (740+) at 65–70% LTV typically see 6.50–7.25% in mid-2026. Most cash-out files include a 3-year prepayment penalty.

## Related Questions (People Also Ask)

How does cash-out refinance work on a rental property? +

A cash-out refinance replaces your current mortgage with a new larger one, with the difference paid to you as cash at closing. DSCR cash-out refis qualify on the property's rental income vs the new PITIA — not your personal income. Max LTV 75%; 3–6 months seasoning before using the new appraised value.

Is cash-out refinance worth it on a rental property? +

Often yes — especially if the equity will be redeployed into another acquisition, a higher-yielding investment, or used to consolidate higher-rate debt. If the cash pulled out earns more than the new monthly carry costs, it pencils.

How long do I have to wait to refinance after buying a rental? +

For DSCR cash-out at the new appraised value, 3–6 months ownership is standard. Rate-and-term refinances (no cash out) can sometimes happen immediately if there's a clear benefit to the borrower.

Are cash-out refinance proceeds taxable? +

No. Cash-out refinance proceeds are not taxable income — you're borrowing against equity, not realizing gain. (Consult your CPA; this is not tax advice.)

What's the difference between a HELOC and a cash-out refi on a rental? +

A HELOC is a revolving variable-rate line, typically capped at 65–70% CLTV on investment property when available. A DSCR cash-out refi is a fixed-rate 30-year mortgage at up to 75% LTV. Cash-out refis usually win for larger amounts.

Will a cash-out refi hurt my credit? +

The credit pull at application is a hard inquiry (5–10 point temporary drop). Long-term impact comes from how the new payment affects your debt utilization.

Can I do a cash-out refi if my DSCR is below 1.0? +

Yes, at a lower LTV (typically 65–70% instead of 75%) and a higher rate. We can go down to 0.75 DSCR on cash-out, with case-by-case exceptions below that on no-ratio programs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

How much equity can I pull from my rental with a DSCR cash-out refi? +

Up to 75% LTV on most programs; 80% on strong-file exceptions. The actual amount depends on your current appraised value, existing loan balance, and DSCR on the new loan.

Do I need to provide tax returns for a DSCR cash-out refinance? +

No. DSCR cash-out qualifies entirely on the property's rental income vs the new PITIA. No tax returns, no W-2s, no employment verification.

How long do I need to own the property before cash-out? +

3 months minimum at most lenders to use the new appraised value. Some stricter programs require 6 months.

Can I do a DSCR cash-out on a property held in an LLC? +

Yes. LLC-held properties are routinely eligible for DSCR cash-out. The LLC remains on title; we lend to the LLC.

What if my new DSCR is below 1.0? +

We can go down to 0.75 DSCR on cash-out at lower LTV. The lower the DSCR, the lower the LTV cap and the higher the rate.
