## What Is a DSCR Loan?

A DSCR loan — Debt Service Coverage Ratio loan — qualifies the borrower based on the rental income of the subject property rather than personal income. The qualifying ratio is the property's gross monthly rent divided by its monthly debt obligations (PITIA: principal, interest, taxes, insurance, HOA).

### DSCR Formula

DSCR = Gross Monthly Rental Income ÷ Monthly Debt Obligations

A 1.0 DSCR means the property breaks even. Higher ratios produce better pricing.

Because qualification is based on the property, DSCR loans require no W-2s, no tax returns, no employment verification, and no debt-to-income calculation.

## Why Nevada Is a Strong Market for DSCR Loans

Nevada has no state income tax, business-friendly LLC and trust formation, and one of the most liquid investor real estate markets in the West. The Las Vegas valley absorbs continuous in-migration from California, and the metro's logistics and tech employment base (Switch, Tesla via Sparks-Reno corridor) supports steady rental demand.

STR investors should pay particular attention to local jurisdiction rules — Clark County (unincorporated) and the City of Las Vegas have very different STR licensing regimes. Henderson is generally STR-friendlier than the City of Las Vegas; North Las Vegas remains the most permissive. Our STR DSCR program qualifies legally permitted STRs using AirDNA projections.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a DSCR loan for a Las Vegas Strip-area Airbnb? +

Yes — provided the property is legally permitted as an STR by the controlling jurisdiction. The City of Las Vegas restricts STRs heavily; Clark County unincorporated and Henderson are more permissive. We qualify using AirDNA projected revenue.

What is the minimum DSCR for a Nevada investment property? +

1.0 standard, 0.75 case-by-case. North Las Vegas and outer Henderson properties typically clear 1.20+ at 75% LTV without strain.

Are South Lake Tahoe STR loans available? +

Yes. South Lake Tahoe has its own STR permit cap and inspection requirements — confirm a transferable permit or new permit availability before contracting. We finance permitted STR properties using full-year AirDNA projections.

Can foreign nationals buy Las Vegas rental property with a DSCR loan? +

Yes. We finance foreign national investors on Las Vegas, Henderson, and Reno properties with no U.S. credit required.

Does Nevada have rent control affecting DSCR underwriting? +

No. Nevada has no statewide rent control or rent cap. DSCR qualifying rent is the appraiser-determined market rent or in-place lease.
