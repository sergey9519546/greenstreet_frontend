## What Is a DSCR Loan?

A DSCR loan qualifies the borrower based on the rental income of the subject property rather than personal income. The qualifying ratio is gross monthly rent divided by PITIA (principal, interest, taxes, insurance, HOA).

### DSCR Formula

DSCR = Gross Monthly Rental Income ÷ Monthly Debt Obligations

No W-2s, tax returns, employment verification, or DTI calculations required.

## Why Michigan Is a Strong DSCR Market

Michigan offers something most coastal investor markets don't: **cash flow at scale**. Single-family rentals in metro Detroit, Lansing, Flint, and Saginaw routinely produce gross rent yields above 1.0% — meaning a $150,000 home renting for $1,500/month is common. That math drives DSCRs well above 1.0 even at 80% LTV.

The state's investor-friendly profile is reinforced by reasonable property tax rates (vs. Illinois or Ohio), strong eviction processing times, and Michigan's status as a non-judicial foreclosure state. For STR investors, northern Michigan — Traverse City, Petoskey, Charlevoix, the Sleeping Bear corridor — is one of the most established summer vacation-rental regions in the U.S.

## DSCR Loan Requirements in Michigan

### DSCR Loan Requirements — Michigan

- **Minimum credit score:** 620
- **Minimum DSCR:** 0.75 (1.0+ preferred)
- **Maximum LTV:** 85% purchase / 75–80% cash-out refinance
- **Loan amount:** $100,000 to $15,000,000
- **Property types:** SFR, condo, 2–4 unit, 5+ unit multifamily
- **Loan terms:** 30-year fixed, ARM options, interest-only
- **Borrower types:** Individual, LLC, corporation, foreign national
- **Reserves:** 3–6 months PITIA

One Michigan-specific note: many Detroit and Flint single-family properties trade well below the $100,000 minimum loan threshold. For these, investors typically use our **portfolio DSCR program**, which packages multiple low-balance properties into a single loan — usually 5–20 properties at a time.
