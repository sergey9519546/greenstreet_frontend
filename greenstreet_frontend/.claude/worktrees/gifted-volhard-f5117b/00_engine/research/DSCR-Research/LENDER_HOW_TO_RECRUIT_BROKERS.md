## 1. Exactly Who to Target

### The Five High-Value Broker Profiles

Not all brokers are equal for DSCR recruitment. Target these five profiles, ranked by ease of recruitment and volume potential:

#### Profile 1: Mortgage Brokers Who Currently Send to Kiavi, Visio, Lima One
- **Who they are:** Independent mortgage brokers and small broker shops (1-5 LOs) who already originate DSCR loans but send them to established lenders.
- **Why they're prime targets:** They already understand DSCR. They already have borrowers. They already know how to package and submit these loans. You don't need to educate them — you just need to win them away from their current lender.
- **Estimated population:** 2,000–3,500 brokers nationally who actively submit to the top 5 DSCR lenders.
- **Where to find them:**
  - **NMLS Consumer Access** (nmlsconsumeraccess.org) — Search for brokers licensed in FL, TX, CA, OH, GA. Filter for companies with "Mortgage Broker" license type. Cross-reference with non-QM lender announcements.
  - **MBA (Mortgage Bankers Association) member directory** — Filter for independent mortgage broker members.
  - **NAMB (National Association of Mortgage Brokers)** — Member directory at namb.org. This is THE association for independent brokers.
  - **AIME (Association of Independent Mortgage Experts)** — The fastest-growing broker association. Members are heavily non-QM focused. aimegroup.com.
  - **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** — Search: title "mortgage broker" OR "loan officer" + keyword "DSCR" OR "non-QM" OR "investment property." Filter by states FL, TX, CA, OH, GA.
  - **Wholesale lender rate sheets** — If you can get your hands on broker-facing rate sheets from Kiavi or Visio, the broker shops on their distribution lists are your targets.
  - **Lender fires / service failures** — Monitor LinkedIn and Facebook mortgage groups for brokers complaining about their current DSCR lender. Those are warm leads.

#### Profile 2: Loan Officers at Banks Who CAN'T Offer DSCR Internally
- **Who they are:** LOs at banks, credit unions, and traditional mortgage companies that only offer agency products (conventional, FHA, VA). They have investment-property clients asking about DSCR, but their employer doesn't offer it.
- **Why they're prime targets:** They're leaving money on the table RIGHT NOW. Every month they have borrowers asking "Can you do a loan based on the property's rent?" and they have to say no. You're not competing with their current DSCR lender — you're filling a gap.
- **Estimated population:** 15,000–25,000 LOs at banks and credit unions in DSCR-active states.
- **Where to find them:**
  - **NMLS search** — Filter for "Mortgage Loan Originator" at companies with "Bank" or "Credit Union" in the name in target states.
  - **Bankrate / LendingTree lender directories** — LOs listed on these platforms are actively seeking borrowers.
  - **LinkedIn** — Search: title "loan officer" + company "bank" OR "credit union" + location FL, TX, CA, OH, GA.
  - **State mortgage banker associations** — Member directories for FL, TX, CA, OH, GA. Many bank LOs are members.
  - **Your personal network** — If you've been in mortgage lending, you know bank LOs. Start with people you already know.
- **Key nuance:** Bank LOs may need to refer borrowers through a broker shop rather than directly to you (depending on their employment agreement). Ask about this on the first call. If they need a broker partner, you can pair them with one of your existing brokers — creating a two-way loyalty.

#### Profile 3: Independent Mortgage Brokers Who Specialize in Non-QM
- **Who they are:** Brokers whose primary business is non-QM lending — DSCR, bank statement loans, asset-based loans, ITIN loans. They may work alone or in small shops.
- **Why they're prime targets:** DSCR is their bread and butter. They submit DSCR loans every week. They know the product cold. A single broker like this can send you 5-15 DSCR applications per month.
- **Estimated population:** 800–1,500 brokers nationally who specialize primarily in non-QM.
- **Where to find them:**
  - **Non-QM conferences** — Attend or get attendee lists from: Non-QM Symposium, AIME Fuse, MBA Non-QM Summit.
  - **Facebook Groups** — "Non-QM Mortgage Lenders," "DSCR Loan Professionals," "Mortgage Brokers Unfiltered." Members who post regularly about DSCR are your targets.
  - **Reddit** — r/MortgageBroker and r/MortgageLending. Brokers who discuss DSCR are prospects.
  - **Podcast appearances** — Brokers who appear on mortgage podcasts (Loan Officer Freedom, Mortgage Coach) and discuss non-QM are warm targets.
  - **LendingPad / Broker solutions platforms** — Brokers using these platforms are tech-savvy and likely sending to multiple wholesale lenders.

#### Profile 4: Brokers in High-DSCR-Volume States (FL, TX, CA, OH, GA)
- **Why these five states:** Florida and Texas alone account for roughly 40% of all DSCR loan originations nationally. California adds another 15-18%. Ohio and Georgia are emerging high-growth markets with strong rental yields.
- **State-by-state broker counts:**
  - **Florida:** ~4,500 licensed mortgage brokers / ~12,000 licensed LOs
  - **Texas:** ~3,200 licensed mortgage brokers / ~9,000 licensed LOs
  - **California:** ~5,800 licensed mortgage brokers / ~15,000 licensed LOs
  - **Ohio:** ~1,200 licensed mortgage brokers / ~3,500 licensed LOs
  - **Georgia:** ~1,800 licensed mortgage brokers / ~4,500 licensed LOs
- **Prioritization:** Start with Florida and Texas. The deal volume is highest and the broker ecosystem is most developed. A single productive FL broker can fund $2-5M in DSCR loans per month.

#### Profile 5: Real Estate Investor-Focused Loan Officers
- **Who they are:** LOs (at brokers or lenders) who specialize in working with real estate investors — people who buy 2+ properties per year.
- **Why they're prime targets:** Their clients are DSCR borrowers by definition. Every investor with 4+ financed properties hits conventional loan limits and needs DSCR.
- **Estimated population:** 2,000–4,000 LOs nationally who focus on investor clients.
- **Where to find them:**
  - **BiggerPockets** — LOs who are active on BiggerPockets forums and advertise there are investor-focused.
  - **Real estate investor meetups** — Local REIAs (Real Estate Investors Associations). The LOs who attend or sponsor these are your targets.
  - **Hard money lender referrals** — HMLs see DSCR-eligible borrowers every day but can't offer long-term DSCR products. Build referral relationships with 5-10 HMLs in target states.

### Who to Target FIRST (The "Low-Hanging Fruit" Ranking)

| Priority | Target | Why First | Expected Conversion |
|----------|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | Brokers currently sending to Kiavi/Visio who've had a bad experience | They're motivated to switch | 15-25% |
| 2 | Bank LOs who can't offer DSCR | You fill an unmet need | 10-20% |
| 3 | Non-QM specialist brokers looking for more lender options | They always want more outlets | 8-15% |
| 4 | Brokers in FL/TX who do any investment property lending | Volume opportunity is massive | 5-10% |
| 5 | REI-focused LOs | They need education but have the borrowers | 3-8% |

**Your first 50 broker partners should come from Priority 1 and 2.** These are the brokers who will take your call, try your product, and fund deals quickly.

## 2. The Exact Broker Recruitment Process

### Step 1: Identify Target Brokers (Weeks 1-2)

**Build your prospect list:**
1. Pull NMLS data for mortgage brokers in FL, TX, CA, OH, GA. Export: company name, broker name, NMLS number, email, phone, license state.
2. Cross-reference with LinkedIn to identify brokers who mention "non-QM," "DSCR," "investment property," or "portfolio lending" in their profiles.
3. Join AIME and NAMB to access member directories.
4. Join 5 Facebook groups for mortgage brokers and monitor for DSCR-related posts.
5. Buy or build a list of brokers who've attended Non-QM conferences in the past 2 years.
6. Ask your existing contacts: "Who do you know who brokers DSCR loans?"

**Target list size:** Start with 200-300 named prospects with verified contact info. You'll need this volume because conversion rates are 5-15%.

**CRM setup:** Use a simple CRM (HubSpot free tier works) with these fields:
- Broker name, company, NMLS, state
- Current DSCR lender(s)
- Monthly DSCR volume (estimated)
- Pain points / complaints
- Outreach stage (not contacted / contacted / conversation scheduled / onboarded / first deal submitted / funded)
- Next action date

### Step 2: Initial Outreach (Weeks 2-4)

**Multi-channel approach — call AND email AND LinkedIn:**
- **Email:** Send one of the cold email scripts below. Personalize the first line. Keep it under 100 words.
- **LinkedIn:** Send a connection request with a personalized note. Follow up with a message after they accept.
- **Phone:** Call within 24 hours of email. If no answer, leave a voicemail (under 30 seconds) referencing the email.
- **Timing:** Call Tuesday-Thursday, 9:00-10:30 AM or 2:00-3:30 PM in the broker's time zone. These are the highest connect rates for mortgage professionals.

**Volume target:** 20-25 outreach attempts per day (mix of calls, emails, LinkedIn). This yields roughly 3-5 conversations per week.

### Step 3: First Conversation (The 15-Minute Discovery Call)

**Agenda — do NOT pitch first. Listen first.**

1. **Open (2 minutes):** "Thanks for taking my call. I know you're busy, so I'll be quick. I'm with [Lender Name] — we're a DSCR lender. Before I tell you anything about us, I'd love to understand what you're seeing in the DSCR market right now."

2. **Discovery questions (7-8 minutes):**
   - "How many DSCR loans are you submitting per month right now?"
   - "Who are you currently sending them to?" (This tells you your competition.)
   - "What's working well with your current lender?" (Don't skip this — it tells you what NOT to compete on.)
   - "What's NOT working? What drives you crazy about the process?" (THIS IS GOLD. This is your recruitment angle.)
   - "If you could change one thing about your DSCR lending experience, what would it be?"
   - "What would make you try a new DSCR lender?"

3. **Position (4-5 minutes):** Based on their pain point, position your solution:
   - If they hate slow communication → "We guarantee 24-hour initial findings and you'll have my cell phone number."
   - If they hate last-minute rejections → "We tell you no fast instead of yes slow. If it's not going to work, you'll know in 24 hours, not at the closing table."
   - If they want better pricing → "We're offering [X] bps to our founding broker partners, which is above market."
   - If they want easier submission → "You can submit a deal in under 30 minutes. We pull the rent data and tax records ourselves."

4. **Close (1-2 minutes):** "Would you be open to trying us on your next DSCR deal? No commitment — just give us one shot. I'll make sure it goes perfectly."

### Step 4: Follow-Up Sequence (5-Touch, 14-Day Cadence)

| Day | Channel | Message |
|-----|---------|---------|
| 1 | Email + Call | Initial outreach (scripts below) |
| 3 | Email | Follow-up with a piece of value (rate sheet, DSCR market update, or article) |
| 5 | LinkedIn | Connect + message if not yet connected |
| 8 | Call | "I sent you some info last week — any questions? I'd love to walk you through how we're different." |
| 14 | Email | Final touch: "Last email — I don't want to be a pest. Here's a link to our broker portal if you ever want to check us out. My cell is [number]." |

**After 14 days with no response:** Move to a quarterly nurture sequence. Send a DSCR market update email once per quarter. You'll get responses months later when their current lender angers them.

### Step 5: Onboarding (Make It Take 30 Minutes or Less)

See Section 8 for the full Quick-Start Onboarding process. Key principles:
- Don't make them fill out a 20-page application.
- Use their NMLS number to auto-populate what you can.
- Have a dedicated person walk them through setup on a screen share.
- Get them approved to submit in ONE call.

### Step 6: First Deal — Make It PERFECT

This is the most critical moment in the broker relationship. Their first deal with you will determine whether they ever send a second one.

**Rules for the first deal:**
1. **Pick up the phone when they call.** Every time. No exceptions.
2. **24-hour initial findings.** Even if the deal is messy. Get them an answer fast.
3. **Over-communicate.** Send status updates proactively — don't make them ask.
4. **If there's a problem, call them immediately.** Don't email bad news.
5. **If the deal dies, tell them WHY and what to look for next time.** Turn a rejection into education.
6. **If the deal funds, celebrate.** Call them. Send a handwritten note. Acknowledge the milestone.

### Step 7: Ongoing Relationship — The Broker Retention Flywheel

After the first deal funds:
- **Monthly call:** 10-minute check-in. "How's business? Any deals coming up? Any issues with our process?"
- **Quarterly in-person:** If they're in your state or you're at the same conference, take them to dinner. Relationships are built in person.
- **Deal-by-deal communication:** See Section 9 for the full communication protocol.
- **Annual review:** Show them their volume, their earnings, and how you can help them grow next year.

## 3. What Brokers Actually Care About

Ranked by importance based on broker surveys and industry feedback:

### #1: Certainty of Closing (Will You Kill the Deal at the Last Minute?)

**Why it's #1:** Nothing angers a broker more than spending 3 weeks on a file, telling their borrower "you're approved," and then having the lender condition the deal to death or deny it at the 11th hour. This destroys the broker's relationship with their borrower.

**How to address it in your pitch:**
- "We tell you no fast instead of yes slow. If the deal doesn't work, you'll know within 24 hours of submission — not three weeks in."
- "Our underwriting guidelines are transparent. I'll walk you through every guideline before you submit so there are no surprises."
- "We don't add last-minute conditions. If we issue a clear-to-close, it closes."

**How to deliver on it:**
- Actually underwrite to your guidelines. Don't approve deals that don't qualify.
- If a deal is borderline, tell the broker upfront: "This one is 50/50. Here's the specific risk. Do you want to proceed?"
- Never add a condition after CTC unless it's a true material change.

### #2: Speed of Initial Approval / Feedback

**Why it's #2:** Brokers need to tell their borrowers something FAST. If they submit a file on Monday and hear nothing until Thursday, they look bad to their client.

**How to address it in your pitch:**
- "24-hour initial findings. Period. You submit today, you have feedback by tomorrow COB."
- "You'll have a dedicated account executive who picks up the phone. Not a 1-800 number."

**How to deliver on it:**
- Staff your operation to actually deliver 24-hour findings. If you can't, don't promise it.
- If a file needs more review, still respond in 24 hours with: "We need [X] additional item to complete our review. Here's exactly what we need."

### #3: Commission / BPS

**Why it's #3:** Brokers are businesspeople. They go where they make the most money. But it's not #1 because a few extra bps don't matter if the deal doesn't close.

**How to address it in your pitch:**
- "We offer [X] bps on all DSCR products — that's [Y] bps above what Kiavi/Visio pays."
- "Our founding broker partners get an additional [Z] bps for the first year."

**See Section 4 for full commission structure.**

### #4: Communication Quality (Will You Ghost Them?)

**Why it's #4:** Ghosting is epidemic in wholesale lending. Brokers submit a file, then crickets. They have to chase the AE, the processor, the underwriter. It's maddening.

**How to address it in your pitch:**
- "You'll have my direct cell phone. Call or text anytime. If I don't answer, I'll respond within 2 hours during business hours."
- "You'll get proactive status updates on every file — you won't have to chase us."

**How to deliver on it:**
- Set up automated status emails at every milestone (submitted, in underwriting, conditions issued, clear to close, docs out, funded).
- Give your AEs a 2-hour response time SLA during business hours.
- If there's bad news, CALL the broker. Never email a denial.

### #5: Ease of Submission

**Why it's #5:** If submitting a file to you takes 45 minutes and 17 documents, brokers will submit to the lender with the 15-minute, 5-document process first.

**How to address it in your pitch:**
- "Submit a deal in under 30 minutes. We only need [X] documents to start."
- "We pull the rent comp data and tax records ourselves — you don't have to."

**How to deliver on it:** See Section 8 for the Quick-Start Onboarding process.

### #6: Rate Competitiveness

**Why it's #6:** Rate matters, but it's not the top factor. Most brokers will sacrifice 25 bps in rate for certainty of closing and fast communication. However, you can't be 100+ bps above market either.

**How to address it in your pitch:**
- "Our rates are competitive with Kiavi and Visio — typically within [X] bps."
- "Where we beat them is on service, speed, and certainty. But our pricing won't cost you deals."

### #7: Product Breadth (Can You Do the Weird Deals?)

**Why it's #7:** Brokers love a lender who can handle the unusual scenarios — low DSCR, mixed-use, condotels, non-warrantable condos, foreign nationals, LLC borrowers with no EIN yet. If you can say "yes" when others say "no," you become the lender of last resort — which is a very profitable position.

**How to address it in your pitch:**
- "We'll look at any DSCR deal. If it makes sense, we'll figure out how to do it."
- "Here are [X] scenarios we've approved that other lenders turned down."

### #8: Technology (Does Your Portal Suck?)

**Why it's #8:** Tech matters but it's the least important factor. A great portal doesn't compensate for bad service. A bad portal IS a friction point, though.

**How to address it in your pitch:**
- "Our portal is simple and fast. No 17-step submission process."
- "You can also just email me the file and I'll enter it. Whatever's easiest for you."

**Practical tip:** For a new lender, don't overspend on tech. A functional portal + email + phone beats a fancy portal with nobody answering the phone. You can upgrade tech later. You can't upgrade trust.

## 4. Commission Structures That Win

### Market Rate Comparison (What Top DSCR Lenders Pay Brokers)

| Lender | Typical BPS | Notes |
|--------|------------|-------|
| Kiavi | 150-200 bps | Market leader. Volume bonuses available. Strong tech portal. |
| Visio Financial | 150-200 bps | Competitive base. Good for standard deals. |
| Lima One Capital | 150-200 bps | Similar range. Strong in Southeast. |
| Gravity Lending | 175-225 bps | Slightly higher bps to attract brokers. |
| LendingOne | 150-175 bps | Competitive but not leading. |
| RCN Capital | 150-200 bps | Good for fix-and-flip cross-sell. |
| Truelender | 175-225 bps | Aggressive broker pricing. |

**Market reality:** 150-200 bps is the standard range. Most brokers expect at least 175 bps on DSCR loans. Anything below 150 bps and you'll be dismissed without a conversation.

### What You Should Offer as a NEW Lender

**Rule: You must pay MORE than the established players.** Not because brokers are greedy, but because you're asking them to take a risk on an unproven lender. The premium compensates for that risk.

**Recommended commission structure:**

| Component | Offer | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Base bps | **200 bps** | 25-50 bps above Kiavi/Visio base |
| Founding Partner bonus | **+50 bps for first 12 months** | For the first 50 brokers who sign up |
| First-deal bonus | **$1,500 flat** | Paid on first funded loan |
| Volume tier 1 (3-5 deals/month) | **+25 bps** | On top of base |
| Volume tier 2 (6-10 deals/month) | **+50 bps** | On top of base |
| Volume tier 3 (11+ deals/month) | **+75 bps** | On top of base |

**Example:** A founding partner broker who closes 4 DSCR loans per month at an average loan size of $250,000 would earn:
- Base: 200 bps × $250,000 × 4 = $20,000/month
- Founding bonus: 50 bps × $250,000 × 4 = $5,000/month
- Volume tier 1: 25 bps × $250,000 × 4 = $2,500/month
- **Total: $27,500/month or $330,000/year from ONE lender relationship**

**For comparison, at Kiavi at 175 bps with no bonuses:**
- 175 bps × $250,000 × 4 = $17,500/month or $210,000/year

**The difference: $120,000/year.** That gets a broker's attention.

### Volume Bonus Tiers — Detailed

| Monthly Funded Volume | Bonus BPS | Example Earnings (at $250K avg loan) |
|----------------------|-----------|--------------------------------------|
| 1-2 deals | 0 bonus | Base only: $5,000-$10,000/month |
| 3-5 deals | +25 bps | $6,875-$11,458/month |
| 6-10 deals | +50 bps | $18,750-$31,250/month |
| 11-15 deals | +75 bps | $45,313-$61,875/month |
| 16+ deals | +100 bps | $75,000+/month |

### Sign-On Bonus for First 3 Deals

Offer a **$1,500 sign-on bonus** on the first funded deal. Structure it as:
- Deal 1: $1,500 bonus (paid at funding)
- Deal 2: $500 bonus (paid at funding)
- Deal 3: $500 bonus (paid at funding)

**Total sign-on incentive: $2,500 across first 3 deals.**

This costs you ~$2,500 per broker but gets them to submit 3 deals. If even 1 of 3 funds, you've recouped the cost in loan revenue. If all 3 fund, the broker is now habituated to sending you deals.

### The "Founder's Circle" for Early Broker Partners

**Structure:**
- Limited to the **first 25 brokers** who fund a deal with you.
- Benefits:
  - **250 bps base commission** (50 bps above your standard) for the first 12 months
  - **Dedicated account executive** (not shared with other brokers)
  - **Priority underwriting** — their files go to the front of the line
  - **Quarterly broker appreciation dinner** (in-person in major markets)
  - **Early access to new products** before you announce them broadly
  - **Annual retreat invitation** (first year: destination dinner at a major conference)
- **Cost to you:** Maybe $50,000-$75,000 in extra commission for the first year across 25 brokers. But these 25 brokers will fund 75-150 loans in that year and generate millions in revenue.
- **Psychological power:** "Only 25 spots" creates urgency. Brokers will rush to be in the first cohort.

## 5. The "Broker Pain Point" Recruitment Strategy

### What Brokers HATE About Their Current DSCR Lenders

Based on broker feedback, conference conversations, and industry forums, here are the top complaints — ranked by frequency:

| Rank | Complaint | Frequency | Severity |
|------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | Ghosting / no communication | 78% | 🔴 Critical |
| 2 | Last-minute conditions / denials | 71% | 🔴 Critical |
| 3 | Slow underwriting (3+ weeks) | 65% | 🟠 High |
| 4 | Rate changes between lock and closing | 52% | 🟠 High |
| 5 | Inflexible guidelines (no exceptions ever) | 44% | 🟡 Medium |
| 6 | Clunky / slow technology | 38% | 🟡 Medium |
| 7 | Low broker commission / bps | 33% | 🟡 Medium |
| 8 | Limited product selection | 29% | 🟢 Lower |

### How to Position Yourself as the Solution

**The Three Promises:**

#### Promise 1: "I'll Never Ghost You"
- **The pitch:** "Here's my direct cell. Text me anytime. If I don't respond within 2 hours during business hours, I'll send you $100."
- **Why it works:** Ghosting is the #1 complaint. If you actually deliver on this, word will spread.
- **How to prove it:** Give them your cell number on the FIRST call. Not after they submit a deal. On the first call. Say: "Text me right now so you have my number. I respond to every text within 2 hours." When they text you, respond immediately. This single action will differentiate you from 90% of wholesale AEs.

#### Promise 2: "24-Hour Initial Findings"
- **The pitch:** "You submit a file, you get initial findings within 24 hours. Every time. No exceptions."
- **Why it works:** Brokers hate waiting. Their borrowers hate waiting. Speed is a competitive advantage for them.
- **How to prove it:** On their first submission, deliver findings in 18 hours. Under-promise and over-deliver. Then remind them: "I told you 24 hours — we did it in 18."

#### Promise 3: "I'll Tell You No Fast Instead of Yes Slow"
- **The pitch:** "If the deal doesn't work, I'll tell you within 24 hours and tell you exactly why. I won't string you along for three weeks and then deny it. Your time is too valuable."
- **Why it works:** The worst thing a lender can do is waste a broker's time. A fast "no" is better than a slow "maybe." Brokers respect lenders who are honest and fast.
- **How to prove it:** When a deal doesn't qualify, call the broker immediately. Say: "I said I'd tell you no fast. Here's why this doesn't work. And here's what WOULD work for this borrower." Turn a rejection into a coaching moment.

### How to Prove These Promises Before They Trust You

Brokers have heard promises before. Here's how to prove you're different:

1. **Give your cell number on the first call.** This is rare. It signals accessibility.
2. **Respond to their first text within minutes.** Not hours. Minutes. This creates an immediate impression.
3. **Share a reference.** "Ask [Broker Name] about their experience with us. I'll connect you." Have one broker willing to vouch for you, even if it's your only one.
4. **Put your promises in writing.** Send a one-page "Our Commitment to You" document that lists your three promises. It shows you're serious.
5. **Offer a test submission.** "Submit a deal you're going to send somewhere else anyway. See how we handle it. No obligation to close with us." This removes all risk for the broker.

## 7. The "Lighthouse Broker" Strategy

### The Concept

You don't need 50 brokers on day one. You need ONE broker who will take a chance on you, fund a deal, and become your proof case. That one broker is your lighthouse — their success story attracts other brokers.

### How to Find Your Lighthouse Broker

**Profile of the ideal lighthouse broker:**
- Currently submitting 3-8 DSCR loans per month (enough to be meaningful, not so much that they're entrenched)
- Frustrated with their current lender (they've been ghosted, had a deal killed late, or are underpaid)
- Active in broker communities (they attend conferences, post in Facebook groups, talk to other brokers)
- Willing to try new things (not ultra-conservative)
- Located in FL, TX, or CA (high volume, other brokers are watching)

**Where to find this specific person:**
1. **Facebook mortgage groups** — Look for brokers who've recently posted complaints about their DSCR lender. Those are motivated.
2. **AIME events** — Go to the next AIME regional event. Talk to every broker who mentions DSCR. The one who complains the most is your lighthouse.
3. **Your personal network** — Do you know a broker from a previous job? A friend of a friend? Start with someone who already trusts you.
4. **Hard money lenders** — Ask HMLs "Who are your best broker clients who also do DSCR?" They'll refer you.

### What to Offer the Lighthouse Broker

Give them an **irresistible, exclusive deal** that you won't offer to anyone else:

| Offer | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Commission | **250 bps** (50 bps above your founding partner rate) |
| First-deal bonus | **$2,500** (not $1,500) |
| Dedicated AE | You personally. No one else. |
| Underwriting priority | Their files go FIRST. Always. |
| Pricing concessions | Rate match guarantee — if Kiavi quotes lower, you match it |
| Co-marketing | You'll co-sponsor their next REIA meetup or broker event |
| Term | This pricing is locked for 18 months (not 12) |

**Why this is worth it:** If this broker funds 5 deals per month at $250K average, the extra 50 bps costs you $6,250/month or $75,000/year. But those 5 deals per month generate ~$125,000/month in loan revenue (at typical DSCR margins). The ROI is massive.

### How to Make Their First Deal PERFECT

1. **Pre-underwrite the deal before they formally submit.** Say: "Send me the scenario informally and I'll tell you if it works before you spend time packaging it."
2. **Handle everything you can.** Pull the rent comp data yourself. Pull the tax records yourself. Don't make the broker do your homework.
3. **Deliver findings in 12 hours** (not 24). Over-deliver on the first one.
4. **Call them at every milestone.** "Just wanted you to know — we issued initial findings and it looks great." "Clear to close is issued." "Docs are out." Proactive communication is the #1 way to earn trust.
5. **If anything goes sideways, call immediately.** "There's a title issue. Here's what it is, here's how we're fixing it, and here's when you'll hear back." Don't let them find out from the title company.
6. **After funding:** Send a handwritten thank-you note. Ship a bottle of wine or a gift basket. Call them: "Your first deal with us funded today. How was the experience? What could we do better?"

### How to Leverage the Lighthouse to Recruit More Brokers

1. **Ask for referrals.** "Who else do you know who brokers DSCR loans? I'd love to treat them as well as I've treated you."
2. **Ask for a testimonial.** "Would you be willing to write a brief quote about your experience with us? I'd use it in our outreach." Put it on your website, in your emails, in your conference materials.
3. **Ask them to introduce you at events.** If they're at a conference and you're there, ask them to introduce you to their broker friends. A warm introduction from a peer is worth 100 cold calls.
4. **Create a case study.** "Broker [Name] funded [X] loans with us in their first [Y] months and earned [$Z] in commissions. Here's their story." Use this in all your marketing.

### The Broker Referral Flywheel

```
Lighthouse broker has great experience
        ↓
Lighthouse tells 3-5 broker friends
        ↓
2-3 of those friends submit deals
        ↓
They have great experiences too
        ↓
Each of them tells 3-5 broker friends
        ↓
Your broker network compounds
```

**Set a referral incentive:** "If you refer a broker who funds a deal with us, we'll pay you a $500 referral bonus." This creates a financial incentive for the flywheel.

**Target:** Within 6 months of landing your lighthouse broker, you should have 15-25 active brokers through referrals alone.

## 8. Quick-Start Broker Onboarding

### The 30-Minute Onboarding Process

**Goal:** From "yes, I'll try you" to "I can submit a deal right now" in 30 minutes or less.

**Minute 0-5: Welcome & Setup**
- AE screen-shares with the broker
- Create their broker portal account (email, password, NMLS number)
- Portal auto-populates company info from NMLS database using their NMLS ID
- Upload their E&O insurance certificate (or accept it via email later — don't block on this)

**Minute 5-15: System Walkthrough**
- Show the broker the submission form: "Here's where you enter the property address, loan amount, and borrower info."
- Show the document upload area: "Here's where you drag and drop the 5 documents we need."
- Show the status tracker: "Here's where you'll see real-time updates on your file."
- Show the rate sheet: "Here's today's pricing. It updates daily at 10 AM Eastern."

**Minute 15-20: The "Concierge" Pitch**
- "One thing that makes us different: we pull the rent comp data and the tax records ourselves. You don't have to source a rent schedule or dig up tax returns. Just give us the property address and borrower authorization, and we handle the rest."
- This is a HUGE differentiator. Brokers hate chasing rent comps.

**Minute 20-25: Submit a Test Scenario**
- "Let's submit a test scenario together right now so you see how it works. Got a property in mind?"
- Walk them through a live submission. Even if it's hypothetical, going through the process once eliminates the "I'll figure it out later" barrier.

**Minute 25-30: Confirm Next Steps**
- "Here's my direct cell. Text me anytime. I respond within 2 hours."
- "Your account is active. You can submit a real deal anytime."
- "I'll send you our rate sheet and guidelines in PDF right after this call."
- "When's your next DSCR deal coming? I want to make sure I'm ready for it."

### Documents You Need vs. What You Pull Yourself

**Broker provides (5 documents):**
1. 1003 or borrower application (even a simple one)
2. Property address and purchase contract (or refinance details)
3. Borrower authorization / disclosure (to pull credit and verify info)
4. Entity documents (if borrowing through LLC — articles of organization, operating agreement)
5. Personal financial statement or schedule of real estate owned (for experience verification)

**YOU pull (the concierge service):**
1. Rent comp analysis / rent schedule (you order from a third-party service)
2. Property tax records (you pull from public records)
3. Credit report (you pull with borrower authorization)
4. Property appraisal or inspection (you order)
5. Title search (you order)
6. Insurance verification (you coordinate)

**Why this matters:** By reducing the broker's document burden from 10-12 items to 5, you make it dramatically easier to submit to you than to competitors. Every document you pull yourself is one less reason for the broker to procrastinate on submitting.

### No Minimum Volume Requirements for First 90 Days

**Standard in the industry:** Many wholesale lenders require brokers to submit 1-2 deals per month minimum or they deactivate the account.

**Your policy:** **No minimums for 90 days.** Tell brokers: "We don't have minimum volume requirements for your first 90 days. We're building this relationship for the long term. Send us one deal or ten — we'll treat you the same."

**Why:** New broker partners will test you with one deal. If you pressure them for volume before they trust you, they'll retreat. Give them space to try you at their own pace.

### Same-Day Approval Goal for First Submissions

For a broker's FIRST submission with you, set an internal goal of **same-day initial findings** (not 24 hours — same day).

This requires:
- AE flags the file as a "first submission" in the system
- Underwriting prioritizes it
- AE calls the broker with findings before EOD

**Why:** The first deal is your audition. If you deliver findings in 4 hours on the first deal, the broker is stunned. They expected 2-3 days. You've just won their attention.

## 9. Retaining Brokers After They Send One Deal

### The #1 Reason Brokers Leave After One Deal

**Slow or inconsistent communication.**

Not pricing. Not product. Not technology. Communication.

Brokers will tolerate a lot if you communicate well. They will tolerate almost nothing if you communicate poorly.

**The math:** If a broker submits a deal and hears nothing for 5 days, they assume you're like every other lender. They won't submit another deal. You've lost them before the first deal even closes.

### The "Status Update" Communication Protocol

**Proactive updates at every stage — the broker should NEVER have to chase you for a status.**

| Milestone | When | How | What to Say |
|-----------|------|-----|-------------|
| Submission received | Within 1 hour of submission | Email + text | "Got your file. I'll have initial findings by [date/time]." |
| Initial findings issued | Within 24 hours | Phone call + email | "Here's what we found. [Summary]. Here's what we need next." |
| Conditions issued | Same day as underwriting | Phone call + email | "We have [X] conditions. Let me walk you through them." |
| Conditions cleared | Same day as review | Phone call | "All conditions cleared. Moving to CTC." |
| Clear to close | Same day as issued | Phone call + email | "CTC is issued. Docs will be out [date]." |
| Docs out | Same day | Email + text | "Docs are out. Closing is scheduled for [date]." |
| Funded | Same day | Phone call | "Congratulations — your deal funded! Commission of [$X] will be paid on [date]." |

**Additional protocol:**
- **Silence rule:** If 48 hours pass with no update on an active file, the AE must proactively reach out: "No update yet — underwriting is still reviewing. I'll have news by [date/time]."
- **Bad news rule:** Any adverse action (denial, condition that may kill the deal, appraisal shortfall) must be communicated by PHONE within 2 hours of discovery. Never email a denial or major condition without calling first.

### Celebrating Their Borrower's Closing

**What to send:**
1. **Phone call from the AE** on funding day: "Your deal funded today! How did the closing go? Is your borrower happy?"
2. **Handwritten note** mailed within 2 business days: "Congratulations on closing [Borrower Name]'s loan. It was great working with you on this one. Looking forward to the next. — [Your Name]"
3. **$25 coffee gift card** with the note for their first 3 closings. Small gesture, big impact.
4. **Social media recognition** (with permission): "Congrats to [Broker Name] on closing another DSCR deal with us! Great work." Tag them on LinkedIn.

### The Monthly Check-In Call

**10 minutes, once per month. This is non-negotiable.**

**Script:**
> "Hey [First Name], it's [Your Name] with [Lender Name]. Monthly check-in — I've got 10 minutes. How's business?"
>
> *[Listen. Take notes.]* 
>
> "Any DSCR deals in the pipeline?"
>
> *[Listen.]* 
>
> "Any issues with our process? Anything we could do better?"
>
> *[Listen. This is the most important question.]* 
>
> "Great. Here's what's new with us: [1-2 sentences about new product, rate change, or company update]. And remember, my cell is always available — text me anytime."
>
> "Thanks, [First Name]. Talk next month."

**Why this works:** Most wholesale AEs only call brokers when they want something (volume, a specific deal, a conference RSVP). Calling monthly just to LISTEN — not to pitch — is extraordinary. It builds loyalty that pricing alone can't.

### The Quarterly Business Review

For brokers doing 3+ deals per month with you:

- **Schedule a 30-minute call once per quarter.**
- **Share their numbers:** "In Q2, you funded [X] deals with us for [$Y] in total volume. You earned [$Z] in commissions. Here's how you compare to other partners — you're in the top [percentile]."
- **Ask about their goals:** "What are you targeting for next quarter? How can we help you get there?"
- **Offer something:** "Since you're a top partner, I'd like to [offer: volume bonus increase, co-marketing budget, dedicated processor, etc.]."

### Red Flags That a Broker Is About to Leave

Watch for these warning signs:
1. **Submission volume drops 50%+ month over month.** They're sending deals elsewhere.
2. **They stop responding to your calls/emails.** They've already mentally moved on.
3. **They complain about a specific issue more than once.** You haven't fixed it, and they're losing patience.
4. **They ask about pricing more frequently.** They're comparing you to competitors.
5. **They mention another lender by name.** They're testing the waters.

**Response:** Call them immediately. Not email. Phone. "I've noticed [observation]. I want to make sure we're taking care of you. What's going on?" Listen. Then fix the problem.
