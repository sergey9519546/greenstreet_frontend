- Pre-2027 investments: 12/31/2026 cliff still applies
- Post-2026 investments: 5-year deferral + 10% step-up (was 7-year + 15% in TCJA)
- **QROF (rural): 30% step-up at year 5** (new tier, 50% substantial improvement threshold)
- Decennial designation cycle begins July 1, 2026 (tighter census-tract eligibility)

### 2. OBBBA made significant tax changes
- **QBI: 20% → 23%** for 2026, inflation-indexed thereafter
- **§179: $2.5M → $2,560,000** for 2026
- **100% bonus depreciation: permanent** (no 2027 sunset)
- **§163(j) ATI: EBITDA-based** (restored depreciation add-back)

### 3. Section 1071 narrowed scope reduces DSCR compliance burden
- 1,000-loan threshold (up from 100) means most small business-purpose DSCR lenders are exempt
- Captures 92-93% of small business loan volume with much less data
- 15 data points (down from 20), LGBTQI+ + application method REMOVED
- Effective June 30, 2026; compliance January 1, 2028

### 4. SR 26-2 narrowly applies to DSCR
- $30B asset threshold means most non-bank DSCR lenders NOT directly subject
- But INDIRECTLY applies via bank warehouse facilities + third-party model governance
- DSCR Sovereign OS would be a "third-party model" for any bank user

### 5. STR regulation is fragmented
- 50 states + 3,000+ municipalities have varying rules
- Minut 2026 guide covers 8 key states
