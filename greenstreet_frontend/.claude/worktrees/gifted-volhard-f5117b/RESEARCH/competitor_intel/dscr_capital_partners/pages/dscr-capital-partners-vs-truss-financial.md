# DSCR Capital Partners vs Truss Financial: 2026 Head-to-Head

📅 Updated May 2026✍️ Independent Comparison⏱ 7 min read🏦 NMLS #2591548

Truss Financial Group is a non-QM mortgage shop best known for self-employed and bank-statement borrowers. DSCR is one of their products alongside bank statement loans, asset-based loans, and conventional non-QM. They market heavily to self-employed business owners and have a national footprint.

This is an independent head-to-head between **DSCR Capital Partners** and **Truss Financial** — what each one specializes in, where pricing differs, and which scenarios favor each lender.

## The 30-Second Summary

**Truss Financial** is a non-QM generalist focused on self-employed and bank statement borrowers. Strong on owner-occupied non-QM, P&L-only loans, and asset-based lending. DSCR is competitive but not their core specialty. Limited foreign national / ITIN. No California bridge.

**DSCR Capital Partners** is a wholesale-broker DSCR shop with access to 8-12 wholesale investors. We're DSCR-specialized and shop pricing across multiple lenders per file. Better positioned on long-term DSCR pricing, foreign national, ITIN, condotel, STR, and California jumbo bridge.

## Where Truss Financial Wins

- **Self-employed and bank-statement loans.** Truss is one of the best-known names in self-employed non-QM. If you're a business owner buying or refinancing a primary residence or owner-occupied second home, they're a strong choice.
- **P&L-only loans.** CPA-prepared profit-and-loss-statement qualification (without tax returns) for self-employed borrowers. Niche product Truss does well.
- **Asset depletion programs.** High-net-worth borrowers using investment account balances to qualify rather than employment income. Truss has a polished program here.
- **One-stop non-QM shop.** If you need a DSCR for your rental AND a bank statement loan for your primary, Truss can handle both inside the same operation.

## Where DSCR Capital Partners Wins

- **DSCR-specialty pricing.** Truss's DSCR is one of many products. Our entire pipeline is DSCR. We see significantly more DSCR scenarios per month and shop pricing across 8-12 wholesale investors per file — better placement on standard files.
- **Lower FICO floor.** 620 vs Truss's 660. Material gap on sub-680 files.
- **Foreign national and ITIN.** Multiple dedicated programs on our panel vs limited / case-by-case at Truss.
- **STR / Airbnb DSCR.** AirDNA-based projections accepted across our panel. Truss is more conservative here.
- **5+ unit multifamily and condotel.** Multiple programs available; Truss is more SFR / 2-4 unit focused.
- **California jumbo bridge.** $1M-$20M CA bridge (8.5% IO, 14-21d close) is a different product Truss doesn't offer.
- **Faster close.** 21-30 days standard vs Truss's 25-40.

## Which Lender Should You Choose?

**Choose Truss Financial if:**

- You need a bank statement, P&L-only, or asset depletion loan for a primary residence
- You're self-employed and want a single lender for both DSCR rental and owner-occupied financing
- Your DSCR file is clean (660+ FICO, DSCR ≥ 1.10, SFR property) and Truss's quoted rate is competitive

**Choose DSCR Capital Partners if:**

- You're focused on a DSCR investment property loan and want multi-lender shopping
- Your file has any complexity — sub-660 FICO, sub-1.0 DSCR, STR, condotel, 5+ unit, foreign national, or ITIN
- You're closing a California $1M+ jumbo file (DSCR or bridge)
- You want a 21-30 day close (vs Truss's 25-40)
- You want a single loan officer for the entire file

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Truss Financial a good DSCR lender?**

Truss is a credible non-QM lender particularly strong on self-employed primary residence loans. For DSCR investment properties, multi-lender shopping typically uncovers better pricing because Truss's DSCR is one of several products rather than their specialty.

**How do Truss DSCR rates compare to DSCR Capital Partners?**

Truss's 2026 DSCR rates run roughly 7.25-8.50%. DSCR Capital Partners typically prices in the 6.50-7.75% range. The gap reflects our multi-lender shopping model vs single-program quotes.

**Does Truss do foreign national DSCR loans?**

Truss's foreign national program is limited and case-by-case. ITIN borrower options are similarly limited. We close both types regularly through dedicated programs across our wholesale panel.

**What is Truss's minimum FICO for DSCR?**

Truss's DSCR floor is typically 660 in 2026. Below that, our panel goes to 620 on multiple programs.

**Can I use Truss for my primary and DSCR Capital Partners for rentals?**

Yes — this is a perfectly reasonable split. Truss's strength is owner-occupied non-QM (bank statement, P&L, asset depletion). Our strength is investment property DSCR. Many investors use a different lender for each side.
