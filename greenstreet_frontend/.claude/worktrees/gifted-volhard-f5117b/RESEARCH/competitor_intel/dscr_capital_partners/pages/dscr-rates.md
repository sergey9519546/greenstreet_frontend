## Rates by State (Where State Matters)

Most DSCR lenders price uniformly across all 50 states. A handful of states have state-specific overlays. The state spread is usually under 0.125%, but worth noting on jumbo files.

- **Texas:** Some programs require 1.20+ DSCR (vs 1.00 elsewhere). State-specific cash-out rules apply.
- **California:** Jumbo files ($1M+) face extra appraisal review. Bridge programs are state-specific. [CA guide →](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/blog/dscr-loans-california-2026/)
- **Florida:** Condo/condotel scrutiny is higher post-Surfside. Hurricane-zone insurance verification required. [FL guide →](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/blog/dscr-loans-florida-2026/)
- **New York:** Mortgage tax adds material cost; some programs avoid NY entirely.
- **Other 46 states:** Same baseline pricing across all DSCR programs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are DSCR rates in 2026?**

6.50–9.50% range across all scenarios. Standard purchase: 7.00–8.00%. Cash-out: 7.25–8.75%. Foreign national: 7.75–9.50%. Condotel/multifamily: 0.25–0.75% premium over SFR baseline.

**Are DSCR rates the same for purchase and refinance?**

Purchase and rate/term refi price similarly. Cash-out refis run 0.25–0.50% higher. Gap widens above 70% LTV cash-out.

**Do DSCR rates differ by state?**

Mostly no. Texas, California, Florida, and New York have minor overlays. State spread typically under 0.125%.

**Why are foreign national DSCR rates higher?**

Foreign national borrowers lack US credit profile and face additional underwriting complexity. Lenders price in 0.75–1.50% premium.

**Can I lock my DSCR rate during the application?**

Yes. Lock periods run 30–45 days standard. Extensions cost 0.125–0.25% in additional points. Lock once you have the property under contract.
