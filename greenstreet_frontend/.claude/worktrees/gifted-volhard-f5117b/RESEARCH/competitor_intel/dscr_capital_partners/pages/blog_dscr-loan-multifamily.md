# DSCR Loans for Multifamily: 2–4 Units, 5+ Units & Apartment Buildings

DSCR loans aren’t just for single-family rentals — they’re one of the cleanest ways to finance **multifamily property**, from a duplex to a 5+ unit apartment building. You qualify on the building’s **rent roll**, not your personal income, so scaling into multifamily doesn’t require tax returns or DTI.

## 2–4 Unit vs. 5+ Unit Multifamily

There are two lanes, and the line falls at five units:

- **2–4 units (residential):** duplexes, triplexes, and [quadplexes](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/blog/dscr-loan-for-quadplex/) are residential DSCR loans — the most common, with the best pricing and up to ~80% LTV.
- **5+ units (commercial-residential):** small apartment buildings qualify on the property’s net rental income. Still no personal income docs, but expect slightly tighter LTV, larger reserves, and DSCR measured on the whole rent roll.

## How Multifamily DSCR Works

The lender totals the building’s gross rents (actual leases or market rent), subtracts the full payment (PITIA), and checks the ratio. A building renting for $9,000/month with a $7,000 payment has a 1.29 DSCR. Strong, stabilized rent rolls price best; value-add buildings with vacancy may need a [no-ratio](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/blog/no-ratio-dscr-loan/) structure or a bigger down payment.

## What You Can Finance

- Duplex, triplex, quadplex (2–4 unit)
- 5–10 unit apartment buildings
- 10+ unit and small apartment complexes
- Mixed-use (residential over retail) on a case-by-case basis
- [Co-living and rent-by-the-room](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/blog/dscr-loan-co-living-property/) properties

## Multifamily DSCR Requirements

### Typical Guidelines

- **Credit:** 660+ preferred for 5+ unit; 620+ on 2–4 unit
- **LTV:** up to ~80% (2–4 unit), ~70–75% (5+ unit)
- **DSCR:** 1.0–1.25 target; no-ratio available
- **Reserves:** 6+ months PITIA common on larger buildings
- **Vesting:** LLC standard

## Frequently Asked Questions

Can you get a DSCR loan on 5+ units? +

Yes. DSCR loans finance 5+ unit apartment buildings by qualifying on the building’s rent roll instead of your personal income. LTV is usually a bit lower than 2-4 unit and reserves are larger, but no tax returns are required.

Can I use a DSCR loan for an apartment building? +

Yes — small apartment buildings (5–10 units and up) are eligible. The lender underwrites the property’s net rental income and the DSCR across all units; financing closes in an LLC.

What are the DSCR requirements for multifamily? +

Generally 620+ credit for 2-4 unit and 660+ for 5+ unit, up to ~80% LTV (lower for 5+), a 1.0–1.25 DSCR target (no-ratio available), and 3–6+ months of reserves.

Is mixed-use property eligible for a DSCR loan? +

Often yes, case by case — typically residential-over-retail where the residential portion drives the income. The mix of residential vs. commercial square footage affects eligibility and LTV.
