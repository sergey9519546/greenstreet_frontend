## 1. Claim Statement

> Non-QM mortgage originations totaled **$239.3 billion** across **697,605 loans** in calendar year **2025**, representing approximately **10% of total U.S. mortgage originations**.

**Year disambiguation:** Calendar year 2025 (January 1 – December 31, 2025), sourced from HMDA 2025 data published by CFPB in mid-March 2026. NOT calendar year 2024 (which was $182B / 9% per Polygon).

## 2. Source 1 (Primary — Methodology-Transparent)

**Polygon Research — "Non-QM Market Data"** (updated April 19, 2026)
- URL: https://www.polygonresearch.com/non-qm-market
- Data vintage: **2025 HMDA**
- Exact figures stated: **$239 billion**, **697,605 loans**, **10%** of market
- "Polygon Research calculates that Non-QM loans represented 10.2% of total U.S. mortgage originations in 2025 by loan count and 10% by dollar volume, totaling over $239 billion across 697,605 loans."
- Methodology: Each HMDA loan record 2018–2025 evaluated against year-specific Ability-to-Repay and Qualified Mortgage standards from 12 CFR §1026.43. Classification uses observable loan characteristics: pricing thresholds, product features, fee structures, purchaser type. Public whitepaper available.
- **Why primary:** Polygon is the only public source with documented loan-level methodology applied to ALL funded HMDA loans (not securitization data, not lender-name heuristics, not sampled locks).

**Polygon Research Blog — "How to Analyze the Non-QM Market with Loan-Level Data"** (corroborates)
- URL: https://www.polygonresearch.com/blog/how-to-analyze-the-non-qm-market-with-loan-level-data
- Exact phrasing: **"Non-QM loans in 2025, representing $239.3 billion in origination volume."**
- This is the source that yields the **precise $239.3B** figure in the corpus (not the rounded "$239B" on the market page).

## 4. Source 3 (Independent — Industry Trade Press)

**iEmergent — "2025 HMDA Data Findings Part 1"** (Laird Nossuli, posted April 27, 2026)
- URL: https://www.iemergent.com/insights/2025-hmda-data-part-1
- **Independent HMDA analysis (third-party analytics firm).** Total 2025 U.S. mortgage originations: **6.75 million loans / $2.12 trillion volume**.
- **Cross-check arithmetic:** 10% of $2.12T = ~$212B; 10.2% of 6.75M = ~689K loans. Both figures consistent with Polygon's $239B / 697,605 (within reasonable variance from Polygon's full-HMDA-coverage vs. iEmergent's totals).
- **Implication:** Polygon's $239B / 697,605 is the only figure in the public market that explicitly uses Regulation-Z QM/ATR classification on complete 2025 HMDA. iEmergent corroborates the denominator ($2.12T / 6.75M) is correct.

## 11. Refinements and Caveats

1. **Methodology variance matters.** Polygon's $239B uses full HMDA + QM/ATR classification (includes portfolio loans, excludes no lender). Optimal Blue's lock-based "9% in December 2025" implies full-year non-QM ≈ $190B (lower). The $239B figure is the more inclusive market definition; both methodologies are defensible.

2. **The $239B figure rounds up from $239.3B** as stated in Polygon's secondary blog post — preserving both precision ($239.3B) and headline rounding ($239B) is acceptable.

3. **10% market share** is properly stated as "share of total U.S. mortgage originations." MBA's 2026 forecast of $2.2T single-family / iEmergent's $2.12T all-residential 2025 are both consistent with this share.

4. **Future-proofing:** Q1 2026 Optimal Blue data shows non-QM continuing at 8–9% lock share (https://nationalmortgageprofessional.com/news/non-qm-share-climbs-9-conforming-market-shrinks, June 10, 2026). Trajectory supports the 10% full-year 2025 figure as a stable, not anomalous, peak.
