## Production notes

**Pacing:** Average 163 wpm. Slightly slower on the HOOK and OUTCOME (more weight), slightly faster on EXPLAINERS 4–6 (factual, lean).

**Breaths:** Implicit at every period. No need to write `[breathe]` — Trustworthy Man voice breathes at sentence ends naturally.

**No filler words:** No "um," no "you know," no "right?" — those would undermine the authority tone.

**No marketing tells:** Avoided "leverage," "robust," "unlock," "seamless," "game-changing," "elevate," "transform your workflow," "in today's market." If you find yourself adding any of these, delete them and rewrite the line in plain English.

**The single self-aware line** ("Not 'sixty seconds' the way vendors say it") is the only wink in the script. One is fine. Two would be cute. Zero would feel like every other DSCR ad.
