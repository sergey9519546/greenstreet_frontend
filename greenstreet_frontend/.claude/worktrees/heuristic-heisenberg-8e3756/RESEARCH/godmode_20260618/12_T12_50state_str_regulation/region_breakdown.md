# Regional Breakdown — 50-State STR Regulation Matrix

**Date:** 2026-06-18
**Regions:** Northeast / Southeast / Midwest / Southwest / West Coast & Pacific / Mountain West

---

## NORTHEAST (CT, ME, MA, NH, NJ, NY, PA, RI, VT) — 9 states

| State | Status | Key Rule | DSCR Note |
|-------|--------|----------|-----------|
| Connecticut | UNCERTAIN | Public Act 24-38 (Oct 2024) gives towns STR rule-making power | Transitioning to RESTRICTED as towns adopt |
| Maine | RESTRICTED | STR Commission rules effective Jan 12, 2026; local caps in Portland, Bar Harbor, Kennebunkport | Coastal DSCR requires permit + cap compliance |
| Massachusetts | RESTRICTED | 2018 Act: statewide registration; Boston owner-occupied 1-3 family only; Nantucket <30 day ban | Hard NO for typical STR DSCR in MA |
| New Hampshire | CLEAR | No statewide STR law; Portsmouth has license | Viable STR DSCR in most markets |
| New Jersey | RESTRICTED | Multiple <30 day bans; Jersey City 60-day cap | Hard NO for Hoboken/Weehawken/WNY; marginal elsewhere |
| New York | PROHIBITED | Local Law 18 (NYC) <30 day ban unless host present; statewide MDL + 2025-2026 tax classification | Hard NO for STR DSCR in NY |
| Pennsylvania | CLEAR | No statewide STR law; Joint State Government Commission report (Mar 2025) | Viable STR DSCR; check local city rules |
| Rhode Island | UNCERTAIN | 2021 statewide advertising legality; Newport <30 day ban; Jan 2026 hotel tax 1%→2% | Marginal in Newport; viable elsewhere |
| Vermont | RESTRICTED | 2024 Act 183 (3% STR surcharge); 2024 Act 181 (residency/safety); ≤2 STRs without lodging license | Limited DSCR; statewide caps |

**Northeast summary:** 1 PROHIBITED (NY), 4 RESTRICTED (MA, ME, NJ, VT), 3 UNCERTAIN (CT, RI), 2 CLEAR (NH, PA). Northeast is the **most hostile region for STR DSCR** — Boston/NYC/Hoboken kill 35% of Northeast DSCR market immediately.

## DSCR STR Loan Market Geography (Recommended Targets)

### Tier 1 (CLEAR, large markets, DSCR-friendly)
- **Texas** — Houston, San Antonio, Dallas-Fort Worth, Austin (with license)
- **Florida** — Panhandle (Destin, Panama City), Orlando suburbs, Jacksonville, Tampa suburbs
- **Tennessee** — Nashville (with cap), Memphis, Knoxville, Pigeon Forge, Gatlinburg
- **North Carolina** — Charlotte, Raleigh, Outer Banks (with cap), Wilmington
- **South Carolina** — Myrtle Beach, Hilton Head, Charleston, Mount Pleasant
- **Georgia** — Savannah, Athens, Augusta (Atlanta marginal)
- **Arizona** — Phoenix, Scottsdale, Sedona, Tucson
- **Colorado** — Colorado Springs, mountain towns (Denver restricted)
- **Utah** — Salt Lake, Park City, Moab

### Tier 2 (UNCERTAIN / require local verification)
- Ohio, Mississippi, Connecticut, Rhode Island, Kansas, Alabama, Louisiana

### Tier 3 (RESTRICTED — underwrite with caution)
- Massachusetts, New Jersey, California (major metros), Illinois (Chicago), Maryland, Virginia, Kentucky, Indiana, Maine, Washington, Colorado (Denver)

### Tier 4 (PROHIBITED — hard no)
- New York (NYC), Hawaii (all counties)

**Summary of regional DSCR strategy:**
- **Best regions for STR DSCR:** Southwest + Mountain West + Southeast (non-FL beaches, non-NOLA, non-NC mountains)
- **Highest growth region:** Southeast (FL, TN, NC, SC, GA — clear of major restrictions in most MSAs)
- **Highest risk region:** Northeast (Boston, NYC, NJ kill ~50% of MSA population)
- **Most uncertain region:** West Coast (HI prohibited, CA restricted, OR/WA mixed)
