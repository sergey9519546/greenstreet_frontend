## Why Cleveland Investors Cash Out

Cleveland cash-out borrowers pull equity from rehabbed or appreciated doors and redeploy into more. DSCRs are very strong at these prices, so refinances clear the ratio easily. The mechanics mirror our universal [Cash-Out Refinance DSCR program](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/blog/dscr-cash-out-refinance/): up to 75% of appraised value, the existing loan is paid off, and the rest comes back to you — tax-free\* loan proceeds. \*Consult your tax advisor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Why are Cleveland DSCRs among the best in the country? +

Very low prices against solid rents push rent-to-price ratios high, so the income easily covers the payment — meaning strong DSCRs and ample borrowable equity at 75% LTV.

What are point-of-sale inspections and do they affect my cash-out? +

Several Cleveland-area suburbs require a city inspection at transfer/sale and sometimes refinance. They can add repair conditions and time — we flag the local rule up front so it doesn’t derail the close.

Can I cash out a Cleveland BRRRR? +

Yes — Cleveland is a core BRRRR market. After rehab and lease-up (and any seasoning), we refinance on the new value and market rent. See our [Cleveland BRRRR guide](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/blog/dscr-brrrr-cleveland/).
