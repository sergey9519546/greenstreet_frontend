## STEP 1: LEAD → PRE-QUALIFIED

**Industry conversion: 30–40% → Target: 50%**

### THE SPEED RULE

A lead contacted within 5 minutes is **9x more likely to convert** than one contacted at 30 minutes. This is not theory — it's verified across every lending vertical. In DSCR, the stakes are even higher because investors are shopping multiple lenders simultaneously. The first lender to give them a number wins 60% of the time.

**Implementation:**

| Time Since Lead | Likelihood of Contact | Likelihood of Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 minutes | 85% | 50%+ |
| 5–15 minutes | 60% | 30% |
| 15–30 minutes | 35% | 18% |
| 30–60 minutes | 15% | 8% |
| 1–4 hours | 8% | 4% |
| 4–24 hours | 3% | 1% |
| 24+ hours | 1% | 0.5% |

**Rule:** Every lead gets a call within 5 minutes. No exceptions. If the assigned LO is on another call, a backup LO dials. If no LO is available, the call center dials. A human voice within 5 minutes is non-negotiable.

### THE INSTANT PRE-QUALIFICATION

On the first call, your goal is simple: **give them a number.** Not "we'll get back to you." Not "let me check with underwriting." A real, defensible rate and loan amount estimate based on what they tell you right now.

**First Call Script — Exact Questions (in order):**

```
1. "I see you're looking at a DSCR loan — tell me about the property.
   What's the address or at least the ZIP code?"
   → [Enter into pricing engine for instant rent/tax estimates]

2. "Is this a purchase or a refinance?"
   → [Determines LTV cap: 75–80% purchase, 70–75% refi]

3. "What's the property worth, or if it's a purchase, what's the
   contract price?"
   → [Locks loan amount range]

4. "What are the current rents, or if it's not rented yet, what do
   you expect to get monthly?"
   → [Determines DSCR — the entire product]

5. "What loan amount are you looking for, or do you want us to
   calculate the maximum?"
   → [Most investors want maximum. Say "Let me show you the max."]

6. "How's your credit? Roughly — 740+, 700–740, 680–700, or below
   680?"
   → [Determines rate tier and pricing add-ons]

7. "Is the property in an LLC or your personal name?"
   → [LLC is standard for DSCR; personal name is fine but worth noting]

8. "How many properties do you currently own?"
   → [Portfolio investor = higher value, different treatment]
```

**After these 8 questions (takes ~3 minutes), you deliver the hook:**

> *"Based on what you've told me, here's where you stand: On a [purchase/refi] of that property, you're looking at approximately a **[RATE]% rate** with **[POINTS] points** origination, and you'd qualify for a loan up to **$[AMOUNT]**. That gives you a DSCR of about **[1.2X–1.4X]**, which comfortably clears our minimum. Want me to send you this in writing so you can compare?"*

**Say yes to that question.** Then immediately send the email below.

| Reason | % of Lost Leads | Prevention |

If you don't reach them on the first call, execute this cadence without fail:

```
HOUR 2:               Call #3 — ring 3x, leave brief voicemail

                       I have your DSCR rate ready. Can I send it over?"

                       but your pre-qualification window is closing.
                       Reply YES if you'd still like to move forward."
```

**After 48 hours with no response:** Move to nurture sequence (1 touch per week for 4 weeks, then monthly).

## STEP 2: PRE-QUALIFIED → APPLICATION

**Industry conversion: 40–50% → Target: 60%**

### THE #1 KILLER: FRICTION

The typical DSCR application asks 40–60 questions and requires 10–15 document uploads. Most investors are looking at this on their phone at 9 PM after a full day. They abandon.

**Your application must take under 10 minutes.** Here's how:

### FRICTION REDUCTION CHECKLIST

| Friction Point | Solution | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Re-entering info you already have | Pre-fill EVERYTHING from the first call | Cuts 15+ fields |
| Property data lookup | Auto-pull from county records: tax amount, lot size, year built, sq ft | Cuts 8 fields |
| Rent estimation | Auto-pull from RentCast/Zillow Rent Zestimate as pre-fill; borrower confirms | Cuts 2 fields |
| Insurance estimate | Auto-estimate based on property value, location, and coverage type | Cuts 3 fields |
| Typing on mobile | Use toggle selectors, dropdowns, and auto-complete — zero free-text fields except name and entity | 3x faster |
| Document upload intimidation | Don't ask for documents IN the application. Ask after. | Cuts abandonment 40% |
| Credit pull anxiety | Soft pull only at application. Hard pull only at commitment. State this explicitly. | Cuts abandonment 25% |

### THE "APPLICATION APPOINTMENT" TECHNIQUE

The highest-conversion approach: **schedule a specific time to complete the application together over the phone.**

**Script:**

> *"I know applications are a pain. Here's what I'd suggest — let's set up a 15-minute call where I fill out the application FOR you. You just answer a few questions, and I handle the typing. I can do tomorrow at 10 AM or 2 PM — which works better?"*

This technique converts at **70–80%** of scheduled appointments vs. 30–40% of self-serve applications. The reason is simple: you've eliminated all friction and created a specific commitment.

**Appointment confirmation text (sent immediately):**

```
Confirmed: Your DSCR application call with [LO Name]
📅 [Day], [Date] at [Time]
📞 [LO] will call you at [Phone Number]
⏱ Takes about 15 minutes
📋 Have handy: property address, entity name (if LLC), and
   approximate credit score

Reply CANCEL to reschedule.
```

### THE SOFT PULL VS. HARD PULL CONVERSATION

Many investors are terrified of hard credit pulls. They've been told by their financial advisor (or a Facebook group) that inquiries kill their score. Address this directly:

**Script:**

> *"Two things about credit: First, at application, we only do a soft pull — it has zero impact on your score. We don't do a hard pull until you've reviewed your formal approval and said 'let's do this.' Second, even the hard pull typically drops your score by 2–5 points, and it bounces back in 60 days. If you're shopping multiple lenders, all mortgage inquiries within 45 days count as one event. So you're protected."*

### DOCUMENT REQUEST STRATEGY: NEVER ASK FOR EVERYTHING AT ONCE

The worst thing you can do: send a 15-item document checklist on day one. The borrower looks at it, gets overwhelmed, and ghosts.

**The phased approach:**

| Phase | When | What You Request | How You Frame It |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1** — With application | Day 0 | Purchase contract OR current mortgage statement; property insurance declaration page | "Just two things to get started" |
| **Phase 2** — Post-application | Day 1 | Entity documents (LLC operating agreement, articles of organization); personal ID (driver's license) | "A few quick items to keep things moving" |
| **Phase 3** — Post-conditional approval | Day 3–5 | Property rent roll or lease; bank statement showing reserves; any property-specific items (HOA, flood cert) | "Here's exactly what we still need — only [X] items left" |

Each phase should feel small and achievable. **Never send a document request with more than 5 items.** If you need 8 items, split it into two requests.

## STEP 3: APPLICATION → APPROVED

**Industry conversion: 70–80% → Target: 85%**

### THE #1 KILLER: MISSING DOCUMENTS

Applications stall because borrowers don't send documents. This is not a borrower problem — it's a **process problem.** Your job is to make document submission so easy that it's harder to ignore than to comply.

### THE DOCUMENT COLLECTION PLAYBOOK — DAY BY DAY

**Day 0 (Application Day):**
- Application submitted → Auto-send document checklist (Phase 1 only — 2–3 items)
- LO calls within 1 hour: *"Great news — your application is in. I just need [2 items] to get underwriting started. Can you snap a photo of those right now?"*
- If borrower provides documents immediately → fast-track to conditional approval

**Day 1:**
- Email: *"We received [items]. Still waiting on [remaining items]. Here's exactly what I need: [specific list with descriptions]."*
- Include screenshots or examples of what each document looks like
- Offer: *"If it's easier, text me photos of these documents at [LO cell]."*

**Day 2:**
- Call: *"Hey [Name], checking on [missing items]. Do you have any questions about what I need?"*
- Common issue: Borrower doesn't know what an "operating agreement" is → explain in plain English
- Alternative: *"Can I pull your entity info from the state filing database? That saves you a step."*

**Day 3:**
- Final follow-up on critical missing items
- Email: *"Your file is on hold pending [items]. Once I have these, we can issue your conditional approval — usually within 24 hours. Want to get this done today?"*

**Day 4:**
- Escalate: LO calls directly
- Script: *"I want to make sure we don't lose your rate lock window. We need [items] to move forward. Can you commit to getting these to me by [specific day]?"*
- Get a specific commitment date. Write it down. Follow up on that date.

**Day 5+:**
- "We'll get it for you" approach:
  - Entity documents → Pull from Secretary of State database
  - Insurance → Call the borrower's insurance agent directly (with authorization)
  - Tax records → Pull from county assessor API
  - Rent data → Pull from RentCast/Zillow
  - Mortgage statement → Borrower's online portal screenshot is sufficient

### THE CONDITIONAL APPROVAL FAST-TRACK

**The most powerful conversion tool you have:** Issue conditional approval within **48 hours** of application, even if some documents are still outstanding. Here's why:

1. **It creates momentum.** The borrower feels like the loan is happening.
2. **It creates commitment.** They've invested time; they're not starting over with another lender.
3. **It identifies exactly what's still needed.** No ambiguity.
4. **It's rare.** Most DSCR lenders take 5–10 business days for initial findings. You do it in 2. That's your competitive advantage.

### MANAGING THE APPRAISAL (THE BIGGEST DELAY SOURCE)

Appraisals take 5–14 days and are the #1 source of timeline blowups. Here's how to manage them:

1. **Order the appraisal on Day 1** — don't wait for all documents. Order it as soon as the application is in and the borrower has authorized it.
2. **Use AMC with turnaround SLAs** — contract with appraisal management companies that guarantee 7-day turnaround for DSCR properties.
3. **AVM as backup** — for refinances under $1M with strong DSCR (≥1.4), offer an AVM (automated valuation model) in lieu of a full appraisal. Saves 7–10 days and $400–$600.
4. **Proactive appraisal management** — on Day 5, call the appraiser (or AMC). On Day 7, call again. On Day 10, escalate. Never let an appraisal sit idle.
5. **Prepare the borrower for the appraisal value conversation** — if the property might not appraise, have that conversation BEFORE the appraisal comes back. Script: *"Based on comparable sales I'm seeing, the value looks solid. But if the appraisal comes in lower than expected, we have options — we can adjust the loan amount, you can bring cash to close, or in some cases we can dispute the appraisal. Let's cross that bridge if we come to it."*

### THE "24-HOUR INITIAL FINDINGS" PROMISE

Your competitive differentiator: **"Submit your application today, and you'll have initial findings within 24 hours — guaranteed."**

This is rare in DSCR. Most lenders take 3–5 business days. By promising (and delivering) 24-hour initial findings, you:

- Create urgency for the borrower to submit their application
- Build trust by keeping your word
- Lock in the borrower before they shop further
- Identify deal-breaking issues early (saving everyone time)

**How to deliver on this promise:**
- Underwriter reviews new files first thing every morning
- Auto-underwriting engine handles the DSCR calculation, LTV check, and credit review
- Human underwriter adds property-type eligibility, condition list, and sign-off
- Total time from submission to initial findings: 4–18 hours

## STEP 4: APPROVED → CLOSED

**Industry conversion: 80–90% → Target: 95%**

### THE #1 KILLER: LAST-MINUTE SURPRISES

The approved loan that doesn't close almost always dies because of a surprise — a new condition, a cost increase, a timeline delay that the borrower didn't expect. These are preventable.

### THE "NO SURPRISES" PROTOCOL

**Rule 1: No new conditions after conditional approval.** Once you've issued conditional approval, the ONLY conditions that can be added are ones directly related to satisfying existing conditions (e.g., the appraisal comes in low, so you need to adjust the loan amount). You cannot add new document requirements, new credit conditions, or new property requirements. If something surfaces that wasn't on the conditional approval, **you eat it.** This policy forces thorough initial review and eliminates the #1 source of borrower frustration.

**Rule 2: Closing costs match the initial estimate.** If your Loan Estimate said $X in closing costs, your Closing Disclosure cannot exceed $X + 10% (and even that should be rare). Build a buffer into your initial estimate so you're never over. If costs come in lower, great — the borrower is pleasantly surprised. If you underestimated, you absorb the difference. This is the "closing guarantee."

**Rule 3: Proactive communication.** The borrower should never have to ask "what's happening with my loan?" They should receive updates before they think to ask.

### CLOSING TIMELINE MANAGEMENT

**Target: 21 days from application to close.** Here's the timeline:

```
DAY 0:   Application submitted
DAY 0:   Appraisal ordered; title ordered; insurance requested
DAY 1:   Document collection (Phase 2)
DAY 2:   Conditional approval issued (48-hour promise)
DAY 3:   Remaining conditions identified
DAY 5:   Documents due from borrower
DAY 7:   Appraisal expected back (7-day AMC SLA)
DAY 8:   Appraisal review; clear property condition
DAY 10:  All conditions cleared (target)
DAY 11:  Clear-to-close issued
DAY 12:  Closing Disclosure prepared and sent
DAY 15:  CD review period ends (3 business days)
DAY 16:  Closing / signing
DAY 17:  Funding
DAY 18:  Recording
```

| Day | Email | Subject Line | Content |
|---|---|---|---|

| Day 3 | #2 | "Conditional approval ✅ — [X] steps to close" | Celebrate the milestone, list remaining items |

### COORDINATING WITH TITLE, INSURANCE, AND BORROWER

The three-party coordination (title company, insurance agent, borrower) is where loans die. Your LO or processor must own this coordination — do not leave it to the borrower.

**Title coordination:**
- Order title on Day 0 with your preferred title company
- Follow up on Day 5 to confirm title commitment receipt
- Review title commitment within 24 hours — flag any issues (liens, judgments, easements)
- Clear title exceptions by Day 10

**Insurance coordination:**
- Request insurance binder from borrower's agent on Day 1
- If borrower doesn't have an agent, provide 2–3 referrals
- Follow up on Day 5 if binder not received
- Review binder for correct coverage amounts and lender clause
- Clear insurance condition by Day 8

**Borrower coordination:**
- Send wire instructions on Day 10 (allow 5 business days for wire)
- Confirm closing location and time by Day 12
- Remind borrower to bring government-issued ID
- Confirm cash-to-close amount (no surprises)

## STEP 5: THE "STALL" RECOVERY PLAYBOOK

**When a borrower goes silent, here's what's happening and what to do about it.**

### WHY BORROWERS STALL

| Reason | Signal | % of Stalls |
|---|---|---|
| **Shopping your rate** | They're comparing; won't commit | 35% |
| **Cold feet / fear** | "Let me think about it" | 20% |
| **Life event** | Job change, family issue, health | 15% |
| **Deal fell through** | Property issue, seller backed out | 15% |
| **Overwhelmed by process** | Too many documents, too much work | 10% |
| **Found better deal** | Another lender offered lower rate | 5% |

### THE "TEMPERATURE CHECK" CALL — EXACT SCRIPT

When a borrower hasn't responded in 48+ hours:

> *"Hey [Name], it's [LO] from [Lender]. I wanted to check in — I know things can get busy, and I want to make sure I'm not dropping the ball on my end. Are you still looking at moving forward with this loan, or has something changed? No pressure either way — I just want to make sure we're on the same page."*

**If they say "still thinking about it":**

> *"Totally understand. What's the main thing you're weighing? Is it the rate, the terms, or just timing? I ask because if it's the rate, I might have some flexibility. If it's timing, I can adjust the closing date. And if you're comparing other offers — honestly, I'd love to see what you're looking at. Sometimes I can beat them, and if I can't, I'll tell you straight up."*

**If they say "found a better rate":**

> *"That's great — I mean it. Can I ask what they quoted you? Not to pressure you, but if you can share the rate and points, I'll tell you honestly if I can match or beat it. If they're genuinely cheaper, I'll say so. But I've seen a lot of 'low rate' quotes that have hidden fees or prepay penalties that make them more expensive over 3 years. I'd hate for you to get surprised at the closing table."*

### THE "RATE HOLD" OFFER

> *"Here's what I can do: I'll hold this rate for 7 days. No commitment from you. If you find something better, great. If not, your rate is locked in at [RATE]%. Either way, you win. Fair?"*

This works because:
- It removes pressure (they don't have to commit)
- It creates a deadline (7 days)
- It signals confidence (you believe your rate is competitive)
- It costs you nothing (rates don't move much in 7 days)

### THE "COMPARISON GUARANTEE"

> *"Send me any other quote you have — rate, points, closing costs, prepay penalty — and I'll do a side-by-side comparison. Total cost over 3 years. If I can beat it, I will. If I can't, I'll tell you to go with them. Deal?"*

This is devastating because most borrowers have never done a true total-cost comparison. When they see that a 7.25% rate with 2.5 points costs more than your 7.5% rate with 1.0 point over 3 years, the decision is obvious. And you never had to say "we're cheaper" — the math did the work.

### THE "WALK AWAY GRACEFULLY" MOVE

Sometimes the best conversion tactic is to stop selling:

> *"I've been in your shoes, and I know when it's not the right time. No hard feelings at all. I'm going to close out your file for now, but I'll check in on [DATE — 30 days out] to see if anything's changed. If you ever need anything in the meantime, my number's the same."*

This works paradoxically because:
- It removes sales pressure (which was probably causing the stall)
- It demonstrates confidence and respect (which builds trust)
- It leaves the door open (30-day follow-up)
- Many borrowers come back because the lender who respected their space earned their trust

## STEP 7: BROKER-SPECIFIC CONVERSION TACTICS

**Brokers are different from direct borrowers. They submit leads differently, they evaluate lenders differently, and they convert differently.**

### THE BROKER UPDATE SLA

| Event | Your Response Time | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| New submission | Acknowledge within **2 hours** | Brokers judge lenders by first-response speed |
| Initial findings | Deliver within **24 hours** | This is your #1 differentiator vs. 3–5 day competitors |
| Condition updates | Same business day | Brokers need to relay info to borrowers |
| Status inquiry | Within **1 hour** | Slow responses = broker moves deals elsewhere |
| Problem/dispute | Within **30 minutes** | Problems are when trust is won or lost |

**If you can't deliver full findings in 24 hours, deliver a preliminary response:**

> *"We've received the file and done an initial review. The deal looks viable — DSCR pencils at 1.3x based on submitted rents. Full conditions by tomorrow EOD. One potential item: [flag any concern early]."*

### THE BROKER DASHBOARD

Build a real-time portal where brokers can see the status of every deal:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  BROKER DASHBOARD — [Broker Name]                           │
├──────────┬──────────┬─────────────┬───────────┬────────────┤
│ Property │ Status   │ Next Step   │ Est Close │ Last Update│
├──────────┼──────────┼─────────────┼───────────┼────────────┤
│ 123 Main │ In U/W   │ Appraisal   │ Mar 21    │ 2hr ago    │
│ 456 Oak  │ Cond Appr│ Insurance   │ Mar 25    │ 1d ago     │
│ 789 Elm  │ Submitted│ Doc Review  │ TBD       │ 3d ago     │
│ 321 Pine │ Closed   │ —           ✓ Mar 10   │ —          │
└──────────┴──────────┴─────────────┴───────────┴────────────┘

Pipeline: 4 active | Funded YTD: 7 | Commission YTD: $24,500
```

**Why this matters:** Brokers hate calling lenders for updates. It wastes their time and makes them feel out of control. A dashboard gives them control and visibility — and it keeps them from calling your competitor to check on a deal they submitted there instead.

### THE "DEAL SAVER" — TAKING OVER DYING DEALS

Brokers often have deals stuck at other lenders — bogged down in conditions, surprise requirements, or slow underwriting. This is your opportunity.

**Script (to broker):**

> *"I hear [Lender X] is dragging on your deal. Here's what I'll do: submit it to us, and I'll have initial findings within 24 hours. If we can do the deal, we'll close it in 21 days from today. If we can't, I'll tell you within 24 hours so you're not waiting around. Either way, you're not losing any more time."*

**Deal saver conversion rate:** 40–50% of deals you take over from other lenders will close with you. The broker is already motivated — they just need someone who can execute.

### HOW TO BE THE BROKER'S FAVORITE LENDER

| Behavior | Impact | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Return every call the same day | Trust | Daily |
| Deliver initial findings in 24 hours | Differentiation | Every deal |
| Never add surprise conditions | Loyalty | Every deal |
| Proactively solve problems (don't just report them) | Advocacy | As needed |
| Pay commissions within 48 hours of funding | Retention | Every deal |
| Send deal status updates without being asked | Efficiency | 2x per week |
| Say "no" fast when a deal doesn't work | Respect | As needed |

**The last point is critical.** Brokers respect a lender who says "This deal doesn't work for us because [specific reason]" within 24 hours more than a lender who strings them along for 3 weeks. A fast "no" lets the broker move on. A slow "maybe" wastes their time and destroys trust.

## STEP 9: THE "CLOSE RATE" CALCULATOR

### THE MODEL

```
INPUTS:
  Leads per month:          [L]
  Lead → Pre-qual rate:     [A]%
  Pre-qual → App rate:      [B]%
  App → Approved rate:      [C]%
  Approved → Closed rate:   [D]%

CALCULATION:
  Pre-qualified = L × A%
  Applications  = L × A% × B%
  Approved      = L × A% × B% × C%
```
