## The Miami Catch: Rules Change by Municipality

"Miami" isn't one rulebook — STR legality depends on the exact city the property sits in, and the differences are large:

- **City of Miami** — short-term rentals generally require a Business Tax Receipt, a Certificate of Use, and state/county vacation-rental registration; allowed uses vary by zoning district.
- **Miami Beach** — among the most restrictive in the country, with short-term rentals prohibited in many residential areas and notoriously steep fines for violations.
- **Unincorporated Miami-Dade County** — its own vacation-rental registration and standards apply.

A DSCR loan finances the property regardless, but the permit determines whether the STR income projection is usable. **Confirm the municipality's current STR rules before you go under contract** — it's the single most common way a Miami STR deal falls apart.
