## Our Scoring Framework: 6 Factors That Define a "Best" Lender

A lender's strength isn't a single number — it's a profile across these six factors. We weight each based on what actually moves the needle on the borrower's economics and close probability.

#### Rate Range (25%)

The single largest financial factor. We compare published rate sheets and head-to-head quotes on identical files.

#### Max LTV (20%)

Higher LTV = lower down payment = better leverage. The spread between an 80% and 75% LTV lender is real money.

#### FICO Floor (15%)

How low the lender will go on credit. Critical for 620–680 borrowers who get pushed to second-tier pricing or denied.

#### Program Coverage (20%)

Does the lender do foreign national, ITIN, condotel, 5+ unit, sub-1.0 DSCR, jumbo bridge? Each "no" is a deal that doesn't close.

#### Close Speed (10%)

Standard files in 21–30 days. Bridge or rush files in 14–21. Slow close kills deals when sellers have backup offers.

#### Service & Communication (10%)

Single loan officer per file, real cell-phone access, weekly file status. Call-center handoffs are a soft cost.

## How to Pick the Right DSCR Lender for Your File

Match your file's complexity to the lender's strength. The rough decision tree:

1. **Clean SFR file, 720+ FICO, 1.20+ DSCR, $200K–$1M loan:** Any lender on this list will quote competitively. Shop 3-4 for best rate.
2. **Foreign national or ITIN borrower:** Wholesale broker with multiple foreign-national programs. Many direct lenders don't offer this at all.
3. **Sub-1.0 DSCR (no-ratio program):** Specialty wholesale lender required. Few direct lenders price below 1.0.
4. **Condotel or non-warrantable condo:** Limited lender pool — check coverage before applying.
5. **5+ unit multifamily:** Commercial DSCR lender or wholesale broker with multifamily program.
6. **Jumbo bridge in California ($1M+):** Specialty bridge lender required. Standard DSCR shops don't underwrite this.
7. **BRRRR (acquisition + rehab + refi):** Hybrid lender (Kiavi, Lima One, RCN) for the front end, then DSCR refi at stabilization.

## Wholesale Broker vs Direct Lender: The Real Trade-off

The single most-misunderstood choice in this market. Both models work — they trade off differently.

### Direct Lender (Kiavi, Easy Street, Visio, Lima One, etc.)

- **Pros:** Single underwriting team, predictable program rules, often faster on simple files, single point of accountability.
- **Cons:** One program means one set of overlays. If your file falls outside that program (FICO too low, property type uncommon, scenario unusual), they decline. No comparison shopping.

### Wholesale Broker (DSCR Capital Partners and others)

- **Pros:** Multi-lender shopping per file. Higher approval probability on complex scenarios. Almost always wins on rate due to competitive bidding.
- **Cons:** More moving parts on the back end — though the borrower never sees this. Reputable brokers handle all lender coordination.

If your file is clean and simple, both models work. As complexity rises (FICO, property type, borrower type, loan size), the broker model wins more often.

## Red Flags When Evaluating a DSCR Lender

- **No NMLS license listed:** Every legitimate mortgage lender has an NMLS number. Look it up on the NMLS Consumer Access site before submitting any application.
- **Upfront fees before approval:** Reputable lenders don't charge application fees on the front end. Credit pulls and appraisals come after pre-approval.
- **Quoted rate that's too good to be true:** A rate 100+ basis points below market on a non-strongest-tier file is a bait quote that won't survive lock.
- **Pressure to skip the shopping process:** A confident lender won't pressure you out of getting other quotes. They know they'll win or lose on their merits.
- **Can't explain prepayment penalty terms clearly:** Most DSCR loans have prepays. The lender should walk you through 3-2-1 vs 5-4-3-2-1 and when each makes sense.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Who is the best DSCR lender in 2026?**

No single "best" for every file. Wholesale brokers shop across lenders for each file, which typically wins on complex scenarios. Direct lenders win on simple files where their program overlay fits. Match your file complexity to lender strength.

**What is the lowest DSCR loan rate available in 2026?**

As of May 2026, the lowest DSCR rates run 6.50–7.25% for the strongest tier (760+ FICO, 1.25+ DSCR, 65% LTV, prepay, SFR). Most lenders price 7.00–8.50% on standard files. [Full rate breakdown →](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/dscr-loan-rates/)

**What FICO score do I need for the best DSCR loan rate?**

Best-tier pricing requires 760+ FICO. Rate spread between 620 and 760 is typically 1.50–2.25% on the same file. [FICO matrix →](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/blog/dscr-loan-credit-score-matrix/)

**Should I use a direct DSCR lender or a wholesale broker?**

Direct lenders for simple, clean files where their program fits. Wholesale brokers (like us) for complex files — foreign national, condotel, sub-1.0 DSCR, jumbo, ITIN, 5+ unit. Brokers also typically win on rate via competitive bidding.

**Can I get a DSCR loan with 620 credit?**

Yes. Several DSCR lenders price down to 620 FICO. Expect 1.50–2.25% higher rates than a 760 file, and LTV capped at 70–75%. [620 FICO DSCR guide →](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/blog/dscr-loan-with-620-credit-score/)

**How long does a DSCR loan take to close?**

Standard files: 21–30 days. Bridge or rush files: 14–21 days. Slower lenders or complex files: 30–45 days. Close speed varies more by lender than by program type.
