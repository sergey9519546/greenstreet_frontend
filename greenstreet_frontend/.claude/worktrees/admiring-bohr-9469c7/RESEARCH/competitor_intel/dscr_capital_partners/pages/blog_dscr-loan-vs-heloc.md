# DSCR Loan vs. HELOC: Which Is Better for Rental Property?

📅 Updated June 2026✍️ By [Arin Baghermian](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/team/arin-baghermian/)⏱ 7 min read🏦 NMLS #2591548

If you want to **pull equity out of a rental property**, the two main options are a **DSCR cash-out refinance** and a **HELOC** (home equity line of credit). They work very differently — and for investment property, one is usually far easier to get. Here’s the head-to-head for 2026.

## DSCR Cash-Out vs. HELOC at a Glance

| Factor | DSCR Cash-Out Refinance | HELOC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualifies on | Property’s rental income (no tax returns) | Your personal income & DTI (full docs) |
| Available on investment property? | Yes — it’s built for it | Limited — many banks only do HELOCs on a primary residence |
| Structure | New first mortgage, lump sum | Revolving line behind your existing loan |
| Rate | Fixed (30-yr) or ARM | Variable, tied to prime |
| Max LTV (investment) | Up to ~75–80% | Often capped lower on rentals (if offered) |
| Closes in LLC? | Yes | Rarely |
| Income docs | None | Required |

## The Catch with a HELOC on a Rental

The biggest issue: **most lenders won’t write a HELOC on an investment property** — and the few that do require full income documentation, charge higher rates, cap LTV lower, and won’t lend to an LLC. If your rental is held in an entity or your tax returns don’t show strong income, a HELOC is often a dead end.

## Why Investors Choose the DSCR Cash-Out

A [DSCR cash-out refinance](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/blog/dscr-cash-out-refinance/) sidesteps all of that: it qualifies on the property’s rent, requires no tax returns, closes in an LLC, and gives you a fixed-rate first mortgage with a lump sum you can redeploy into the next deal. For a buy-rehab-rent ( [BRRRR](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/blog/dscr-loan-for-brrrr/)) strategy, the DSCR cash-out is the standard exit.

## When a HELOC Still Makes Sense

- You’re pulling equity from your **primary residence** (not a rental) to fund a down payment.
- You want a **revolving line** you can draw and repay repeatedly rather than a one-time lump sum.
- You qualify easily on income and want to keep your existing low first-mortgage rate untouched.

For tapping equity _in the rental itself_, though, the DSCR cash-out is almost always the more practical route. Check current [DSCR LTV limits](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/blog/dscr-ltv-limits/) to see how much you can pull.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Can you get a HELOC on a rental property? +

Sometimes, but it’s much harder than on a primary residence. Many banks don’t offer investment-property HELOCs at all; those that do require full income documentation, charge higher rates, cap the LTV lower, and usually won’t lend to an LLC.

Is a DSCR cash-out refinance better than a HELOC for investors? +

For pulling equity out of a rental, usually yes. A DSCR cash-out qualifies on the property’s rent (no tax returns), closes in an LLC, offers fixed rates, and reaches ~75–80% LTV — where investment HELOCs are limited or unavailable.

Do HELOCs require income verification? +

Yes. A HELOC is underwritten on your personal income and debt-to-income ratio with full documentation. A DSCR loan does not — it qualifies on the property’s cash flow.

How much equity can I pull from a rental with a DSCR loan? +

Up to roughly 75–80% of the appraised value, minus your existing loan payoff. The exact amount depends on the property’s DSCR, your credit, and the program.
