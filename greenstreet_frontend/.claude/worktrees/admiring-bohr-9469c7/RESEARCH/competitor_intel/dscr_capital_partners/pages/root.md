Common Questions

## DSCR Loan FAQs

Everything you need to know about DSCR and rental property loans.

What is a DSCR loan and how does it work?
+

A DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) loan qualifies the borrower based on the rental income of the investment property — not personal income. The DSCR is calculated by dividing the property's gross rental income by its monthly debt obligations. DSCR Capital Partners offers DSCR loans with rates from 5.75%, up to 85% LTV, and credit scores starting at 620.


Do I need to show personal income to qualify?
+

No. DSCR and rental property loans from DSCR Capital Partners do not require W-2s, tax returns, or employment verification. Qualification is based on the rental income potential of the property itself, making these loans ideal for self-employed investors and business owners.


Can first-time investors get a DSCR loan?
+

Yes. DSCR Capital Partners welcomes first-time real estate investors on DSCR, long-term rental, and short-term rental loans. No prior landlord experience is required. We also welcome first-time homebuyers purchasing investment properties.


Do you lend to foreign nationals?
+

Yes. DSCR Capital Partners has specialized programs for foreign nationals investing in U.S. rental real estate. We work with international investors to provide competitive DSCR and rental property loan products with a streamlined process tailored for non-U.S. residents.


Can I use projected Airbnb income to qualify?
+

Yes. Our short-term rental loan program accepts AirDNA projected income data to qualify properties as Airbnb, VRBO, or vacation rentals. This allows investors to finance short-term rental properties even without an existing rental history.


What credit score do I need for a DSCR loan?
+

DSCR Capital Partners offers DSCR loans for borrowers with credit scores starting from 620. Better rates and terms are typically available for scores above 700, but we work with a wide range of credit profiles for investment property financing.


What is the maximum loan amount?
+

DSCR Capital Partners offers DSCR and rental property loans up to $15 million, making us a strong choice for high-value investment properties, large portfolios, and commercial rental assets.


What is an investment property loan?
+

An investment property loan is a mortgage used to purchase or refinance a property that is not your primary residence — such as a rental home, multi-family building, short-term rental, or commercial property. Investment property loans typically have different qualifying criteria than primary residence mortgages. DSCR Capital Partners specializes exclusively in investment property loans, qualifying borrowers based on rental income rather than personal income.


Do you offer no income verification loans?
+

Yes. DSCR Capital Partners offers no income verification loans (also called no-doc loans or no income doc loans) for real estate investors. Instead of W-2s, tax returns, or pay stubs, we qualify your loan based on the rental income the property generates. This makes our loans ideal for self-employed borrowers, business owners, and anyone whose personal income doesn't reflect their full financial picture.


Can I get a landlord loan or rental property loan under my LLC?
+

Yes. DSCR Capital Partners offers LLC loans for real estate investors who hold properties in a limited liability company. Financing under an LLC provides liability protection and can simplify bookkeeping for your portfolio. We work with single-member and multi-member LLCs for DSCR loans, long-term rental loans, and short-term rental loans.


What is a no-doc loan and how does it work?
+

A no-doc loan (no documentation loan) is a mortgage that does not require the borrower to provide traditional income documentation such as W-2s, tax returns, or bank statements. DSCR Capital Partners offers no-doc investment property loans where qualification is based entirely on the property's rental income and debt service coverage ratio (DSCR). This is ideal for investors with complex income structures or those who prefer to keep personal finances separate from their real estate business.


What types of investment properties do you lend on?
+

DSCR Capital Partners provides investment property loans for single-family residences (SFR), condos, multi-family properties (1–4 units), multi-family 5+ units, condotels, and co-ops. We also finance short-term rentals (Airbnb, VRBO, vacation rentals) and long-term rental properties nationwide.
