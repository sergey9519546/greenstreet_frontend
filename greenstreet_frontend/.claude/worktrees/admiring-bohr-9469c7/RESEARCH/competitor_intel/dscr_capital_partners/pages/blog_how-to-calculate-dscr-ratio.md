# How to Calculate Your DSCR Ratio (With Examples)

📅 Updated February 2026✍️ By [Arin Baghermian](https://dscrcapitalpartners.com/team/arin-baghermian/)⏱ 8 min read🏦 NMLS #2591548

Before applying for a DSCR loan, the single most important number you need to know is your property's Debt Service Coverage Ratio. Understanding how to calculate your DSCR — and how lenders interpret it — can help you find better properties, structure deals more effectively, and walk into your loan application with confidence.

This guide breaks down the DSCR formula with real examples, explains what a good DSCR looks like, and shows you how to improve it before applying.

## The DSCR Formula

### DSCR = Gross Monthly Rental Income ÷ Monthly Debt Obligations (PITIA)

**PITIA** = Principal + Interest + Taxes + Insurance + HOA (if applicable)

The result tells you how well the property's income covers its debt. A DSCR of 1.0 means the property breaks exactly even. Above 1.0 means positive cash flow. Below 1.0 means the rent does not fully cover the debt payment.

## DSCR Calculation Examples

### Example 1 — Strong DSCR (Single-Family Rental)

- **Monthly rent:** $2,800
- **Monthly mortgage payment (P&I):** $1,750
- **Property taxes:** $350/month
- **Insurance:** $120/month
- **HOA:** $0
- **Total PITIA:** $2,220/month
- **DSCR:** $2,800 ÷ $2,220 = 1.26 ✓ Strong

### Example 2 — Borderline DSCR (Condo)

- **Monthly rent:** $1,900
- **Monthly mortgage payment (P&I):** $1,400
- **Property taxes:** $220/month
- **Insurance:** $80/month
- **HOA:** $300/month
- **Total PITIA:** $2,000/month
- **DSCR:** $1,900 ÷ $2,000 = 0.95 — Below 1.0 but may still qualify

### Example 3 — Short-Term Rental (Airbnb)

- **AirDNA projected monthly income:** $4,200
- **Monthly mortgage payment (P&I):** $2,100
- **Property taxes:** $400/month
- **Insurance:** $180/month
- **HOA:** $250/month
- **Total PITIA:** $2,930/month
- **DSCR:** $4,200 ÷ $2,930 = 1.43 ✓ Excellent

## What Is a Good DSCR?

### DSCR Ranges — What They Mean

- **1.25 and above:** Excellent. Qualifies for best rates and highest LTV.
- **1.10 – 1.24:** Strong. Qualifies for most programs with competitive terms.
- **1.00 – 1.09:** Acceptable. Qualifies for standard programs, may have minor rate adjustments.
- **0.75 – 0.99:** Below breakeven. Some lenders (including DSCR Capital Partners) have programs for this range depending on overall file strength.
- **Below 0.75:** Difficult to finance with a DSCR loan. May need a different product.

## How Lenders Determine Rental Income

How a lender calculates your rental income depends on the property type:

- **Existing long-term rental:** The current signed lease is used. If no lease, the lender orders a rent schedule (Form 1007) from the appraiser.
- **Vacant property (purchase):** The appraiser provides a market rent estimate based on comparable rentals in the area.
- **Short-term rental (Airbnb/VRBO):** DSCR Capital Partners uses AirDNA projected income — no rental history required.

## How to Improve Your DSCR Before Applying

If your DSCR is coming in lower than you would like, here are the most effective ways to improve it:

- **Increase your down payment.** A larger down payment reduces your loan balance, which lowers your monthly P&I payment and improves your DSCR directly.
- **Buy down your interest rate.** Paying points upfront to lower your rate reduces your monthly payment and boosts your DSCR.
- **Negotiate a higher rent.** Even a modest rent increase — say $150/month — can push a 0.95 DSCR above 1.0.
- **Choose an interest-only loan.** An interest-only option reduces your monthly payment significantly, improving DSCR. Ask us about I/O DSCR loans.
- **Look for lower HOA properties.** High HOA fees are a DSCR killer. Properties with low or no HOA have a structural DSCR advantage.
- **Consider short-term rental income.** In the right market, Airbnb income can be 2–3x long-term rental income, dramatically improving DSCR.

## DSCR vs. DTI — What Is the Difference?

Conventional loans use Debt-to-Income ratio (DTI), which measures your personal income against your total monthly debt payments. DSCR loans replace DTI entirely — instead of looking at your personal finances, the lender looks only at whether the property pays for itself. This is why self-employed borrowers, portfolio investors, and anyone with complex income structures prefer DSCR loans. Your personal income is irrelevant to the qualification.

## Common DSCR Calculation Mistakes to Avoid

- **Using net rent instead of gross rent.** DSCR uses gross rental income before expenses like vacancy, maintenance, or property management.
- **Forgetting HOA fees.** HOA fees are included in PITIA and can significantly impact DSCR for condo and townhouse properties.
- **Using the wrong insurance figure.** Make sure you are using landlord/investment property insurance, which is typically higher than homeowner insurance.
- **Ignoring flood insurance.** If the property is in a flood zone, flood insurance is required and adds to your PITIA.
