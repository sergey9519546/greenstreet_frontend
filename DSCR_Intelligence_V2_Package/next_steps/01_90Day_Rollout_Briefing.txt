 SECTION 2



 Three Strategic Findings That Reshape the
 Strategy
 Finding 1: The Top Persona Isn't Who You'd Expect
 The Multi-State Portfolio Scaler (SA-002) scored 8.90 on the Funding-Desirability Index, the
 highest of any persona in the swarm. This is the six-to-twenty-door landlord who is
 DTI-blocked from conventional financing and therefore has both high approval probability
 on DSCR programs and near-certain repeat-borrowing behavior. This persona funds loans
 from $1M to $3.2M, accepts specialty-lender pricing, and is reachable through LinkedIn and
 search rather than Meta broad campaigns. The implication is that the budget allocation
 should front-load Google Search and LinkedIn for SA-002, not treat Meta broad as the default
 top-of-funnel. Month 1 launches SA-002 first, not the conventional first-time-investor
 segment that most DSCR marketing defaults to.

 Finding 2: 40% of Marginal Declines Are Lender-Fit Issues
 Approximately 40 percent of marginal-decline cases in the swarm's evidence base are curable
 by re-shopping the file rather than true underwriting declines. This finding elevates the
 decline-letter triage question (FF-08 Q-012) from a customer-service nicety to a high-leverage
 intake design decision. One optional form field surfaces five of the eight edge-case personas
 in a single question. The Compensated-Exception Shopper persona (SA-011) and five
 edge-case personas (EG-001, EG-005, EG-006, EG-007, EG-008) are all reachable through this
 single intake addition. The operational implication is that the decline-letter audit becomes a
 core service offering, not a side feature. Marketing copy around "Declined elsewhere? Bring
 the decline letter" becomes a primary hook, not a tertiary one.

 Finding 3: Property Type Dominates DSCR for Approval Prediction
 The most counter-intuitive finding from the approval-pattern analysis is that property-type
 cleanliness dominates DSCR and FICO for approval prediction. The 1.30+ DSCR cell with 720+
 FICO shows only 50 percent approval rate, compared to 100 percent approval in the
 1.20-to-1.29 DSCR cell with the same FICO band. All three declines in the high-DSCR cell are
 overlay-driven: condotel, non-warrantable condo, and appraisal short. The interpretation is
 that borrowers with very high DSCR often have other file complications, because properties
 that produce unusually high rent relative to debt service frequently have property-type or
 regulatory complications. The practical implication is that FF-08 screens property type early
 in the intake flow (Q-002, step 1) before requesting DSCR estimates (Q-008, step 2), and that
 AC-09 V2 creative leads with property-type clarity, not DSCR thresholds.

 SECTION 6



 KPI Dashboard: Weekly and Monthly Review
 The swarm's operational health is tracked through a 14-KPI dashboard reviewed weekly by
 marketing-ops and monthly by leadership. The KPIs are grouped into four categories: funnel
 volume, funnel quality, funded-loan economics, and swarm-health metrics. Funnel volume
 tracks raw activity (form starts, form completions, lead count by persona). Funnel quality
 tracks tier distribution, persona fit rate, and documentation upload rate. Funded-loan
 economics tracks the metrics that actually matter (cost per Tier A-or-B lead, cost per pre-qual
 letter, cost per loan in underwriting, cost per funded loan). Swarm-health metrics track the
 swarm's learning loop (specialty-lender referral rate, decline-letter audit conversion,
